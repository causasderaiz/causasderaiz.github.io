Cazador Resources seeks gold prospecting on the Zêzere

Cazador Resources seeks rights to prospect for gold and other metals over 297 km² of five municipalities, including the shores of the Castelo de Bode reservoir on the Zêzere

Castelo de Bode reservoir, Zêzere river: Ferreira do Zêzere, Vila de Rei, Alvaiázere, Ansião and Figueiró dos Vinhos

🗳 Public consultation until 4 January 2027 · "Vila de Rei" prospecting and exploration request, DGEG case MNPPP518

🗳 Take part in the public consultation

Canadian company Cazador Resources has asked the Directorate-General for Energy and Geology (DGEG) for rights to prospect for gold, copper, lead, zinc, silver, tin and tungsten over about 297 km² of five municipalities, in two blocks, one of them along the Castelo de Bode reservoir on the Zêzere. The public consultation runs on participa.pt until 4 January 2027.

What is at stake

Figueiró dos Vinhos council says it will not allow interventions against landowners' will; the councils of Ferreira do Zêzere, Vila de Rei and Ansião say they have not yet taken a position. Ferreira do Zêzere wants to know which techniques will be used, where, with what impacts and what effects on water, homes, farming and landowners. Submissions are accepted on participa.pt until 11:59 pm on 4 January 2027.

Castelo de Bode's water. The southern block follows the Castelo de Bode reservoir, which APA describes as the country's largest source of public water supply and essential for Greater Lisbon. The technical report plans to take 10 to 15 m³ of water a day for drilling, by tanker or by pumping straight from watercourses, and to produce 7 to 11 tonnes of drilling mud in settling ponds dug into the ground. Gold in this region lies in quartz veins with pyrite and arsenopyrite, an arsenic mineral.

Soil, woodland and tracks. Each drill site takes a cleared and levelled 10 by 15 metre platform, with two settling ponds of up to 5 by 3 metres and 2.5 metres deep. Trenches can be 50 metres long and 1.8 metres deep. Access tracks are opened for backhoes and heavy machinery; restoring the land, the report says, "will be assessed" at the end.

People who live on and farm the land. The area crosses ten parishes in five municipalities, with villages, homes and farmland. The technical report itself lists the effects prospecting can have: restrictions on farming and tourism, pressure on housing and services, noise, soil and water contamination and habitat destruction. The councils want to know where and how prospecting would be done.

    The public consultation opens on participa.pt, until 4 January 2027.

    Source: Participa ↗
  2. 17 Nov

    DGEG publishes the map of the area: about 297 km² in two blocks, from Ansião to the banks of the Zêzere in Ferreira do Zêzere and Vila de Rei. Ferreira do Zêzere council says it will give "priority to defending the territory and the interests of the people".

    Source: Council position (Ferreira do Zêzere) · Médio Tejo/Lusa ↗
  3. 16 Nov

    Ansião council clarifies that this is only a request.

    Source: Jornal de Leiria ↗
  4. 13 Nov

    Figueiró dos Vinhos council says that "under no circumstances" will it allow, within its powers, any intervention that goes against the will of each landowner.

    Source: Figueiró dos Vinhos Council ↗
  5. 14 Oct

    Cazador Resources signs the request's technical report: a 106 km² Northern Area and a 191 km² Southern Area, five years of work and mechanical drilling.

    Source: Cazador Resources ↗
  6. 7 May

    Cazador Resources reformulates the request and removes Pedrógão Grande and Sertã "due to the negative opinion" of those councils on the original 494 km² request. The requested area goes down to 358 km², in two blocks.

    Source: Cazador Resources ↗
20252 entries
  1. 17 Apr

    Sertã council unanimously ratifies its 3 April opinion: incompatibility with the municipal master plan, possible harm to areas of high environmental, landscape, tourism and farming value, and no guarantees of good practice.

    Source: Sertã Council ↗
  2. 27 Mar

    Pedrógão Grande council unanimously votes against the request, as it breaches the municipal master plan, and asks for the whole municipality to be removed. The deputy mayor says he is "against this prospecting in defence of quality of life".

    Source: Pedrógão Grande Council ↗
