No. 169 🗳 Public consultation Mining and quarries

The banks of the Zêzere may open up to prospecting for gold and other metals

Castelo de Bode reservoir, Zêzere river: Ferreira do Zêzere, Vila de Rei, Alvaiázere, Ansião and Figueiró dos Vinhos

🗳 Public consultation until 4 January 2027 · "Vila de Rei" prospecting and exploration request, DGEG case MNPPP518

🗳 Take part in the public consultation

Canadian company Cazador Resources has asked the Directorate-General for Energy and Geology (DGEG) for rights to prospect for gold, copper, lead, zinc, silver, tin and tungsten over about 297 km² of five municipalities, in two blocks, one of them along the Castelo de Bode reservoir on the Zêzere. The public consultation runs on participa.pt until 4 January 2027.

What is at stake

The councils of Ferreira do Zêzere, Vila de Rei and Ansião say they have not yet taken a position. Ferreira do Zêzere wants to know which techniques will be used, where, with what impacts and what effects on water, homes, farming and landowners. Information sessions are held in Ferreira do Zêzere on 23 November (6 pm) and 24 November (2 pm in Frazoeira and 6 pm in Águas Belas), in Vila de Rei on 23 and 27 November (2 pm, José Cardoso Pires Municipal Library) and in Ansião on 26 November (7 pm, Escola Tecnológica e Profissional de Sicó, Avelar). Submissions are accepted on participa.pt until 11:59 pm on 4 January 2027.

Castelo de Bode's water. The southern block follows the Castelo de Bode reservoir, which APA describes as the country's largest source of public water supply and essential for Greater Lisbon. Ferreira do Zêzere council asks for clarity on the effects on water resources.

People who live on and farm the land. The area crosses ten parishes in five municipalities, with villages, homes and farmland. The councils want to know where and how prospecting would be done and what effects it would have on homes, farming and landowners.
