No. 57 ◐ Monitoring Hydrogen and green fuels

Green hydrogen and fuels cluster in Sines: five projects with PIN status

Sines, Alentejo coast

◐ 5 projects with PIN status

Five projects tied to hydrogen, ammonia and synthetic fuels hold PIN status in Sines, part of the 13 projects in the "chemical and petrochemical" sector that, according to AICEP, represent the largest share of PIN projects alongside tourism.

Sines concentrates several National Interest Project (PIN) status projects tied to hydrogen, ammonia and synthetic fuels: Madoqua H2, GreenH2Atlantic, Green Amonia Express, Navigator Green Fuels and MDQ Synfuels. They are part of the 13 projects in the "chemical and petrochemical" sector that, according to a 2024 business-press survey, represent the largest share of PIN projects granted in Portugal, alongside tourism.

There is no public, official and complete list of all PIN projects in Portugal: sector figures come from AICEP statements to the press, not from an open registry. This cluster adds to two other cases in the same area (the AI gigafactory in Sines and the Southwest Wind Farm), both linked to the same pressure on water, energy and land on the Alentejo coast.

As of now, there is no known citizen organisation specifically monitoring this hydrogen cluster.
