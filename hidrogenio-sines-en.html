No. 57 ⚡ Active situation Hydrogen and green fuels

Five hydrogen, ammonia and synthetic fuel projects in Sines have PIN status

Sines, Alentejo coast

⚡ 5 projects with PIN status

Five projects tied to hydrogen, ammonia and synthetic fuels hold PIN status in Sines, part of the 13 projects in the "chemical and petrochemical" sector that, according to AICEP, represent the largest share of PIN projects alongside tourism.

What is at stake

Pine and cork oak woodland. For the Madoqua plots, the Calb battery factory and the Repsol solar park alone, in area 1A3 of the ZILS, the study counts 13,449 trees to be felled across 74.8 hectares: more than 9,000 maritime pines, more than 1,000 stone pines, around 2,000 eucalyptus and 1,034 cork oaks, a protected tree. The same area is home to Rhaponticoides fraylensis and Thymus capitellatus, legally protected plants (case 130).

The birds. In the public consultation on the MadoquaPower2X project, Sines council pointed out that the pipeline crosses areas critical for protected species and asked for bird monitoring to involve scientific bodies.

Water. Juntos pelo Cercal warns that an ammonia leak contaminates air, soil and water, causing ecological damage above all in aquatic ecosystems.

The people of Sines and Cercal. The ammonia plant is classified at the upper tier of hazard. The pipe rack, with two ammonia pipes and one steam pipe, runs less than 100 metres from homes. The Juntos pelo Cercal movement opposes it over the health and safety risks of leaks and explosions.
