No. 123 ◐ Monitoring Pine woodland felling

Cabeço da Flauta: stone pines felled next to Lagoa de Albufeira

Herdade do Cabeço da Flauta, Meco, Sesimbra, Setúbal district

◐ Pine wood felled · Quercus and PAN ask for clarification

On 2 March 2026 more than 2,000 tonnes of stone pines were felled on the Herdade do Cabeço da Flauta estate, next to Lagoa de Albufeira in Sesimbra, within the lagoon's Ramsar site. ICNF says the felling did not need authorisation. The Meco Livre movement organised a protest, Guardiões da Lagoa de Albufeira urged people to ask the authorities for clarification, and Quercus and PAN challenged it.

What is at stake

<p>Herdade do Cabeço da Flauta, at Meco (Castelo parish, Sesimbra), is a private estate with stone pine woodland next to Lagoa de Albufeira. It is not in a Special Conservation Zone or a Special Protection Area, but it lies within the Lagoa de Albufeira Ramsar site, which includes the pine woods around the lagoon, and is covered by the Lagoa de Albufeira Urbanisation Plan.</p>

  1. 31 Mar

    Quercus criticises the authorities for relying on a "legal vacuum" and points out that the estate lies within the Lagoa de Albufeira Ramsar site and is covered by the Lagoa de Albufeira Urbanisation Plan. It calls for an assessment of the impacts on hydrology, sediments and ecological connection to the lagoon.

    Source: Correio da Manhã/Lusa ↗
  2. 21 Mar

    PAN tables a draft resolution in the Assembly of the Republic, puts questions to the Government and asks ICNF, CCDR-LVT and the Meco Livre movement for clarification.

    Source: Agroportal ↗
  3. 18 Mar

    Residents report extensive felling in Mata da Apostiça and Cabeço da Flauta. ICNF says no protected species were felled and Sesimbra council says it is monitoring the situation with ICNF.

    Source: O Setubalense ↗
  4. 15 Mar

    Dozens of people protest at Cabeço da Flauta. ICNF replies that the land is not in a Special Conservation Zone and that the forestry operation does not need the institute's authorisation.

    Source: Correio da Manhã ↗
  5. 14 Mar

    The Meco Livre movement calls a protest at the estate entrance and demands the immediate suspension of the felling licences, full disclosure of the administrative file and replanting by the owner.

    Source: Diário do Distrito ↗
  6. 2 Mar

    More than 2,000 tonnes of stone pines are felled on the Herdade do Cabeço da Flauta estate, next to Lagoa de Albufeira. Images shared by the Meco Livre movement go viral.

    Source: NiT ↗
  7. Mar

    Guardiões da Lagoa de Albufeira, who describe themselves as a group of local citizens, share template emails for the media and for the council, ICNF, CCDR-LVT, APA and the GNR's SEPNA. They ask about the authorised area, the purpose of the felling, who authorised it, whether there is a reforestation plan or environmental impact assessment, and where the timber goes.

    Source: Guardiões da Lagoa de Albufeira ↗
