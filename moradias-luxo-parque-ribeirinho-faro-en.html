Parque Ribeirinho, Faro: 28 luxury villas on the shore of the Ria Formosa

Faro, Faro district

⚡ Villa project under council review · Petition for suspension

⚡ Sign the petition

In Faro's Parque Ribeirinho, inside the Ria Formosa Natural Park and the Natura 2000 network, Glensee Properties, based in Albufeira, whose sole partner is Grimpeur Holdings, registered on the Isle of Man, wants to build 28 luxury villas, from €3.75 million each, 20 metres from the lagoon. The subdivision dates from 1990; the house designs were submitted to the council in October 2026 and a petition calls for suspension.

What is at stake

The subdivision was approved by Faro council on 27 July 1990, before the management plan of the Ria Formosa Natural Park and the Coastal Programme existed. The council accepted the infrastructure works in 2005, and the Parque Ribeirinho was laid out around the plots, on land ceded by the developer. The project is now called The Waterfront and is promoted by Glensee Properties, based in Albufeira, whose sole partner is Grimpeur Holdings, registered on the Isle of Man.

The lagoon and its birds. The plot lies inside the Ria Formosa Natural Park and the Ria Formosa / Castro Marim Natura 2000 site, next to the salt pans and salt marsh. Flamingos, spoonbills, black-winged stilts, Kentish plovers and little terns are seen in the park and the salt pans. The little tern is Vulnerable in the Portuguese Red Data Book and has some of the country's largest colonies in the Ria Formosa.

The park and the lagoon shore. The Parque Ribeirinho has about 16 hectares of paths along the lagoon, walked and cycled every day. The project puts up 28 houses of about 600 m², with 56 swimming pools, 20 metres from the shore, and reserves 2,400 m² of gardens for the condominium. The petition calls it a veiled privatisation of public land in a city facing a housing crisis.

Rising seas. The council acknowledges that Faro is one of the Algarve municipalities most exposed to sea-level rise and that the houses may be reached by the sea within the next hundred years.
