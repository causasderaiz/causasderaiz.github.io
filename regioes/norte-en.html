Region · Northern Portugal

Places at risk in Northern Portugal

From Barroso to Gerês and the Mirandese Plateau, from the mountains of Trás-os-Montes to the rivers around Porto, northern Portugal has landscapes that many visit and many call home. Several are now under pressure from mines, prospecting, energy projects and polluting discharges. In Covas do Barroso, local people are fighting a lithium mine. Here are the places at risk we document, who defends them and how to help protect them.

Lithium mine in Covas do Barroso

The people of Covas do Barroso, in Boticas (Trás-os-Montes, Portugal), have been protesting against the open-pit lithium mine that Savannah Resources wants to open in a centuries-old farming area. The Mirandela Administrative and Tax Court suspended the administrative easement granting access to the commons; on August 13, the Assembly of Commoners overwhelmingly rejected Savannah's attempt to present recent agreements as a sign of social acceptance of the project.

Boticas, Vila Real, Trás-os-Montes, Portugal (Barroso region) · ⚖ In court
