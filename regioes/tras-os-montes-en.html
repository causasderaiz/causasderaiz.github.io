Trás-os-Montes: environmental struggles and conflicts

Trás-os-Montes, in the districts of Bragança and Vila Real, is a land of mountains, plateaus, rivers and villages. It is also where many mining and prospecting requests, and large energy projects, are concentrated. In Covas do Barroso, in Boticas, local people are fighting a lithium mine. These are the places at risk we document in the region, who defends them and how to help.

Lithium mine in Covas do Barroso

The people of Covas do Barroso, in Boticas (Trás-os-Montes, Portugal), are fighting the open-pit lithium mine that Savannah Resources wants to open in a centuries-old farming area. In June 2026, the Mirandela Administrative and Tax Court suspended the easement granting access to the commons, but the Government invoked the public interest and works resumed; in August, the Commons of Covas do Barroso said Savannah's agreements with other communities do not amount to social consent.
