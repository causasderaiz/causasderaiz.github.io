Places at risk in Madeira

From the mountain trails to Curral das Freiras and the beaches of Porto Santo, Madeira has landscapes that many visit. Some are under pressure from the privatisation of trails, high-rise construction and new tourism infrastructure. Here are the places at risk we document, who defends them and how to help protect them.
