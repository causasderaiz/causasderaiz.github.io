Places at risk in Lisbon and the Tagus Valley

From the Arrábida hills to Meco, from Costa da Caparica to Sintra and Cascais, the Lisbon region has beaches, hills and pine woods that many people visit. Many are under pressure from urban development, tourism, quarries and pollution. Here are the places at risk we document, who defends them and how to help protect them.
