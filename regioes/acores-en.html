Places at risk in the Azores

From the island lakes to the soils of Terceira and the deep sea around the archipelago, the Azores have places that many visit. Some are under pressure from intensive livestock farming, long-standing contamination and interest in deep-sea mining. Here are the places at risk we document, who defends them and how to help protect them.
