Places at risk in Central Portugal

From the Zêzere river to the Castelo de Bode reservoir, from the Lis river to Figueira da Foz, central Portugal has rivers, mountains and cork oak woodland that many know. Mines, dams, solar and wind plants, data centres and polluting discharges now put pressure on several of these places. Here are the places at risk we document, who defends them and how to help protect them.
