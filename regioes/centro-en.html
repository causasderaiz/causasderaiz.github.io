Places at risk in Central Portugal

From the Zêzere river to the Castelo de Bode reservoir, from the Lis river to Figueira da Foz, central Portugal has rivers, mountains and cork oak woodland that many know. Mines, dams, solar and wind plants, data centres and polluting discharges now put pressure on several of these places.
