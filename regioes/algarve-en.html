Places at risk in the Algarve

From the beaches of Albufeira, Armação de Pêra and Lagos to the Albandeira coast, from the Salgados lagoon to the Foupana stream, the Algarve has places that many people visit. Many are under pressure from resorts, hotels, golf courses, dams and landfills. Here are the places at risk we document, who defends them and how to help protect them.
