Places at risk in Alentejo

From the coast of Melides and Galé to Alqueva, from Serra d'Ossa to Sines and Odemira, the Alentejo has beaches, cork oak woodland and plains that many seek out. Mega solar plants, wind farms, mines, quarries, industry and resorts now put pressure on several of these places. Here are the places at risk we document, who defends them and how to help protect them.
