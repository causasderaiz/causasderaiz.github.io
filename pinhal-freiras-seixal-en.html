Pinhal das Freiras: urban development plan threatens the Fernão Ferro/Lagoa de Albufeira conservation area

Pinhal das Freiras, Mata de Belverde, Seixal municipality

⚡ Petition with 7,700 signatures delivered to Parliament, rapporteur appointed · Seixal Council approved 2.5 million m² urban development plan · Protest outside the council offices on 1 September 2026

The Pinhal das Freiras, in Mata de Belverde, Seixal municipality, is part of the Fernão Ferro/Lagoa de Albufeira Special Conservation Area, protected under the Natura 2000 network since 2000. In August 2024, Seixal Municipal Council approved a 2.5 million m² urban development plan for the area, excluding green spaces, with buildings up to 19 metres tall; the Salvar a Floresta do Seixal movement estimates the loss of around 50,000 trees, 2.5 km² of forest and 200 km of trails and cycle paths, with capacity foreseen for more than 90,000 new residents. The association met with Seixal Municipal Council in 2025 and 2026 and organised a protest outside the council offices on 1 September 2026.

The Pinhal das Freiras, in Mata de Belverde, Seixal municipality, is a forested area that forms part of the Fernão Ferro/Lagoa de Albufeira Special Conservation Area (ZEC), protected under the Natura 2000 network since 2000. Plans to urbanise the area date back at least to the 1990s, but gained new urgency in August 2024, when Seixal Municipal Council approved a 2.5 million m² urban development plan for the site, excluding green spaces from the project design.

The citizens' movement Salvar a Floresta do Seixal, which has opposed the plan since then, estimates the loss of around 50,000 trees, 2.5 km² of forest, and 200 km of trails and cycle paths from the municipality's trail network, which had received more than €72,000 in European funding through POSEUR. According to the movement, the planned building complex, up to 19 metres tall, could have capacity for more than 90,000 new residents, with serious impacts on the quality of life of those already living in the area.

In March 2025, residents formalised the environmental association Seixal+Verde, which launched a petition to the Portuguese Parliament against the urbanisation of Pinhal das Freiras (UOPG 33), asking that the area be turned into a Nature Reserve or Natural Park, or that strict environmental rules be applied to the plan. The petition, delivered with more than 7,700 signatures, was admitted as petition no. 166/XVII/1, with a rapporteur appointed in June 2026, awaiting review by the Environment and Energy Committee. It is currently closed to new signatures.

Seixal+Verde met with Seixal Municipal Council in 2025 and 2026, and organised a public protest outside the council offices on 1 September 2026, calling for the project to be revised.
