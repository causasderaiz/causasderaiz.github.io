Serra d'Arga: lithium stopped, mountain protected

Serra d'Arga, Caminha, Viana do Castelo, Ponte de Lima and Vila Nova de Cerveira

✓ Victory: mountain out of the lithium tender and a Special Area of Conservation · Protected Landscape in public consultation from 6 Nov to 22 Dec 2026

Serra d'Arga was in the Government's lithium prospecting tender, with around 25,000 hectares. After years of walks and protests by SOS Serra d'Arga and other Alto Minho movements, backed by the municipalities, the mountain was dropped from the tender in 2022 and has been a Special Area of Conservation since February 2026. Its classification as a Protected Landscape is in public consultation until 22 December.

What is at stake

Serra d'Arga, in Alto Minho, covers around 10,000 hectares across Caminha, Viana do Castelo, Ponte de Lima and Vila Nova de Cerveira. A study funded by Norte 2020 found 10 habitat types of Community interest, 546 plant species (32 rare or threatened) and more than 180 vertebrate species. An area of nearly 25,000 hectares of the mountain was among the eight in the national lithium prospecting tender.
