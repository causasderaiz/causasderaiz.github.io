No. 164 ⚡ Active situation Very high voltage power line

REN's 75-metre pylons replace old very high voltage poles over the villages of Benedita and Turquel, Alcobaça, and residents ask for the lines to be moved

Benedita and Turquel, Alcobaça, Leiria district

REN renewed the lines with taller pylons on the same route; the movement still demands moving them to the hills

For 50 years REN's very high voltage lines have run over the villages of Benedita and Turquel, in Alcobaça. When REN began replacing the old pylons with 75-metre towers, residents formed the Lesados-Linhas de Muito Alta Tensão movement: they held vigils, demonstrated at Parliament and ask for the lines to move to the Serra dos Candeeiros, where there are no homes.

What is at stake

REN's very high voltage lines have crossed villages in the Benedita and Turquel parishes, in Alcobaça municipality, since the 1970s: they pass by the Benedita industrial zone, near the IC2, and through Charneca do Casal Guerra, Casal do Carvalho and Charneca do Rio Seco. In 2024 REN began replacing the old pylons with 75-metre towers, with a 45-metre safety strip on each side, on the same route. REN says it complies with the rules.

People in Benedita and Turquel. The lines have run over and beside homes for 50 years, and the new towers are 75 metres tall. Residents fear electromagnetic fields and noise and link the lines to illness in the villages; ZERO considers their fear legitimate.

The birds. According to the ICNF Manual (2019), very high voltage lines kill birds through collision with the cables.
