Golf in Taboeira, Aveiro: warning over forest clearing

Aveiro Cidade–Ria raises the alarm over forest clearing and a golf course in the Aveiro Sports Park plan, between Taboeira and Azurva

Taboeira and Azurva, next to the Municipal Stadium, Aveiro, Aveiro district

⚡ Complaint to the Public Prosecutor (DIAP Aveiro, case 5259/26.0T9AVR) · Golf course still without a new environmental impact assessment

The Aveiro Sports Park Detailed Plan, approved in October 2025, provides for a 756-bed tourist resort with an 18-hole golf course on 74.4 hectares of forest and floodplain between Taboeira, Azurva and Eixo, plus housing and roads next to the Municipal Stadium. Between 20 September and 23 November 2026, Anna Kuzmina of Aveiro Cidade–Ria photographed the clearing of much of the forest in the plan's first unit and complained to the Public Prosecutor. The Aveiro prosecutor's office opened case 5259/26.0T9AVR, and the golf course still needs a new environmental impact assessment (EIA) because the 2003 one has expired, according to CCDR Centro, the regional planning authority.

What is at stake

The Taboeira golf course was proposed in 2003 and then received a conditional favourable environmental impact statement, which has expired. The current detailed plan was approved by the City Council on 2 September 2025, open to public discussion from 10 to 29 September, approved by the Municipal Assembly on 8 October and published in the Diário da República on 10 November 2025. The Council says the golf course EIA will be carried out before construction.

The forest. Of the plan's three units, only the 6.47 hectares of UE3 stay as forest. In UE1, Aveiro Cidade–Ria photographed, over 64 days between September and November 2026, the loss of much of the woodland she had walked through, with its understorey of shrubs and ferns. She fears the same for the UE2 forest, where the golf course would go. The plan's own environmental assessment admits the loss of part of the local biodiversity as the vegetation cover is removed.

Water and the floodplain. The golf course includes a flood-prone zone and REN areas the plan wants to exclude. APA rates the local water bodies as fair and poor. A golf course needs constant irrigation: the plan proposes rainwater and treated wastewater, but the new EIA has not yet been carried out. The plan's environmental assessment says the Cretaceous aquifer of Aveiro is over-exploited. Back in 2002, the golf course impact study predicted that irrigation and fertilisation would contaminate soils and aquifers.

Forest wildlife. According to Aveiro Cidade–Ria, on August nights at least three nightjars could be seen in the now-cleared part of UE1, and fire salamanders live in Azurva. In a single November afternoon the author recorded 18 bird species, not verified by experts. The forest connects to farmland and a stream with vegetated banks where birds feed, drink and shelter.
