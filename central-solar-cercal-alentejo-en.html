No. 131 ⚡ Active situation Mega solar plants

Cercal Power plans 553,000 solar panels on 816 hectares about a kilometre from Cercal do Alentejo

Cercal do Alentejo, Santiago do Cacém, Setúbal district

⚡ Approved with conditions in 2021 and redesigned in 2022 · no news of construction starting

Cercal Power, part of the Aquila Capital group, wants to install 553,000 solar panels on 816 hectares about a kilometre from the village of Cercal do Alentejo, with a 25.6 km very high voltage line to Sines. In the public consultation, 241 residents objected and ZERO warned of the artificialisation of rural land. APA approved it with conditions in 2021 and the company redesigned the project, moving the panels away from homes.

What is at stake

The Cercal do Alentejo Solar Photovoltaic Plant, by Cercal Power, S.A., part of the Aquila Capital group, plans about 553,000 panels on a site of about 816 hectares, 320 of them occupied, and an investment of 164.2 million euros. It connects to the grid through an overhead very high voltage line of about 25.6 km to the Sines substation.

Birds of prey. The environmental impact study highlights the red kite, Bonelli's eagle, Montagu's harrier, the stone-curlew and the red-necked nightjar for their high conservation status and likely presence. Along the very high voltage line there is a critical area for raptors, linked to Bonelli's eagle nesting, and another for waterbirds near the Campilhas reservoir, with collision risk. ZERO points out that the red kite and Bonelli's eagle rely on these fields for food and sometimes for breeding.

Bats and small mammals. The study highlights the greater and lesser mouse-eared bats in the plant area, and Cabrera's vole, the purple heron and the reed bunting along the line corridor.

Cork oak woodland. The 25.6 km line crosses pine, eucalyptus, cork oak forest and montado, with felling that ZERO considers significant. At the plant, the survey found more cork oaks than expected and the southern area was left without panels. ZERO warns of the urbanisation of the countryside.

The people of Cercal. The plant is about 1 km from the village. In the public consultation, 241 residents objected.

Timeline

202320222021

20231 entry
  1. 5 Oct

    At the "Hands off the Alentejo Coast" demonstration in Sines, the TAMLA platform lists the Cercal plant among the mega plants that degrade land of high ecological and farming value, built "behind people's backs".

    Source: Jornal O Leme ↗
20221 entry
  1. 8 Jul

    APA approves the redesigned project: the company moves the panels 250 to 500 metres from homes, says it will not cut trees and promises to plant six thousand trees and shrubs around and inside the plant.

    Source: Sábado/Lusa ↗
20213 entries
  1. 29 Jul

    APA issues a conditionally favourable Environmental Impact Declaration: protect holm and cork oaks, keep panels away from watercourses and homes. In the consultation, 241 of the 268 residents represented objected to the project.

    Source: Diário de Notícias/Lusa ↗
  2. 4 Jul

    ZERO warns of the "artificialisation of rural land": the plant takes farmland on which red kites and Bonelli's eagles depend. Residents, farmers and tourism businesses run a petition against the "intensive monoculture of solar panels".

    Source: Postal do Algarve/Lusa ↗
  3. 10 May

    The public consultation on the environmental impact study closes. Residents criticise APA for holding an information session only two days before the deadline.

    Source: Postal do Algarve/Lusa ↗
