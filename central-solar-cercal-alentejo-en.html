Cercal do Alentejo Solar Plant: 553,000 panels on 816 hectares next to the village

Cercal do Alentejo, Santiago do Cacém, Setúbal district

◐ Approved with conditions in 2021 and redesigned in 2022 · no news of construction starting

Cercal Power, part of the Aquila Capital group, wants to install 553,000 solar panels on 816 hectares about a kilometre from the village of Cercal do Alentejo, with a 25.6 km very high voltage line to Sines. In the public consultation, 241 residents objected and ZERO warned of the artificialisation of rural land. APA approved it with conditions in 2021 and the company redesigned the project, moving the panels away from homes.

What is at stake

<p>The Cercal do Alentejo Solar Photovoltaic Plant, by Cercal Power, S.A., part of the Aquila Capital group, plans about 553,000 panels on a site of about 816 hectares, 320 of them occupied, and an investment of 164.2 million euros. It connects to the grid through an overhead very high voltage line of about 25.6 km to the Sines substation.</p>

  1. 5 Oct

    At the "Hands off the Alentejo Coast" demonstration in Sines, the TAMLA platform lists the Cercal plant among the mega plants that degrade land of high ecological and farming value, built "behind people's backs".

    Source: Jornal O Leme ↗
20221 entry
  1. 8 Jul

    APA approves the redesigned project: the company moves the panels 250 to 500 metres from homes, says it will not cut trees and promises to plant six thousand trees and shrubs around and inside the plant.

    Source: Sábado/Lusa ↗
20213 entries
  1. 29 Jul

    APA issues a conditionally favourable Environmental Impact Declaration: protect holm and cork oaks, keep panels away from watercourses and homes. In the consultation, 241 of the 268 residents represented objected to the project.

    Source: Diário de Notícias/Lusa ↗
  2. 4 Jul

    ZERO warns of the "artificialisation of rural land": the plant takes farmland on which red kites and Bonelli's eagles depend. Residents, farmers and tourism businesses run a petition against the "intensive monoculture of solar panels".

    Source: Postal do Algarve/Lusa ↗
  3. 10 May

    The public consultation on the environmental impact study closes. Residents criticise APA for holding an information session only two days before the deadline.

    Source: Postal do Algarve/Lusa ↗
