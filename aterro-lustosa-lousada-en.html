Lustosa landfill, Lousada: reconfiguration in public consultation

Union of the Lustosa and Barrosas parishes, Lousada municipality

⚡ Lousada City Council opposes the changes; CCDR Norte public consultation until 30 September 2026

The Lustosa non-hazardous industrial waste landfill, run by RIMA (a company jointly held by Mota-Engil Ambiente, SUMA, Lousada Municipality and IDAMBI), is in a CCDR Norte public consultation until 30 September 2026 over a cell reconfiguration project, including a new cell 2. Lousada Municipal Council opposes the changes; the landfill has previously drawn controversy for receiving industrial waste imported from Italy.

The Lustosa non-hazardous industrial waste landfill, in the Union of the Lustosa and Barrosas parishes, Lousada municipality, is run by RIMA, a company held by Mota-Engil Ambiente e Serviços, SUMA (Serviços Urbanos e Meio Ambiente), Lousada Municipality and IDAMBI, Estudos e Projetos do Ambiente. It operates under an environmental licence renewed in 2017.

In September 2026, CCDR Norte opened a public consultation, running until 30 September, on the landfill's alteration project, which involves reconfiguring already-licensed, operating cells, including infrastructure for a new cell 2. Lousada Municipal Council opposes the proposed changes.

The landfill has drawn controversy before: in 2020, it was confirmed to be receiving non-hazardous industrial waste imported from Italy, prompting the Bloco de Esquerda to question the Government about the origin, quantity and type of that waste, and about the annual environmental reports RIMA is required to submit to the Portuguese Environment Agency and CCDR Norte.
