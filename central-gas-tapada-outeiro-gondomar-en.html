Tapada do Outeiro gas plant: fossil fuel electricity in Gondomar

Medas, Gondomar, Porto district

◐ Contract extended · activists acquitted in 2026

The Tapada do Outeiro natural gas plant in Gondomar still works as a backup for the electricity system, with the contract extended by the Government, and restarted the grid after the 2025 blackout. In 2023 Parar o Gás activists hung banners on one of its towers against fossil gas; in June 2026 Gondomar court acquitted the nine people accused.

What is at stake

The Tapada do Outeiro Combined Cycle Power Plant, in Medas, Gondomar (Porto district), generates electricity from natural gas, a fossil fuel. Since its power purchase agreement ended in 2024 it has worked as a backup for the electricity system, with the contract extended by the Government.

  1. 29 Mar

    The plant's Power Purchase Agreement ends. The Government sets up a transition period until 31 December, later extended to 31 March 2025.

  1. 25 Mar
