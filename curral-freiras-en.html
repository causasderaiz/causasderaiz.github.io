No. 78 ◐ Monitoring Tourism in protected area

Cable Car and Adventure Park at Curral das Freiras

Curral das Freiras, Câmara de Lobos, Madeira Island

◐ Decision challenged

Quercus-Madeira challenges the decision for not ruling out impact on the critically endangered Zino's petrel.

The cable car and adventure park project in Curral das Freiras, Câmara de Lobos, was approved despite being located in a known nesting area of Zino's petrel, one of the world's most endangered seabirds, endemic to Madeira.

Quercus-Madeira formally challenged the decision, warning that the environmental impact study fails to robustly rule out risk to the species, which is critically endangered.

The Ecologist Party, Os Verdes, with a structure in Madeira, reiterated to the regional ICNF its firm opposition to the project, asking for the licensing decision to be reviewed.
