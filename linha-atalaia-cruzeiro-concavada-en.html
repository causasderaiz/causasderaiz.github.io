No. 62 ◐ Monitoring Interconnection power lines

Atalaia-Torre das Vargens power line and Cruzeiro-Concavada branch: public consultation in the Pego Cluster

Gavião, Abrantes and Ponte de Sor, in the Pego power plant area

◐ Public consultation closed

Environmental impact assessment (EIA no. 3736) of power lines in Endesa's Pego Cluster. CHIRO asked for bat monitoring before it proceeds; the public consultation has now closed.

The Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA no. 3736) of the Atalaia-Torre das Vargens power line and the Cruzeiro-Concavada line branch, promoted by Endesa Generación Portugal, was in public consultation until August 18, 2026. These lines are part of the so-called Pego Cluster, the set of four groups of solar, wind and storage projects with which Endesa occupies the former Pego coal-fired power plant site, across the municipalities of Abrantes, Gavião and Ponte de Sor.

CHIRO, a Portuguese bat conservation association, objected to a reformulation of the related Atalaia-Concavada solar project, asking for monitoring of bat populations before, during and after construction, and for the preservation of the main feeding areas and shelters identified.

The case adds to two others already documented by Causas de Raiz in the same area, next to the Pego power plant: the Hyperion II data center and the "Just Transition" project contested by Gavião Council.
