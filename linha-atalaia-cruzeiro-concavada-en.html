Endesa plans power lines for the Pego Cluster between Gavião, Abrantes and Ponte de Sor

Gavião, Abrantes and Ponte de Sor, in the Pego power plant area

⚡ Public consultation closed

Endesa plans two solar plants, Atalaia (79,830 panels on 113 hectares) and Concavada, plus 220 kV power lines in the Pego Cluster, between Gavião, Abrantes and Ponte de Sor. In the 2025 public consultation, 41 of the 42 citizens who took part disagreed, and Gavião council, the bat conservation association CHIRO and the Vulture Conservation Foundation, on behalf of the cinereous vulture, pointed to the clearing of forest and cork-oak montado, the combined effects with other projects and the risk to bats and birds. The APA (Portuguese Environment Agency) issued a conditional favourable decision on 5 January 2026 and, in August 2026, Gavião council again gave an unfavourable opinion on the power line project.

Endesa Generación Portugal plans, in the Pego Cluster, the Atalaia solar plant (55.89 MWp, 79,830 panels on a fenced area of 113 hectares), the Concavada solar plant (22.62 MWp) and 220 kV power lines linking them to the Comenda and Cruzeiro wind farm substations. The Pego Cluster is the set of solar, wind and storage projects with which Endesa is taking over the area of the former Pego coal plant, between Abrantes, Gavião and Ponte de Sor (environmental impact assessment no. 3736).

The first version received an unfavourable opinion from the Assessment Committee, over significant to very significant impacts on forest and cork oaks and the build-up of infrastructure. Endesa removed one of the Atalaia plant's sections, about 60 hectares, and the modified project was in public consultation from 22 October to 4 November 2025. There were 47 submissions: 41 of the 42 citizens disagreed, citing the destruction of montado and habitats, the felling of protected species and the loss of landscape. CHIRO disagreed with the change and asked for bats to be monitored before, during and after construction. The Vulture Conservation Foundation, for the LIFE Aegypius Return project, noted that GPS-tracked cinereous vultures use the area all year round. Biond and Navigator asked for the felled forest to be compensated.

The APA issued a conditional favourable decision on 5 January 2026: Endesa must define the route of the lines, seek authorisation to fell cork and holm oaks, and submit monitoring and compensation plans. The detailed design of the lines (Atalaia-Torre das Vargens and the Cruzeiro-Concavada branch) was in consultation from 29 July to 18 August 2026, and Gavião council again gave an unfavourable opinion, over the combined impacts of several plants around its villages, the landscape of Margem parish and the conflict with Gavião's municipal master plan.

The case adds to two others already documented by Causas de Raiz next to the Pego plant: the Hyperion II data centre and the "Just Transition" contested by Gavião council.
