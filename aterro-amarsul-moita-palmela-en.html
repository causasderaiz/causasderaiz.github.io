Amarsul landfill between Moita and Palmela: regional authority confirms licence breaches

Palmela Ecopark, Pinhal das Formas, Moita, next to Brejos da Moita

⚡ CCDR-LVT confirmed to ZERO that the landfill breaches its licence (Oct. 2026); Brejos da Moita residents accuse Amarsul of leachate discharges

At the Amarsul landfill, on the border between Moita and Palmela, untreated organic waste is being dumped, which the law has banned since 2020. After several complaints by ZERO, the regional authority CCDR-LVT confirmed in October 2026 that the landfill breaches its licence. Residents of Brejos da Moita accuse Amarsul of leachate discharges that are ruining farmland and cutting off their access to drinking water.

What is at stake

Brejos da Moita residents complain that leachate from the landfill is contaminating the water and the land they farm. ZERO filed several complaints with CCDR-LVT.
