Seven hundred and sixty homes on the old Sacavém barracks, around a convent awaiting heritage status

Former Sacavém Barracks, by the Trancão river, Sacavém e Prior Velho, Loures

🗳 Public consultation until 20 November 2026 · Clarissas housing development, Sacavém Barracks Execution Unit (CCDR LVT)

🗳 Take part in the public consultation

The Clarissas development plans 761 homes in 12 plots, for about 2,284 people, on the 10.3 hectares of the former Sacavém Barracks, home to the Convent of Our Lady of the Martyrs and the Conception of Miracles, which is going through heritage classification. ADAL gave an unfavourable opinion on the environmental impact study and filed a complaint with the Public Prosecutor because the barracks buildings were demolished without archaeological monitoring.

What is at stake

The current public consultation, opened by CCDR LVT, is on the detailed design of the development and accepts submissions on participa.pt until 20 November 2026. Licensing by Loures council depends on the environmental impact assessment.

The convent and what lay beneath the barracks. The Convent of Our Lady of the Martyrs and the Conception of Miracles is going through heritage classification. The attached buildings were demolished and, according to ADAL, without archaeological monitoring, with the rubble crushed on site: whatever was there is lost.

People who live in Sacavém. For ADAL, the town has already endured decades of building without the promised facilities. Its last large open area, historically public, will receive about 2,284 new residents without solving what current residents lack.
