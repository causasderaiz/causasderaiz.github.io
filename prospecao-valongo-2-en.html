Valongo 2: mining prospecting threatens the Serra da Nogueira and Montesinho Natural Park

Serra da Nogueira, Bragança and Vinhais municipalities, Bragança district

◐ Public consultation closed with 886 submissions · ICNF and town councils issue unfavourable opinions

The company GMR - Consultores has asked Portugal's Directorate-General for Energy and Geology for prospecting and research rights over nickel, cobalt, copper and platinum-group deposits across a 105 km2 area covering the northern slope of the Serra da Nogueira, between Bragança and Vinhais, overlapping the Natura 2000 Network and the UNESCO-recognised Transboundary Biosphere Reserve of Meseta Ibérica. The ICNF and the Bragança and Vinhais town councils issued unfavourable opinions; the public consultation gathered 886 submissions.

The "Valongo 2" request (MNPPP614), filed in April 2024 by GMR - Consultores, Lda., based in Braga, asks for prospecting and research rights over nickel, cobalt, copper and platinum-group mineral deposits across a 105 km2 area spanning six parishes of Bragança (Castro de Avelãs, Gostei, Nogueira, Rebordãos, Zoio and the Castrelos e Carrazedo union of parishes) and five parishes of Vinhais. The area covers nearly the entire northern slope of the Serra da Nogueira and overlaps the Natura 2000 Network and the Transboundary Biosphere Reserve of Meseta Ibérica, Europe's largest, recognised by UNESCO.

The Institute for Nature Conservation and Forests (ICNF) issued an unfavourable opinion on the request, as did the Bragança and Vinhais town councils. The public consultation, open from 23 September to 4 November 2024, gathered 886 submissions on the participa.pt portal and formally closed on 27 March 2025, with no final decision made public so far by the Directorate-General for Energy and Geology.

Residents and descendants from Trás-os-Montes mobilised against the process through the UIVO movement, revived after first emerging in 2021 over a mining project in Calabor, on the Spanish side of the border. Sara Riso, a biologist and the movement's spokesperson, questions the process's lack of transparency, noting that most public information sessions took place after the consultation had already ended, and warns that GMR - Consultores is only two years old with 1,000 euros in share capital, even though its partners are linked to major mineral extraction projects, including Savannah Resources, the developer behind the Covas do Barroso lithium mine.

Adorinda Gonçalves, an environmental education lecturer at the Polytechnic Institute of Bragança, warns that even the prospecting phase can fragment critical habitats for species such as roe deer, rare birds and butterflies, and that mining requires large volumes of water in a region already affected by drought. Streams that feed the Castanheira Dam and the Baceiro and Tuela rivers rise within the project area. The Carrazedo commons, managed by one of the most affected communities, see the project as a threat to an area the ICNF itself ranks among the best conserved in the country.
