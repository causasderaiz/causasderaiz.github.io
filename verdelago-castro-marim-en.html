Verdelago: beach access routed through a resort in Castro Marim

Verdelago, Castro Marim, Algarve

◐ APA inspection confirmed conditioned access (Oct 2025)

An APA inspection of 29 Algarve beaches found public access to Verdelago beach, in Castro Marim, conditioned through the interior of a tourism development.

Between August and September 2025, the Portuguese Environment Agency inspected 29 licensed beaches in the Algarve in municipalities "where there were reports of irregularities or a history of incidents". In Castro Marim, the inspection confirmed that access to Verdelago beach runs through the interior of a tourism development, via a gate with a buzzer and unclear signage about the public route.

As a corrective measure, the Agency notified the developer to install clear signage indicating the public access route to the beach and to create alternative parking to the west, closer to the sand, removing the dependence on the conditioned entrance through the development.

The Agency's president, José Pimenta Machado, confirmed that around ten violation notices were issued in this inspection and that offenders have been or will be notified. He noted that concessions "are licensed to occupy a given space" and that public beach access must be guaranteed regardless of neighboring tourism developments.

The Verdelago case is not isolated in the Algarve: in the same inspection, Poço Velho beach, in Albufeira, was found with its main access completely blocked by a gate on private land.
