Alagoas Brancas: a freshwater wetland in the middle of Lagoa

Lagoa, Faro district

⚡ Park promised in 2024 still not built · Parliament recommends protection (Mar. 2026)

Alagoas Brancas, a freshwater wetland with more than 300 species in the town of Lagoa, was due to make way for commercial lots and was bought by the council in 2024 with 3.7 million euros from the Environmental Fund for a natural park. The park has still not been built, and the Movimento Salvar as Alagoas Brancas and associations want it classified as a protected area; in March 2026 Parliament recommended that the Government protect it.

What is at stake

Alagoas Brancas is a temporary freshwater wetland in the town of Lagoa, in the Algarve, in a karst depression over an alluvial aquifer. The land had been earmarked for commercial lots since 2007 and was bought by the council in 2024 for a natural park that has not yet gone ahead.
