Sophia and Beira: petition seeks suspension of both mega solar plants

Fundão, Penamacor and Idanha-a-Nova, Beira Baixa

⚡ Petition continues · Sophia cut by 34% after record consultation (Sep 2026)

Two mega solar plants threaten 231 vertebrate species and over 1,158 hectares of montado in Beira Baixa. After a record public consultation with 12,693 submissions, developer Lightsource bp cut the Sophia project by 34% (from 867 to 573 MWp) and removed it from the area with the greatest irrigation potential; the redesigned project will go to a new public consultation in October 2026 and the plant is not expected to start operating before 2031.

The Sophia (867 MWp) and Beira (269 MWp) projects planned for the municipalities of Fundão, Penamacor and Idanha-a-Nova together cover over 1,158 hectares of montado and classified habitats, putting at risk 231 vertebrate species identified in the environmental impact studies.

The petition delivered to Parliament asks for the immediate suspension of both licensing processes until an independent, more thorough environmental assessment is carried out.

The Beira Baixa region concentrates several large-scale projects at once, which environmental organizations flag as a risk of cumulative habitat loss that case-by-case studies fail to capture.

After a record public consultation with 12,693 submissions, developer Lightsource bp announced in September 2026 a 34% cut to the Sophia project (from 867 to 573 MWp), removing it from the area with the greatest irrigation potential; the redesigned project is expected to go to a new public consultation in October 2026.
