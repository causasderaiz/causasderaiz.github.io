No. 34 ⚡ Active situation Mega solar plant

Lightsource bp's Sophia solar plant is cut by 34% after a record public consultation and awaits APA's decision, between Idanha-a-Nova and Penamacor

Fundão, Penamacor and Idanha-a-Nova, Beira Baixa

⚡ Redesigned Sophia (34% smaller): public consultation closed on 21 October with 8,151 submissions, awaiting APA's decision; Beira cancelled by the developer

Lightsource bp's Sophia mega solar plant lies between Idanha-a-Nova and Penamacor, with a 400 kV line to Fundão. After a record public consultation with 12,693 submissions, the project was cut by 34% (from 867 to 573 MWp, with 1,177 fenced hectares). The consultation on the redesigned version closed on 21 October 2026 with 8,151 submissions; ZERO, Rewilding Portugal and Quercus maintain unfavourable opinions and the decision now lies with APA. The Beira plant was cancelled by the developer.

What is at stake

Lightsource bp planned two mega solar plants in Beira Baixa: Sophia (867 MWp), in Fundão, Penamacor and Idanha-a-Nova, and Beira (266 MW, 425,600 modules on about 524 hectares), in Castelo Branco and Idanha-a-Nova, in areas of cork oak woodland and protected habitats.

The birds. According to ZERO, black stork nesting areas remain inside the project, in a very critical area for the species. The environmental report acknowledges frequent crossings by GPS-tracked cinereous vultures, and the new line of about 22 km adds to others already there. For the first version, the Tejo Internacional defence platform also pointed to the pin-tailed sandgrouse.

The montado. The revised project still involves felling 1,450 isolated trees, 1,195 holm oaks and 255 cork oaks, and still takes up patches of montado, habitat 6310.

20262025

202614 entries
  1. 28 Oct

    The Mega-Protesto movement, bringing together 21 civic and environmental organisations and local groups from the Castelo Branco area, calls a demonstration in Lisbon against Sophia on 21 November at 14:30 in Parque Eduardo VII. It says the projects, "approved without transparency, end up being imposed on them", and calls for strategic land-use planning.

    Source: Agroportal (Lusa) ↗
  2. 22 Oct

    Rewilding Portugal maintains its unfavourable opinion: "Reducing the harm to nature does not mean that harm becomes acceptable". The Castelo Branco branch of Quercus fears the reduction may pave the way for a future expansion. The decision lies with APA, with no date set.

    Source: 24notícias ↗
  3. 21 Oct

    ZERO issues an unfavourable opinion on the redesigned version: it acknowledges the redesign "is significant" but says it "does not yet even reach the standard required of plants in renewable energy acceleration zones".

    Source: ZERO ↗
  4. 21 Oct

    Quercus submits its opinion, maintaining an unfavourable position on approving the project as presented.

    Source: Quercus ↗
  5. 15 Oct

    Cidadãos pela Beira Baixa state their deep disagreement with the reformulated Sophia project, which they say amounts to "cosmetic reorganisations", and urge people to take part in the public consultation by 21 October.

    Source: Diário Digital Castelo Branco ↗
  6. 8 to 21 Oct

    APA opens the public consultation on the redesigned Sophia project (Sophia Solar Photovoltaic Plant and associated very high voltage lines), from 8 to 21 October, on the Participa portal. It closes with 8,151 submissions and moves to "under analysis".

    Source: Participa ↗
  7. 2 Oct

    According to the Non-Technical Summary of the reformulated project, the plant lies in Idanha-a-Nova and Penamacor, with 250 hectares of panels and 1,177 fenced hectares in 22 blocks (previously 1,737 hectares in 46), and a 400 kV line carries the power to the Fundão substation.

    Source: APA, EIA process no. 3800 ↗
  8. Oct

    The Salvar a Gardunha campaign, run by Beira Baixa neighbours, publishes a website with step-by-step guidance for taking part in the new public consultation on the Sophia project, in Portuguese, English, German and French.

    Source: Salvar a Gardunha (campaign by Beira Baixa neighbours) ↗
  9. 21 to 23 Sep

    The developer presents the reformulated project at public sessions in Penamacor, Idanha-a-Nova and Fundão.

    Source: O Interior ↗
  10. 14 Sep

    Lightsource bp abandons the Beira project, citing the lack of financial viability of the reformulated solution.

    Source: ECO ↗
  11. 14 Sep

    Lightsource bp announces a 34% reduction of Sophia, from 867 to about 573 MWp, and its withdrawal from areas with irrigation potential.

    Source: SAPO ↗
  12. 9 Sep

    The Environment Agency sets 2 October as the deadline for Lightsource bp to submit the reformulated Sophia project; a new 10-working-day public consultation follows.

    Source: Green Savers ↗
  13. 19 May

    After a complaint to the Administrative Documents Access Commission, the Environment Agency publishes the consultation reports: they point to permanent and irreversible impacts and, for Sophia, with 12,693 submissions, the need for a very significant reduction of the project.

    Source: SAPO ↗
  14. 5 Jan

    The Platform for the Defence of the Tejo Internacional Natural Park announces it will deliver a public petition against the Beira Baixa mega solar plants to Parliament, followed by a demonstration in Lisbon on 31 January.

    Source: Diário Digital Castelo Branco ↗
20254 entries
  1. Dec

    The assessment committee finds that the Beira project, as submitted, cannot be approved, due to significant negative impacts on ecological systems and land use.

    Source: Diário de Viseu/Lusa ↗
  2. Nov

    The public consultation on the Sophia project exceeds ten thousand submissions, a record for environmental assessment procedures in Portugal.

    Source: Mais Beiras ↗
  3. 30 Oct

    The platform and the Spanish association ADENEX contest the Beira and Sophia projects and announce complaints to APA and the European Commission; the platform estimates "between 7,000 and 9,400 birds killed per year".

    Source: Ambiente Online ↗
  4. 9 Oct

    The Platform for the Defence of the Tejo Internacional Natural Park, made up of Portuguese and Spanish citizens and organisations, says the Beira plant "is the biggest threat" to the park and its special protection area for birds, with more than 140 bird species threatened, and vows to use all legal means.

    Source: Diário de Coimbra/Lusa ↗

What you can do now

  • Monitor. When a public consultation or deadline opens, that is when participation has legal effect.
  • Share the sources, not just the headline. They are all listed above.
  • Follow the Conflitos Ambientais WhatsApp channel to hear about consultations and petitions before they close.
  • Talk to those already on the ground: Cidadãos pela Beira Baixa (Citizens for Beira Baixa), Platform for the Defence of the Tejo Internacional Natural Park, Salvar a Gardunha (Beira Baixa neighbours), ZERO, Rewilding Portugal, Quercus (Castelo Branco branch).
  • If an infraction is happening, know who to report to before calling.
