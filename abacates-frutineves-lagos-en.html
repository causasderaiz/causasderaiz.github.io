No. 127 ◐ Monitoring Irrigated avocado

Avocados in Lagos: 128 hectares planted before environmental assessment, over a strategic aquifer

Espiche, Bensafrim and Barão de São João, Lagos, Faro district

◐ Environmental rejection and offence proceedings (2021)

Frutineves planted 128 hectares of avocado trees in Lagos before any environmental assessment, irrigated with water from the Almádena-Odeáxere aquifer. In 2021 CCDR Algarve issued an unfavourable environmental decision, IGAMAOT opened offence proceedings and Regenerarte took the case to court; the Plataforma Água Sustentável and ZERO contested the project.

What is at stake

Between Espiche, Bensafrim and Barão de São João, in the municipality of Lagos, the company Frutineves planted 128 hectares of avocado trees irrigated from two boreholes in the Almádena-Odeáxere aquifer, a strategic water reserve for the western Algarve, before any environmental impact assessment.

  • If an infraction is happening, know who to report to before calling.
