Gás da Terra biomethane plant in Marés, Abrigada (Alenquer)

Marés, Abrigada e Cabanas de Torres, Alenquer

◐ EIA public consultation closed on 29 September 2026 with 417 submissions; under analysis · Municipal Assembly declared the project of no municipal interest

⚡ Sign the petition

The company Gás da Terra wants to build a €20.5 million biomethane plant about 500 metres from homes in Marés, to process around 84,000 tonnes of agricultural and livestock waste a year. Alenquer council, the Abrigada and Cabanas de Torres parish union and Quercus issued unfavourable opinions; around a hundred people protested in Marés in September. The Environmental Impact Assessment public consultation ran on participa.pt from 19 August to 29 September 2026, received 417 submissions and is now under analysis.

What is at stake

Gás da Terra wants to build a 20.5 million euro biomethane plant in Marés, about 500 metres from homes in Abrigada, in the municipality of Alenquer, to process about 84,000 tonnes of farm waste a year.

Timeline

2026

20268 entries
  1. 15 Sep

    Quercus issues an unfavourable opinion, citing impacts on a watercourse, use of public mains water and the spreading of digestate on farmland without identified plots.

    Source: Quercus, 15 Sep 2026 ↗
  2. 15 Sep

    About a hundred people protest in Marés, joined by the Mayor of Alenquer; the developer expects the Environmental Impact Statement by mid-2027.

    Source: Lusa/RTVON, 15 Sep 2026 ↗
  3. 3 Sep

    At a session held by the parish union of Abrigada and Cabanas de Torres, with about 150 people, the parish presents an unfavourable opinion on the location and a residents' committee against the project is announced.

    Source: Voz de Alenquer, 4 Sep 2026 ↗
  4. Sep

    Alenquer Municipal Assembly unanimously approves a proposal declaring the plant of no municipal interest.

    Source: Local council position (Alenquer) · SAPO / Lusa, 25 Sep 2026 ↗
  5. 25 Aug

    A petition, "Alenquer is against 84 thousand tonnes of waste 500 m from home", is launched, addressed to APA and CCDR Lisboa e Vale do Tejo, calling for the project to be rejected at this location.

    Source: Petição Pública ↗
  6. 19 Aug to 29 Sep

    Public consultation on the Environmental Impact Assessment on the Participa portal, which receives 417 submissions.

    Source: Participa ↗
  7. 6 Aug

    At an information session held by Gás da Terra at the Abrigada philharmonic society, residents repeatedly interrupt the presentation, worried about the plant's proximity to homes.

    Source: Voz de Alenquer ↗
  8. Aug

    Alenquer Council issues an unfavourable opinion: there is no evidence that the plant will not produce odours, and there are concerns about access and the EN1/IC2 road.

    Source: Lusa/RTVON, 15 Sep 2026 ↗
