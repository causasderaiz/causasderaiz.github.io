Gás da Terra biomethane plant in Marés, Abrigada (Alenquer)

Marés, Abrigada e Cabanas de Torres, Alenquer

◐ Public consultation closed 15 September with unfavourable opinion from the council and Quercus

Gás da Terra wants to build a €20.5 million biomethane plant about 500 metres from homes in Marés, to process around 84,000 tonnes of agricultural and livestock waste a year. At a public meeting lasting almost three hours, residents rejected the project over fears of bad odours and heavier truck traffic; Alenquer municipal council issued an unfavourable opinion on 26 August. The public consultation closed on 15 September, with Quercus joining the unfavourable opinion over risks to water and soil; still no final licensing decision.

The company Gás da Terra wants to build a €20.5 million biomethane plant in Marés, about 500 metres from homes in Abrigada, Alenquer municipality, to process around 84,000 tonnes of agricultural and livestock waste a year.

At a public meeting in August 2026, at the Sociedade Filarmónica União e Progresso de Abrigada, residents repeatedly interrupted the company's presentation, rejecting the project over fears of bad odours (a problem they say they already suffer from) and increased heavy-vehicle traffic.

Alenquer municipal council issued an unfavourable opinion on 26 August. The public consultation closed on 15 September 2026; Quercus joined the unfavourable opinion, warning of risks to water and soil contamination. It is the second biomethane project from the same promoter to face opposition in Alenquer within a few months. Still no final licensing decision known.
