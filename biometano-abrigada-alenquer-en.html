Gás da Terra biomethane plant in Marés, Abrigada (Alenquer)

Marés, Abrigada e Cabanas de Torres, Alenquer

🗳 EIA public consultation until 29 September · Quercus, council and parishes opposed · Residents request 60-day extension

The company Gás da Terra wants to build a €20.5 million biomethane plant about 500 metres from homes in Marés, to process around 84,000 tonnes of agricultural and livestock waste a year. Alenquer council, the Abrigada and Cabanas de Torres parish union and Quercus issued unfavourable opinions; around a hundred people protested in Marés in September. The Environmental Impact Assessment public consultation runs on participa.pt until 29 September 2026.

The company Gás da Terra wants to build a €20.5 million biomethane plant in Marés, about 500 metres from homes in Abrigada, Alenquer municipality, to process around 84,000 tonnes of agricultural and livestock waste a year.

At a public meeting in August 2026, at the Sociedade Filarmónica União e Progresso de Abrigada, residents repeatedly interrupted the company's presentation, rejecting the project over fears of bad odours (a problem they say they already suffer from) and increased heavy-vehicle traffic.

Alenquer municipal council issued an unfavourable opinion on 26 August, considering that there is no demonstrated guarantee the plant will not produce odours and raising concerns about access, safety and the road network, in particular the EN1/IC2.

The environmental licensing process, in which the proponent named in the Portuguese Environment Agency notices is the company SGDT 1001, Unipessoal, Lda., has two procedures with public consultation on the Participa portal: the Integrated Pollution Prevention and Control procedure, from 19 August to 15 September 2026, and the Environmental Impact Assessment, from 19 August to 29 September 2026.

On 3 September 2026, at a session organised by the Abrigada and Cabanas de Torres parish union and attended by around 150 people, the parish council presented an unfavourable opinion on the plant's location and the creation of a residents' committee against the project was announced. The Citizens' Movement of Abrigada, Marés and Ota then submitted a request to the Environment Agency for the public consultation to be extended by a further 60 days, citing the complexity and length of the documentation.

On 15 September 2026, Quercus issued an unfavourable opinion, pointing to the direct impact on a watercourse, the consumption of more than 20,000 m³ of mains water a year, and the agricultural spreading of around 87,000 tonnes a year of digestate without identifying the plots where it will be applied, with risks of soil and groundwater contamination. In the same period, around a hundred people demonstrated in Marés, joined by the mayor of Alenquer and the presidents of the two parish councils. According to Lusa, the promoter expects to obtain the Environmental Impact Declaration by mid-2027.
