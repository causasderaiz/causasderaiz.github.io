No. 46 ⚡ Active situation Tungsten mining

Borralha Mine (Salto, Montalegre)

Montalegre, Vila Real district

⚖ Legal action against the APA ongoing · Project approved with conditions in January 2026

Minerália wants to reopen the Borralha tungsten mines, closed since 1986, inside the Gerês-Xurés Biosphere Reserve and 2 km from the Venda Nova reservoir; local communities and associations are challenging the project in court.

What is at stake

Minerália wants to reopen the Borralha mines, in Salto (Montalegre), with 15 years of underground mining of tungsten, copper and tin and a new 12-hectare waste heap. The mine lies in Barroso, a FAO Globally Important Agricultural Heritage System, inside the Gerês-Xurés Transboundary Biosphere Reserve and about 2 km from the Venda Nova reservoir, which supplies water to thousands of people in the North, including Braga.
