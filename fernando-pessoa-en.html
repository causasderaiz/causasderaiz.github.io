No. 43 ⚖ In court Large-scale solar plant

Court suspends the licence for the 1,200 MW Fernando Pessoa solar plant in São Domingos, Santiago do Cacém

São Domingos, Santiago do Cacém, Alentejo coast

⚖ Licence suspended by court order

1,200 MW and 1.5 million trees to be felled for continental Europe's largest solar plant, its licence suspended by court order.

What is at stake

The Fernando Pessoa solar plant, by Iberdrola with Prosolia, was presented as Europe's largest solar plant: 1,200 megawatts on about a thousand hectares in São Domingos, Santiago do Cacém, near Sines, requiring the felling of about 1.5 million trees, mostly eucalyptus.

The land. The plant covers around 1,260 hectares in São Domingos and Vale de Água, with up to 1,000 hectares fenced and 555 hectares of panels for 30 years, 1.2% of the municipality. The environmental decision names the felling of 1.5 million eucalyptus trees as the main negative effect; the land includes National Agricultural Reserve areas. According to ProtegeAlentejo, ICNF gave a negative opinion.

The animals of the woodland. According to the environmental decision itself, the tree cover is shelter and breeding ground for several species, and felling hits the smallest and slowest animals hardest. There is a wood pigeon roost, protected by a 500-metre buffer.

The streams. The decision requires the riverside corridors to be kept and the bankside vegetation restored.

The people of Vale de Água. The village of Vale de Água, scattered homes, holiday lets and the Fonte Serne reservoir are close to the plant. In the public consultation the municipal council asked for the project to be scaled down.

    The court rules that the Environmental Impact Statement's effects are suspended, which halts the subsequent permits. The Environment Agency appeals.

    Source: Observador ↗

    The Environment Agency announces it will contest the action and says it has no suspensive effect.

    Source: ECO ↗
  4. Jan

    The Public Prosecutor brings an action at the Beja Administrative and Tax Court against the environmental licence, for non-compliance with land-use and natural resource protection instruments.

    Source: ECO ↗

  1. 4 Dec

    ProtegeAlentejo files an action at the Beja Administrative and Tax Court to annul the Environmental Impact Statement, pointing to unfavourable opinions from ICNF, LNEG and the head of the Environment Agency's impact assessment department.

    Source: ECO ↗
  2. 5 Oct

    At the "Hands off the Alentejo Coast" demonstration in Sines, the TAMLA platform lists the THSiS plant (the original name of Fernando Pessoa, about 1,000 hectares) among the mega plants that will degrade land of high ecological and farming value and lead to the felling of thousands of trees.

    Source: Jornal O Leme ↗

    The Environment Agency approves the Environmental Impact Statement for the Fernando Pessoa plant, by Iberdrola and Prosolia.
