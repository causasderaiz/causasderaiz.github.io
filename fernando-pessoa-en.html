Fernando Pessoa Solar Plant: Europe's largest keeps stalling in court

São Domingos, Santiago do Cacém, Alentejo coast

⚖ Licence suspended by court order

1,200 MW and 1.5 million trees to be felled for continental Europe's largest solar plant, its licence suspended by court order.

What is at stake

The Fernando Pessoa solar plant, by Iberdrola with Prosolia, was presented as Europe's largest solar plant: 1,200 megawatts on about a thousand hectares in São Domingos, Santiago do Cacém, near Sines, requiring the felling of about 1.5 million trees, mostly eucalyptus.
