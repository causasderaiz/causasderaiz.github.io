Kaya Grounds: demolition and a 60-room resort in the Azenhas do Mar pine forest

Azenhas do Mar, Colares, Sintra

🚩 Demolition under way while the prior information request awaits approval, according to the developer

On an old poultry farm in Rua do Pinhal da Saudade, Azenhas do Mar, inside the Sintra-Cascais Natural Park according to the developer, Kaya Grounds announces a 60-room retreat resort with a spa, a restaurant and two natural swimming pools, with opening announced for 2028 on its website and 2029 on Instagram. The developer's posts show the ruins already being demolished; according to the developer, the prior information request (PIP) is awaiting approval by Sintra council; no ICNF opinion or news report on the project is known.

What is at stake

The plot is an abandoned poultry farm. Kindling by Kaya already runs in the ruins, with a sauna, a wood-fired hot tub, ice baths and Sunday suppers. The developer presents itself as Kaya Grounds, part of a Kaya Collective, with no company name published. On Instagram (@kaya.grounds) the project seeks investors ("Become Kaya Grounds investor") and advertises a community farm.

The ruins and the land. The developer's own Instagram posts show an excavator demolishing the ruins of the old poultry farm ("demolition in progress") and the land being prepared "for the new construction", while another account of the same project says the prior information request is still awaiting Sintra council approval.

The pine forest and the hills. The project puts up a main building with a restaurant, spa, indoor pool, saunas and hammam, four practice rooms, a dome, a garden pavilion and two outdoor natural pools, in the pine forest of Azenhas do Mar, facing the Sintra hills.
