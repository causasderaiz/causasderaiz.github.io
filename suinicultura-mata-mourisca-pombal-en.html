No. 167 ⚡ Active situation Intensive farming and livestock

Suinijanardo wants to open a six-shed pig farm 300 metres from the Castelhanas stream in Mata Mourisca, Pombal, and residents object

Mourisca de Baixo, Mata Mourisca, Pombal

⚠ Licensing under review by Pombal Council; on 5 Nov. 2026 the executive proposed a study on odours, water resources and traffic

Suinijanardo wants to run a pig farm in six sheds at Mourisca de Baixo, between Mata Mourisca and Moita do Boi, in Pombal. Residents have organised a signature drive and a petition against the odours, the effluent and the risk to the Castelhanas stream, 300 metres downstream. The decision lies with Pombal Council, which has asked for a new study.

What is at stake

The alarm was raised on 12 January 2026. Pombal Council says there is no licence yet and that approval will be voted by the executive. The Mata Mourisca parish council says most people are against it and that the roads are not fit for lorries. After a procedural conference, on 5 November 2026 the executive proposed a study on odour emissions, protection of water resources, prevention of contamination and traffic volume.

The Castelhanas stream. The stream runs 300 metres downstream of the sheds and residents say the APA opinion ignored it. The European Commission recognises that nitrates from livestock effluent pollute surface and ground water and cause eutrophication, which strips water of oxygen and kills aquatic life.

People in the surrounding villages. Residents count more than ten affected villages, including Moita do Boi, Louriçal, Vale da Sobreira, Foz, Biqueiras and Castelhanas. They fear odours, flies and the loss of value of homes and land. The parish council says the roads are not fit for lorries.
