No to the privatisation of Madeira's natural trails

Autonomous Region of Madeira

⚡ Petition active

The Regional Government handed management of access to iconic trails to a private security company, without public consultation, drawing opposition from citizens and the LIVRE party.

The Regional Government of Madeira decided to hand over management of access to hiking trails to private entities, contracting the security company Strong Charon for 320,000 euros, from April to December, to control access to sites such as Pico do Areeiro, Ribeiro Frio, Achada do Teixeira, Queimadas and Ponta de São Lourenço.

Citizens concerned with preserving the Autonomous Region's natural, cultural and social heritage are speaking out against any privatisation, concession or abusive restriction of public access to the trails. The LIVRE party also publicly spoke out against the measure, calling it "an attack on public natural heritage".

Critics point above all to the lack of public consultation, transparency or democratic debate before the Regional Government's decision.
