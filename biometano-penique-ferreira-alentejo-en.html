No. 165 🗳 Public consultation Biomethane plant

Penique biomethane: VIDA CIVIS contests FBG's plant next to the olive pomace factory

Penique Agro-industrial Park, parish of Odivelas, Ferreira do Alentejo (Beja)

🗳 Public consultation until 25 November 2026 · Process AIA no. 599 (CCDR Alentejo)

🗳 Take part in the public consultation

FBG wants to build a unit in the Penique Agro-industrial Park, in Odivelas, taking in 69,500 tonnes a year of manure, slurry and olive pomace, next to Casa Alta, a pomace factory that is already a source of odours. VIDA CIVIS gave an unfavourable opinion: the study does not assess combined odours or the house 906 metres away. The public consultation closes on 25 November.

What is at stake

The Penique Agro-industrial Park lies about 8 kilometres from the town of Ferreira do Alentejo, among olive groves, orchards and cork and holm oak montado, in the Sado basin (Figueira and Odivelas streams). The study itself admits the region has a high potential for odour emissions, with recorded complaints. In the same area there is another CapWatt biomethane unit in Aljustrel and a CapWatt project in Figueira dos Cavaleiros (AIA no. 574), which VIDA CIVIS already opposed in March 2026.

People in Odivelas and the farmsteads. The study admits it could not assess the cumulative impact of odours, hydrogen sulphide and ammonia together with the plants already running in the park, including Casa Alta, which processes olive pomace. The dispersion model only considers Odivelas, 3 kilometres away, and leaves out the house 906 metres away. The solid digestate sits on a platform covered only against rain, and lorries unload in the open air.

Water and streams. The unit will bring in about 10,220 m³ of water a year by tanker lorry from an EDIA irrigation hydrant, which may cut supply between 15 November and 28 February, in the middle of the pomace season. The 61,587 tonnes of liquid digestate go to farmland the study does not identify, with storage for about 44 days, in a basin where VIDA CIVIS asks for groundwater nitrate monitoring.

The road and the village. About 23 lorries a day use the EN2, up to 35 in the olive season, on top of the lorries for Casa Alta and CapWatt. The plant stores about 34 tonnes of liquefied biomethane, and the fire brigade and civil protection were not consulted in the project design.
