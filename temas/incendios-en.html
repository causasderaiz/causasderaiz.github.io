Forests and wildfires: environmental struggles and conflicts in Portugal

Rural wildfires, clear-cutting, eucalyptus and the felling of cork oaks and pine woods: forests, and what is done with them, are one of the country's major environmental issues. These are the cases we document around forests and wildfires, with those following them and how to take part.
