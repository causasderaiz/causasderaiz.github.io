Mega solar: environmental struggles and conflicts in Portugal

Large-scale solar plants are taking over cork oak woodland, farmland and entire landscapes. Local communities and associations ask that the energy transition does not come at the expense of the land. These are the environmental struggles and conflicts we document around mega solar plants.
