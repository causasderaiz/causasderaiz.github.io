Water: environmental struggles and conflicts in Portugal

Dams, water transfers, desalination, intensive irrigation and polluted rivers: water is at the heart of many environmental struggles. These are the conflicts we document over rivers, streams, lakes and aquifers, with those defending them and how to take part.
