Pollution of the Lis river basin: sewage and pig farm discharges, no solution in sight

Lis river basin, Leiria and Marinha Grande municipalities

⚡ Claimants seek €256,000 in damages after a 2025 discharge of up to 25,000 m³ of raw sewage; Oikos confirms a pollution hotspot on the Lena river in 2026

For decades the river Lis, in Leiria, has received effluent discharges from the basin's roughly three hundred pig farms, reported to the GNR by the Ribeira dos Milagres Environment and Defence Committee; the promised treatment plant is still not built. In August 2025, a breakdown at the Monte Real pumping station released between 20,000 and 25,000 m³ of raw sewage into the river, with swimming banned at Vieira Beach, and in 2026 Oikos confirmed a pollution hotspot on the Lena river, a Lis tributary.

What is at stake

The Ribeira dos Milagres Environment and Defence Committee, whose spokesperson Rui Crespo grew up by the stream, has been reporting discharges to the GNR for years.

What you can do now

  • Monitor. When a public consultation or deadline opens, that is when participation has legal effect.
  • Share the sources, not just the headline. They are all listed above.
  • Talk to those already on the ground: Comissão de Ambiente e Defesa da Ribeira dos Milagres (Ribeira dos Milagres Environment and Defence Committee), Águas do Centro Litoral, Oikos, Associação de Defesa do Ambiente e do Património da Região de Leiria, Marinha Grande City Council, Portuguese Environment Agency.
  • If an infraction is happening, know who to report to before calling.
