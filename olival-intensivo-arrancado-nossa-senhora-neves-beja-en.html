Intensive olive grove uprooted in Nossa Senhora das Neves: five years of complaints by a Beja resident

Nossa Senhora das Neves, Beja, Beja district

✓ Victory: olive grove uprooted in 2023, after five years of complaints

In October 2018 an intensive olive grove was planted about 10 metres from a house, on the edge of the village of Nossa Senhora das Neves, in Beja, against the municipal plan. Then came pesticides without warning, complaints to the council, the GNR, public health and the Ombudsman, and two agreements left unfulfilled. Uprooting began in March 2023.

What is at stake

The plantation lies in the Alqueva irrigation area, where intensive olive groves cover about 60% of irrigated land. Beja council's technical services proposed halting the planting for breaching the municipal plan before it went ahead; the opinion was not acted on. The resident founded the Movimento Alentejo Vivo with other citizens and with ZERO.

The family next door. The olive trees stood about 10 metres from the house. From March 2019 pesticides were applied regularly, without warning, and the family called the GNR environmental unit (SEPNA) each time.

The municipal plan. The grove was planted inside the village boundary, against the municipal plan. The law lets the mayor halt works that breach the plan, but the halt proposed by the council's technical services never went ahead.

The victory. After two agreements between the council and the developer, the first left unfulfilled, the intensive grove began to be uprooted in March 2023, to make way for an extensive carob orchard.
