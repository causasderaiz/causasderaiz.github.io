Vale de São Gião (Milharado, Mafra): hill levelled and asbestos buried next to watercourses

Casais da Serra and Vale de São Gião, Milharado parish, Mafra, Portugal

⚡ Residents report asbestos buried and a hill levelled by LASO; project submitted to the Council, still unlicensed (Jul 2026)

In Vale de São Gião, next to Montachique Park (Mafra), the transport company LASO has since late 2024 bought the former estate of architect Pardal Monteiro and the Casal do Alfaiate, demolished the buildings and levelled the hill and its trees. According to residents and a Mafra Council source quoted by oRegiões, asbestos-cement roofing was buried on site, next to watercourses that were diverted; residents have already filed complaints with ICNF, APA, SEPNA, the GNR and the Council.

In late 2024, according to residents and as Mafra Council confirmed to the newspaper oRegiões, the company LASO bought, in Vale de São Gião, Milharado parish, the estate that once belonged to architect Porfírio Pardal Monteiro, still with the buildings he designed. Within about a month the buildings were demolished, followed by the destruction of the woodland. The buildings were not listed. According to oRegiões, some bodies, namely SEPNA (the GNR's environmental unit), issued administrative offence proceedings, and the works, which were unlicensed at the time, were halted for a few months.

The same company then bought the Casal do Alfaiate, a former branch of the Freixial poultry farms, and immediately began demolishing the buildings and levelling the hill, with machines working from 7 am to 9 pm every day, including weekends. According to oRegiões, sheds with asbestos-cement roofs were demolished and buried in a watercourse that crossed the property and was diverted to its edge; the two watercourses that crossed the properties were merged into one. Residents and a Council source told the newspaper that materials were not separated and ended up buried and piled into "plateaus/mounds". Under Portuguese law (Decree-Law 266/2007), asbestos materials may only be removed by companies authorised by the Working Conditions Authority (ACT).

On 24 June 2026, a reader's letter published in Jornal de Mafra reported that "the mountain was levelled", that the trees (pines, ashes and some cork oaks) were destroyed and buried together with the buildings, and that asbestos-cement roofs are "under the ground, next to an existing watercourse that has since been altered". The author says they submitted a complaint to Mafra Council, which promised to act.

At the Milharado Parish Assembly session of 23 June 2026, the board of the Vale de São Gião residents' association presented three points, attached to the minutes. The parish council president, Pedro Cardoso, replied that, "regarding the situation of the company LASO", he had already filed a complaint with Mafra Council "for a pavement to be built in the area of the works" and "that he had not been aware of the construction project".

According to oRegiões (14 July 2026), residents have filed reports with ICNF, APA, SEPNA, the GNR and Mafra Council, and the case was presented at the Municipal Assembly on 30 June 2026. Meanwhile, the project for these plots has been submitted to the Council, but they are not yet licensed.
