LASO works next to Vale de São Gião, Milharado (Mafra)

Casais da Serra and Vale de São Gião, Milharado parish, Mafra, Portugal

◐ Residents raise the issue at the Parish Assembly (23 Jun 2026); parish council says it did not know about the construction project

Works by LASO Transportes, a heavy and special transport company headquartered in Casais da Serra, next to the Vale de São Gião housing estate (Milharado, Mafra), were raised by residents at the Parish Assembly on 23 June 2026. The parish council president replied that he had already complained to Mafra Council about the lack of a pavement near the works and that he had not known about the construction project.

LASO Transportes, a heavy and special transport company, has its headquarters and fleet maintenance centre in Casais da Serra, in the parish of Milharado (Mafra), next to the Vale de São Gião housing estate.

At the Milharado Parish Assembly session of 23 June 2026, during the public participation period, Henrique Macedo, from the board of the Vale de São Gião residents' association, presented three points, which were attached to the minutes. In reply, the parish council president, Pedro Cardoso, said that, "regarding the situation of the company LASO", he had already filed a complaint with Mafra Council "for a pavement to be built in the area of the works" and "that he had not been aware of the construction project".

Causas de Raiz is following the case and looking for official documents and news about the works. Anyone with information can send it through the Alert page.
