BioAdvance at Figueira da Foz port: biofuel plant without an environmental licence, under investigation for pollution

Vila Verde, Figueira da Foz commercial port, Mondego estuary

⚖️ Coimbra prosecutors investigate a possible crime of pollution endangering the public; according to the APA and CCDR Centro, the plant has run since February 2026 without an environmental licence or EIA approval

BioAdvance built a plant in the port of Figueira da Foz, on the Mondego estuary, to make biodiesel from used cooking oil. It ran without the required licences, lost its PIN (national interest) status and is under investigation by Coimbra prosecutors for a possible crime of pollution endangering the public. Residents complain of odours and fumes and launched a petition, backed by Vila Verde Parish Council; Quercus is calling for the plant to be shut and dismantled.

What is at stake

Residents are contesting the plant's odours and fumes in a public petition, backed by Vila Verde Parish Council. Quercus has joined the opposition.
