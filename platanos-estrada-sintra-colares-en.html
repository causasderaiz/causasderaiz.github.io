No. 174 ⚡ Active petition Tree felling and pruning

Infraestruturas de Portugal fells and prunes century-old plane trees on the EN247 between Sintra and Colares, and associations and residents object

National Road 247 between Sintra and Colares (Galamares), ER 247, EN 247-4 and EN 9-1, Sintra-Cascais Natural Park

⚡ Injunction against the works in Galamares (Jan. 2026) · Petition against abusive pruning (May 2026)

⚡ Sign the petition

On 29 December 2025, Infraestruturas de Portugal began felling two plane trees on National Road 247 in Galamares, inside the Sintra-Cascais Natural Park. Finis Terrae filed an injunction against the works and residents formed pickets. In May 2026, nine associations launched a petition against the pruning of dozens of monumental trees on the roads between Sintra, Colares and Malveira da Serra.

What is at stake

It is not the first time. In October 2021, the ICNF authorised IP to fell 454 of the 495 trees on the stretch between Pé da Serra and Cabo da Roca, with an opinion valid for two years. IP carried out 661 prunings and 480 fellings, 183 of them invasive exotic species. In January 2024, the Park's nature wardens issued an infringement notice because the works went on after the opinion had expired, after the Friends of Sintra's Trees and GEOTA had written to the ICNF and IP contesting the scale of the felling.

The plane trees of the Colares road. The plane trees of the EN 247 follow the tram line between Sintra and Colares. Finis Terrae speaks of systematic, continued felling and wants the trees classified.

Pruning of monumental trees. The petition refers to dozens of monumental trees in the management strip of the ER 247, EN 247-4 and EN 9-1 and asks for a guarantee that they will not be severely pruned.

The Park's opinions. In 2024, the ICNF fined IP for working with an expired opinion. In Galamares, IP and the ICNF say the stretch is an urban zone and needs no opinion, although it lies inside the Sintra-Cascais Natural Park.
