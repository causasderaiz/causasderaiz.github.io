Terms of use

A public map

Causas de Raiz is a public map of environmental struggles and conflicts in mainland Portugal, Madeira and the Azores. This page explains how the content published here can be used.

Using the map

The content of the map is freely accessible and may be viewed, shared and used by anyone, namely for information, teaching, research or journalism.

When quoting or reproducing information from the map, we kindly ask that the source (Causas de Raiz) and a link to the case page be given, so readers can consult the original sources.

Copyright

The texts, summaries, case files, original photographs and design of this site are original work by Causas de Raiz and are protected by Portuguese copyright law (Código do Direito de Autor e dos Direitos Conexos). This right arises automatically upon creation of each piece of content, it does not depend on registration or notice, but we state it explicitly here so there is no doubt.

Database protection

Beyond the copyright in each individual text, the set of cases we gather, verify and organize constitutes a database of its own, protected under the sui generis database right established by Portuguese Decree-Law 122/2000, transposing EU Directive 96/9/EC. This right exists precisely because there has been substantial investment in obtaining, verifying and presenting this information, case by case, with cross-checked and confirmed sources. It protects the collection against extraction or reuse of a substantial part of the data without authorization, even when each individual fact is public.

Base cartography

Maps use data from OpenStreetMap contributors, under their respective open license.

Final note

This page describes how we understand and protect our work, it does not replace legal advice.
