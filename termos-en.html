Terms of use

How to use this content

This is a simple, plain-language summary, not a formal legal document. It describes how you can use what we publish.

Site content

The text, summaries and case sheets may be quoted and shared, crediting the source (Causas de Raiz) and, whenever possible, linking back to the original page. Please do not present it as independently verified fact without that reference.

Accuracy of information

We verify every case before publishing it, following the process described in Methodology. Even so, the situation on the ground can change between checks. Each case page shows the date it was last verified. If you find an error or outdated information, let us know.

Links and external sources

The sources listed for each case link to third-party sites (press, organisations, public bodies). We do not control the content of those sites and cannot guarantee they will remain available at the same address.

Open data

Some structured case data is available for reuse at /dados/casos.json and /dados/casos.geojson. Terms of reuse are agreed case by case; contact us before reusing it publicly.

Contact

Questions about these terms? Write to geral@ulmeiro.org.
