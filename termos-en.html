Terms of use

What's free and what isn't

This page explains what anyone can do with the content and data of Causas de Raiz, and what needs our prior authorization. We wrote this because we are a public resource, but the verification work behind each case has value, and it is ours.

Copyright

The texts, summaries, case files, original photographs and design of this site are original work by Causas de Raiz and are protected by Portuguese copyright law (Código do Direito de Autor e dos Direitos Conexos). This right arises automatically upon creation of each piece of content, it does not depend on registration or notice, but we state it explicitly here so there is no doubt.

Database protection

Beyond the copyright in each individual text, the set of cases we gather, verify and organize constitutes a database of its own, protected under the sui generis database right established by Portuguese Decree-Law 122/2000, transposing EU Directive 96/9/EC. This right exists precisely because there has been substantial investment in obtaining, verifying and presenting this information, case by case, with cross-checked and confirmed sources. It protects the collection against extraction or reuse of a substantial part of the data without authorization, even when each individual fact is public.

What you can freely do

Free

Browse and share

View the map, read case files and share direct links to them on social media, in conversation or in news coverage.

Free

Cite with attribution

Cite a single case in an article, publication or academic work, with clear attribution to Causas de Raiz and a link to the source page.

Free

Use the contact

Suggest corrections, new cases or additional sources through our contact email.

What needs our prior authorization

Authorization

Bulk extraction

Copying, indexing or automatically scraping the full set of data, text or photographs from the site.

Authorization

Incorporating into another database

Integrating our cases, in whole or in substantial part, into another map, report, product or database, academic, institutional or commercial.

Authorization

Republishing as your own

Reproducing texts, case files or photographs from the site elsewhere without attribution to Causas de Raiz.

Partnership or data-sharing requests

We regularly receive requests from other initiatives, researchers and projects to discuss synergies. We are open to that, but any data sharing, formal collaboration or use of our work by third parties is assessed case by case and defined in writing before it happens. Write to geral@causasderaiz.org.

Third-party photographs

Some photographs published in case files are provided by associations, collectives or communities involved in the causes. In those cases, rights over the image remain with the original author; we use them only to illustrate the corresponding case, with the understanding of whoever provided them.

Base cartography

Maps use data from OpenStreetMap contributors, under their respective open license.

Final note

This page describes how we understand and protect our work, it does not replace legal advice. For any question about use or collaboration, contact us directly before acting.
