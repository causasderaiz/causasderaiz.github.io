Know of a struggle that is not on the map?

Tell us what is happening. Nothing goes on the map without checking: we read every submission and only publish with sources anyone can consult. That is why we always ask for at least one link or document.

What happens next: we read your submission, look for independent sources and, if the case meets our methodology, it goes on the map. If not, we keep it under observation.
