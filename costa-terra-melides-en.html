Costa Terra: luxury resort closes off public beaches in Melides

Melides, Grândola (coastline between Tróia and Melides)

⚡ Developer Coporgest threatens injunction against public access to Camarinhas and Duna Cinzenta beaches · Quercus urges Environment Agency to stand firm (Sep 2026)

The luxury resort Costa Terra keeps private security and fencing restricting access to public beaches. An official inspection found seven beaches with conditioned access in Grândola; a year on, Quercus accuses the Government of hiding the fact that the barriers remain.

What is at stake

Between Tróia and Melides, in Grândola, the Costa Terra luxury resort occupies an estate of about 850 hectares on the coast, developed by Discovery Land Company. Private security, fences and identity checks restrict access to public beaches in the area.
