No. 154 ◐ Monitoring Crimes against nature

Swallow nests destroyed: 29 ICNF proceedings and hundreds of removal licences in two years

Mainland Portugal

◐ 29 administrative proceedings (2023 to 2025) · cases under investigation in Golegã and Alcanede

Destroying swallow nests has been banned in Portugal since 1999, even when empty and after the birds have left. Between 2023 and 2025 the ICNF opened 29 administrative proceedings for nest destruction, and in 2024 and 2025 it licensed 680 removals. In 2026 the GNR is investigating the destruction of nests with eggs and chicks in Golegã and at the Alcanede fire station. The point on the map marks the Alcanede fire station as an example: the problem is nationwide.

What is at stake

The ban comes from Decree-Law 140/99, which transposes the Birds Directive: it is forbidden to destroy, damage, collect or keep the nests and eggs, even empty, of any wild bird. Removal can only be licensed by the ICNF in cases set out in law, such as public health, safety or building renovation. Fines range from 250 to 3,740 euros for individuals and from 3,990 to 44,890 euros for companies and condominiums. The ICNF only handles cases inside protected areas; the rest go to the regional authorities (CCDR).

The swallows. House martins and barn swallows build their mud nests under the eaves, balconies and porches of houses. Pulling down a nest in the breeding season kills the eggs and chicks; pulling it down in winter takes away the nest the birds return to in spring.

The licences. In 2024 the ICNF received 348 removal requests and licensed 309. In 2025 it received 397 and licensed 371, 200 of them in the Alentejo. To avoid droppings, the ICNF and SPEA recommend small boards or trays under the nests.

Golegã and Alcanede. In Golegã, near the Paul do Boquilobo wetland, more than 15 active nests with eggs and chicks disappeared in May 2026 from the eaves of a house on Rua Dom João IV. In June, at the Alcanede fire station, a complaint says several nests with eggs and chicks were destroyed while the vehicle yard was being washed.
