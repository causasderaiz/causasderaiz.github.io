Estoi Solar Plant: 87 MWp on National Ecological Reserve land in the Algarve Barrocal

Cerro de Leiria, Santa Catarina da Fonte do Bispo, Tavira, Faro district

✓ Victory: APA rejected the plant in August 2023 and Iberdrola gave up

Iberdrola wanted to build an 87 MWp solar plant at Cerro de Leiria, in Tavira, on National Ecological Reserve land in an aquifer protection area. PROBAAL mobilised local people and the public consultation received 861 submissions, with strong opposition. In August 2023 APA issued an unfavourable decision and Iberdrola dropped the project.

What is at stake

The Estoi Solar Plant, by Iberdrola Renewables Portugal, came out of the 2020 solar auction and planned 87 MWp with battery storage inland in Tavira, connected to the Estoi substation. The land lies in the Algarve Barrocal, on National Ecological Reserve land and in an aquifer protection area. It would cover about 154 hectares, with about 175,800 panels, near Santa Catarina da Fonte do Bispo.

    APA issues an unfavourable decision: very significant, irreversible and non-mitigable negative impacts on ecological systems, water resources, the local economy and the landscape.

  2. 4 Aug

    After the draft unfavourable decision, Iberdrola says it will not appeal and drops the project.

    Source: Algarve Primeiro ↗
  3. 21 Jul

    PROBAAL says it was one of the most participated consultations ever and accuses Iberdrola of not explaining how it chose the site or involving local people, as the Aarhus Convention requires.

    Source: Postal do Algarve ↗
  4. 19 May

    Public consultation on the environmental impact study opens, until 20 July. It receives 861 submissions, 847 from citizens, and APA's report describes strong opposition. ZERO, Quercus, GEOTA, Almargem and the Portuguese Botanical Society also object.

    Source: APA, Public Consultation Report, EIA 3621 ↗
