Ovar dune pine forest: clear-cutting of 363 hectares of pine

Ovar Dunes Forest Perimeter, Ovar, Aveiro district

⚡ ICNF keeps the clear-cuts planned until 2038 · + Pinhal asks Parliament to suspend them (Jun. 2026)

⚡ Sign the + Pinhal petition →

ICNF's plan for the Ovar dune pine forest, about 2,584 hectares, provides for cutting about 363 hectares of pine by 2038. The + Pinhal association opposes clear-cutting over large areas, carried out without an environmental impact study, and is asking Parliament to suspend operations, despite a 2024 parliamentary recommendation to change the cutting method.

What is at stake

The Ovar Dunes Forest Perimeter has about 2,584 hectares of pine forest on the Ovar coastal dunes, managed by ICNF. The 2024 Forest Management Plan provides for cutting about 363 hectares of pine by 2038.

Timeline

20262 entries
  1. 18 Jun

    MEP Catarina Martins (BE) asks the European Commission to stop the felling, "an ecological crime", noting that the 363-hectare cut goes ahead without an environmental impact study, in plots under 10 hectares.

    Source: Agroportal/Lusa ↗
  2. 2 Jun

    + Pinhal takes a petition to Parliament calling for the immediate suspension of operations "based on clear-cutting", an independent investigation into the plan and a new, publicly debated plan. It says that instead of small-area cuts there is "continuous clear-cutting of large areas".

    Source: Diário de Aveiro ↗
20252 entries
  1. 11 Apr

    After visiting felled plots with + Pinhal, MP Inês Sousa Real (PAN) says neither the municipality, ICNF nor the Government is complying with Parliament's recommendation: "It makes no sense for the pine forest to lose more than 360 hectares".

    Source: Rádio Ria ↗
  2. 12 Mar

    Ovar residents formally set up the + Pinhal Nature Conservation Group to oppose the 2024 plan: clear-cuts over large areas, heavy machinery on the dunes, weak control of acacias and no public consultation.

    Source: Campo Aberto ↗
20241 entry
  1. 18 Jul

    Parliament approves a recommendation that the Government, through ICNF, revise the Ovar Dunes Forest Perimeter Management Plan and change its cutting method. It stems from the 2022 petition "Save the Ovar dunes forest perimeter", with over 19,000 signatures.

    Source: 24notícias ↗
