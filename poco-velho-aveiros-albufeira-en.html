No. 72 ◐ Monitoring Beach access

Poço Velho and Aveiros: two blocked-access beaches in Albufeira

Poço Velho and Aveiros, Albufeira, Algarve

◐ APA inspection confirmed blocked access (Oct 2025)

An APA inspection of 29 Algarve beaches found public access to Poço Velho beach, in Albufeira, blocked by a gate on private land. Not an isolated case: nearby Praia dos Aveiros had already been closed off by an identical gate in 2014.

Between August and September 2025, the Portuguese Environment Agency inspected 29 licensed beaches in the Algarve in municipalities "where there were reports of irregularities or a history of incidents". In Albufeira, the inspection confirmed that the main access to Poço Velho beach is closed by a gate on private land, with no regularized parking nearby. The Agency is now identifying and contacting the landowners, aiming to reopen the gate and coordinate parking and access planning with the municipal council.

The Poço Velho case is not isolated in the municipality. In 2014, Praia dos Aveiros, in the same area, had its access blocked by a gate after a tourism developer claimed ownership of the access path in court. The Albufeira court eventually ordered the municipal council to restore the public signage, but the dispute left a precedent for how easily a private developer can, in practice, condition access to a legally public beach.

The 2025 inspection also found other irregularities linked to beach concessions in the Algarve: at Monte Gordo, bathroom facilities closed or restricted to concession clients, despite access to bathrooms being universal by law; and occupation of the sand beyond the licensed area at Armação de Pêra and at Portimão beaches such as Rocha, Alvor and Prainha.

The Agency's president, José Pimenta Machado, confirmed that around ten violation notices were issued in this inspection and that offenders have been or will be notified.
