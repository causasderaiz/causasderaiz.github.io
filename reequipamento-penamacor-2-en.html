Penamacor 2 Wind Park re-equipment: Government authorises felling of 755 cork and holm oaks

Serra de Santo André, Meimoa and Penamacor parishes, Castelo Branco district

🔴 Felling authorised by government order · re-equipment under way

The Government, by joint order of the Secretaries of State for the Environment and Forests, authorised the felling of 451 cork oaks and 304 holm oaks (755 protected trees) for the re-equipment of the Penamacor 2 Wind Park, on the Serra de Santo André ridge, declaring the project of "public utility". The seven existing turbines will be replaced by four new ones, nearly quadrupling annual output, from 23.48 GWh to 88.44 GWh.

By joint order of the Secretaries of State for the Environment and Forests (Despacho n.º 775/2026, of 23 January), the Government authorised the felling of 451 cork oaks and 304 holm oaks, a total of 755 legally protected trees, to enable the re-equipment of the Penamacor 2 Wind Park, on the ridge of the Serra de Santo André, in the parishes of Meimoa and Penamacor, Castelo Branco district.

The project, submitted by the company Lestenergia – Exploração de Parques Eólicos, S.A., was declared of "public utility" by the Government. The goal is to nearly quadruple the park's average annual electricity output, from the current 23.48 GWh to 88.44 GWh per year: to achieve this, the seven existing wind turbines will be removed and replaced with four new, higher-capacity turbines.

As compensation for the felling, the project foresees afforestation of 9.988 hectares (6.885 ha with cork oak and 3.103 ha with holm oak), spread across six discontinuous plots, plus cork oak densification on a further 2.736 hectares; the total compensation area is 12.724 hectares. The case fits a wider pattern: according to Executive Digest, the Government authorised the felling of over 9,000 protected trees in just two months, across several wind repowering projects nationwide.
