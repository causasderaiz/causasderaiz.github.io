Comporta: Dunas Livres challenges eight luxury and golf mega-projects on the dunes and pine forest

Comporta and Carvalhal, Grândola and Alcácer do Sal, Setúbal district

◐ Eight mega-projects under way · beach access restricted until 2027

Since the sale of Herdade da Comporta, eight luxury mega-projects, each covering hundreds of hectares, are advancing over the pine forest and dunes of Comporta, according to Dunas Livres. Golf courses and swimming pools use water in a drought-hit region, homes have become unaffordable for local people, and access to public beaches has been closed with gates.

What is at stake

Herdade da Comporta, 12,000 hectares of rice fields, pine forest and dunes between Tróia and Melides, was sold off in parcels after the collapse of the BES bank. Vanguard Properties, Amorim Luxury and Discovery Land Company launched luxury homes, holiday villages, hotels and golf courses; Discovery Land's CostaTerra alone plans nearly 300 villas. The estate agency Knight Frank ranks Comporta among the world's five most sought-after luxury residential markets. The Aquaterra Group, which already runs over 7,000 hectares of intensive farming in Alcácer do Sal, has offered to buy the remaining 11,000 hectares of the estate, currently under court seizure.

The water. According to Dunas Livres, hotels with golf courses, pools and many tourist beds use a great deal of water in a region already threatened by drought. The group's manifesto warns about exploitation of the Sado aquifer and the risk of saltwater intrusion.

Dunes and pine forest. Each mega-project covers hundreds of hectares of pine forest, dunes and rice fields. The Dunas Livres manifesto counts five new golf courses and four resorts on dune ecosystems on the coast between Tróia and Sines.

The people of Comporta. A small house worth 20,000 euros twenty years ago is now worth a million. Some residents have already left because of house and shop prices. The road to Pego beach was closed by a luxury condominium from October 2022; at Galé-Fontainhas and Camarinhas access is still restricted, and new access works are not due to finish until 2027.
