Stop the tree felling in Costa da Caparica

Praça Padre Manuel Bernardes and Torre das Argolas, Costa da Caparica, Almada

⚡ Residents count more felled trees than announced

A beach-access upgrade project in Costa da Caparica (phase 2), costing around €3 million, led Almada municipal council to announce the felling of 47 trees, but residents count 103 trees already cut or marked for felling in Praça Padre Manuel Bernardes and Torre das Argolas, including at least 23 in June alone. A public petition calls for an immediate halt to the felling of the 34 trees still marked, for the demolition of Praça dos Navegantes to stop, and for more transparency and dialogue with residents before the works proceed.

A beach-access upgrade project in Costa da Caparica (phase 2), costing around €3 million, led Almada municipal council to announce the felling of 47 trees in Praça Padre Manuel Bernardes and Torre das Argolas.

Residents, however, count 103 trees already cut or marked for felling, including at least 23 in June 2026 alone, and question the council's criteria. The council has pledged to plant 120 new trees in compensation.

A public petition, now past 2,600 signatures, calls for an immediate halt to the felling of the 34 trees still marked, for the demolition of Praça dos Navegantes to stop, and for more transparency and dialogue with residents before the works proceed.
