Terra Ruiva solar plant: 83,000 panels and 527 holm oaks felled in the Serra do Caldeirão

Alcarias de Baixo and Amoreira, Cachopo parish, Tavira (Serra do Caldeirão)

🗳 Public consultation until 9 December 2026 · APA process no. 3969

🗳 Take part in the public consultation

Chint Solar Portugal wants to fence off about 69 hectares of the Serra do Caldeirão, in Cachopo, for a solar plant with 83,472 panels, felling 527 holm oaks and one cork oak. The environmental impact study is in public consultation until 9 December.

What is at stake

The hills. About 69 hectares of hillside in the Serra do Caldeirão are fenced off and covered by 83,472 panels, next to the villages of Alcarias de Baixo and Amoreira.

The holm oaks. 527 holm oaks and one cork oak, trees protected by law, are felled. The planting promised in return is on another plot, leased for 20 years. Even so, the developer's study says the impacts on plants and wildlife are "negative and not very significant".

Birds of prey and the lynx. Citizens and associations point out that the hills are habitat for birds of prey and a migration route, with the Bonelli's eagle, and lie close to the Iberian lynx breeding area in Alcoutim.

The soil. Clearing and earthmoving compact the soil and increase erosion on the slopes.

People of the hills. The landscape at the villages' doorstep changes, and a 60 kV overhead line with ten poles runs about 1.5 km to the Tavira substation.
