For a sustainable future for São Luís, Odemira

São Luís, Odemira, Poldreiros do Meio / Lameiros

⚡ Petition active

⚡ Sign the petition →

A mega photovoltaic plant covering 100 to 150 hectares inside the Natura 2000 network is mobilising the Juntos por São Luís movement, part of a national platform against megaprojects.

The Juntos por São Luís movement is raising the alarm over a mega photovoltaic plant planned for the Poldreiros do Meio and Lameiros area, in the parish of São Luís, municipality of Odemira, covering between 100 and 150 hectares inside the Natura 2000 network, in National Ecological Reserve areas.

Juntos por São Luís is part of the Movimento Energia Justa e Sustentável (MEJS), a national platform created by nine civic movements and associations against the spread of solar and wind megaprojects "with unacceptable location and scale", defending a just energy transition.

The petition calls for the project's site to be reviewed and for already artificial land, such as rooftops and industrial zones, to be prioritised before farmland and forest land.
