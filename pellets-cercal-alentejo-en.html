"We can't breathe": pellet factory in Cercal do Alentejo

Cercal do Alentejo, Santiago do Cacém

⚡ Fire contained · Opposition continues

⚡ Sign the petition →

A fire at a pellet factory, active for ten days, exposed the community to toxic smoke and reignited opposition to the plant built next to the village.

A fire at the pellet factory in the Cercal do Alentejo Business Zone began on 21 July, starting in a silo that burst, spreading to wood stored outside and to a neighbouring rural area. It was only brought under control ten days later, on 31 July.

The Juntos pelo Cercal civic movement says residents were exposed to toxic smoke for more than two weeks, with complaints of respiratory problems and eye irritation, and warns that the village's proximity to the factory leaves the community "always exposed to harmful factors" from the industrial zone.

The movement is demanding the immediate suspension of the factory under current conditions, an independent environmental impact study on human health and the ecosystem, and public participation in decisions about the territory.
