Peneda-Gerês National Park: citizens' petition calls for stronger protection

Peneda-Gerês National Park (Mata da Albergaria, Terras de Bouro)

◐ Citizens' petition to Parliament

⚡ Sign the petition →

A group of citizens who use Peneda-Gerês National Park is asking Parliament to stop the Park's degradation: roads closed to organised events, a director chosen by open competition, more resources for ICNF and nature wardens, and visitor limits on roads, viewpoints and waterfalls. The petition came after the Tour of Portugal crossed the Mata da Albergaria, the best-preserved native oak forest in the country.

What is at stake

Peneda-Gerês is Portugal's only national park and part of the Gerês-Xurés Transboundary Biosphere Reserve recognised by UNESCO. In the Homem river valley lies the Mata da Albergaria, the best-preserved native oak forest in the country, with centuries-old oaks, yews, Iberian wolves, Pyrenean desmans and gold-striped salamanders. The petition points out that a 2023 law requires a director for the Park to be appointed by open competition, which has not yet happened.
