For the permanent closure of the Lajedo quarry, Monte Córdova

Monte Córdova, Santo Tirso

⚡ Illegally reactivated · Petition active

After more than a decade inactive, the Lajedo quarry was illegally reactivated and expanded into an ecological reserve zone, reigniting decades of residents' complaints.

The Lajedo quarry, in Monte Córdova, Santo Tirso, sat inactive for over ten years until it was reactivated in 2024, with an illegal expansion into an ecological reserve zone. For decades, residents complained of blasts that cracked walls and threw stones onto roofs, on top of noise and pressure on soils and water resources.

A group of citizens launched a public petition demanding the permanent closure of the quarry, arguing that the operation undermines the well-being and quality of life of the local population. In March 2026, the company operating the quarry went as far as threatening to cut off a public road that had been in use for 30 years.

The dispute between the company and residents remains unresolved, with Santo Tirso City Council also involved in the process.
