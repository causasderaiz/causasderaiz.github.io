No to Wind Turbines on Serra d'Ossa: petition against wind farm in Central Alentejo

Serra d'Ossa, Central Alentejo (Estremoz, Redondo, Borba, Vila Viçosa and Alandroal)

⚡ Petition with more than 430 signatures within days · Calls on Estremoz Council and the Government to halt the project · An earlier project already failed to get approval for measurement towers

A public petition calls for a wind farm project on Serra d'Ossa, in Central Alentejo, to be halted, and for the mountain range to be classified as a protected area.

Serra d'Ossa, in Central Alentejo, is threatened by a project to install wind turbines on its slopes. A public petition, launched on 15 September 2026 and addressed to the Government, the Portuguese Parliament, the Ministry of Environment and Climate Action, the Portuguese Environment Agency and Estremoz Municipal Council, gathered more than 430 signatures within days, calling for the project to be halted and for the mountain range to be classified as a protected area.

Serra d'Ossa is considered one of the most emblematic landscapes of Central Alentejo, with high environmental, scenic, historical and cultural value: besides protected habitats and populations of birds and bats, the range holds megalithic, Roman and medieval archaeological remains. Petition signatories warn that building wind towers, access roads and power lines would endanger this heritage, as well as the nature tourism, wine tourism and rural tourism that support part of Estremoz's economy.

In March 2025, the mayor of Estremoz, José Daniel Sádio, publicly confirmed that a wind project planned for Serra d'Ossa had failed to get approval for the installation of measurement towers, and did not even reach the municipal-interest evaluation stage, unlike another wind project at Herdade da Fonte Figueira, which the council declared of municipal interest at the same meeting. The new petition aims to stop any future attempt to install wind turbines on the range.

The petition calls for the rejection of wind farm projects on Serra d'Ossa, for the range to be classified as a protected area, for a rigorous environmental impact assessment with effective public participation, and for local communities to be involved in every decision about the territory.
