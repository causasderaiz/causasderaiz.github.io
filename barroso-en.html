No. 47 ⚖ In court Lithium mining

Lithium mine in Covas do Barroso

Boticas, Vila Real, Barroso region

⚖ Court suspends easement · Commoners reject Savannah's agreements (Aug 13)

Savannah Resources wants to open an open-pit mine in a centuries-old farming area. The Mirandela Administrative and Tax Court suspended the administrative easement granting access to the commons; on August 13, the Assembly of Commoners overwhelmingly rejected Savannah's attempt to present recent agreements as a sign of social acceptance of the project.

British company Savannah Resources wants to open an open-pit lithium mine in a centuries-old farming area, classified as a Globally Important Agricultural Heritage System by the FAO. Covas do Barroso has resisted the project for eight years.

On 29 May 2026, the Administrative and Tax Court of Mirandela admitted a precautionary injunction filed by the Assembly of Commoners of Covas do Barroso parish against the Ministry of Environment and Energy, suspending the effects of a 228-hectare administrative easement in Covas do Barroso and Romaínho and the geotechnical works that depended on it. Savannah Resources confirmed on 9 June that it had been notified of the temporary suspension order, saying it would resume work once authorised; the company noted this was the third precautionary injunction filed against the project since 2024.

From August 6 to 10, 2026, between 700 and 800 people take part in the Camp in Defence of Barroso, organized by Unidos em Defesa de Covas do Barroso with support from A Sachola, Disgraça and Jornal Mapa.

In parallel, ClientEarth and Unidos em Defesa de Covas do Barroso brought the case to the Court of Justice of the European Union, questioning the legality of the project's environmental licensing.
