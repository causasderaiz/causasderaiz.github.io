Morgavel cork oaks: 1,821 protected trees felled for a wind farm in Sines

Morgavel, Sines municipality, Alentejo coast

⚖ Case against the felling dispatch continues at the Beja court; appeal at the Central Administrative Court South

Donate: Vamos Salvar os Sobreiros (Let's Save the Cork Oaks) →

In 2023, the Environment Ministry declared the Morgavel Wind Farm, by Parque Eólico de Moncorvo (EDP), of essential public interest and authorised the felling of 1,821 cork oaks next to the Southwest Alentejo Natural Park. A citizens' movement took the case to court, but the trees were cut down in 2024 while the case was ongoing. The Public Prosecutor requested a copy of the case for criminal and disciplinary purposes.

What is at stake

The Morgavel Wind Farm, in Sines, lies in a cork oak montado at the gates of the Southwest Alentejo and Vicentine Coast Natural Park. The cork oak is protected by law and is Portugal's national tree. The independent citizens' movement "Together Let's Save the Morgavel Cork Oaks" is challenging the felling in court and says some of the felled trees bore marks of having been stripped for cork.

20244 entries
  1. 25 Nov

    After the Beja court declared the injunction "supervening futile", the movement appeals to the Central Administrative Court South. The main action continues in Beja and the Public Prosecutor requested a copy of the case for criminal and disciplinary purposes.

    Source: Observador/Lusa ↗
  2. 27 Jul

    At the TAMLA platform's third action, on Vasco da Gama beach in Sines during the Músicas do Mundo festival, spokesperson Teresa Manuel denounces the cork oaks felled for the Morgavel wind farm and asks people to help watch over those still standing.

    Source: Dunas Livres ↗
  3. Jun

    The movement denounces the "illegal felling" of hundreds of cork oaks, between 300 and 500 according to spokesperson Avani Ancok, while the case is still in court, and calls a direct action on site.

    Source: Ambiente Online/Lusa ↗
  4. May

    The cork oaks are cut down, according to the movement "in defiance of what the court had provisionally decided".

    Source: Observador/Lusa ↗
20234 entries
  1. Nov

    Through GAIA, the movement files an injunction and then the main action (case no. 321/23.4BEBJA) at the Beja Administrative and Tax Court to annul the dispatch. It is backed by the Regenerativa association of São Luís, Odemira.

    Source: Voz da Planície/Lusa ↗
  2. 5 Oct

    At the "Hands off the Alentejo Coast" demonstration in Sines, the TAMLA platform lists the Morgavel Wind Farm among the projects that will lead to the felling of thousands of trees, such as cork oaks and other legally protected species.

    Source: Jornal O Leme ↗
  3. 26 Aug

    About 200 people march in Lisbon to the Environment Ministry against the felling. The "Let's save the cork oaks" movement calls the compensation measures, planting about 40,000 cork oaks, "a fallacy"; Quercus speaks of "greenwashing".

    Source: DN/Lusa ↗
  4. 1 Aug

    Environment minister Duarte Cordeiro issues a dispatch recognising "the essential public interest of the Morgavel wind project" and authorising the felling of 1,821 cork oaks for the farm, promoted by Parque Eólico de Moncorvo, linked to EDP.

    Source: Ambiente Online/Lusa ↗
