50-million-euro hotel in Vale do Olival, Armação de Pêra

Vale do Olival, Armação de Pêra, Silves municipality, Algarve

⚡ Construction underway on an unstable cliff; petition with hundreds of signatures

Silves Municipal Council issued, on 3 December 2025, the building permit for a four-star hotel with 250 rooms and capacity for 500 guests, a 50-million-euro investment, on one of the last vacant beachfront plots in Armação de Pêra. The land sitting on an unstable cliff sparked strong backlash on social media and a public petition; the council held an information session in December, but construction has continued.

Silves Municipal Council issued, on 3 December 2025, the building permit for a four-star hotel in Vale do Olival, Armação de Pêra, with 250 rooms, capacity for 500 guests, indoor and outdoor pools, and technical and parking areas. The architecture project was approved under the Armação de Pêra Detailed Plan and received favourable opinions from Turismo de Portugal and the Portuguese Environment Agency. The investment is estimated at 50 million euros, with a 36-month construction schedule.

The process sparked strong local controversy, especially on social media, because the plot occupies one of the last vacant beachfront spaces in Armação de Pêra and sits on a cliff considered unstable, on top of worsening existing parking problems in the area. A public petition against the project gathered several hundred signatures.

Facing the backlash, Silves Municipal Council held a public information session on 18 December 2025 at the "Os Armacenenses" football club in Armação de Pêra to explain the licensing process. Site preparation works had already started before that session and continued as of September 2026.
