2026 wildfire wave: Vouzela becomes Europe's largest fire of the year

North and Centre: Vouzela, Torre de Moncorvo, Valpaços, Mirandela, Águeda · involved: ICNF, AGIF · verified 03/09/2026

Over 65,000 hectares burned by the end of August, 58% above the ten-year average. The Vouzela fire, which started on 2 July, is officially Europe's largest fire of 2026, accounting for around 70% of all area burned in the country.

Why this is here

This case asks for no signature. It explains the ground on which the other struggles happen: a country that burns, more often and more intensely each year. Without this context each struggle looks isolated. With it, the pattern shows.
