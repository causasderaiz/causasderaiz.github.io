Sines Industrial Zone: thousands of cork oaks felled for industrial plots

Sines Industrial and Logistics Zone (ZILS), Sines, Alentejo coast

🗳 Clearing of area A2 (347 ha, 3,593 cork oaks) in public consultation until 12 November

🗳 Take part in the public consultation →

To hand plots over to companies, the Sines Industrial and Logistics Zone is being cleared in phases. In March 2026 the Government authorised the felling of almost 3,500 cork oaks; since 1 October the clearing of 347 hectares of area A2, home to 3,593 cork oaks, has been in public consultation until 12 November. GEOTA opposes cutting montado, a protected habitat.

What is at stake

The Sines Industrial and Logistics Zone (ZILS), managed by AICEP Global Parques, contains large areas of pine woodland, cork oak woodland and montado. To hand plots over to companies, the land is cleared in phases, and felling cork oaks, a tree protected by law, requires Government authorisation.

Timeline

20262025

20263 entries
  1. 1 Oct

    Public consultation opens on clearing Execution Unit A2 of the ZILS, until 12 November: 347.62 of its 443 hectares will be cleared in four phases, with 3,593 cork oaks identified in the area.

    Source: AICEP Global Parques ↗
  2. 27 Mar

    A new order authorises felling 3,006 cork oaks on 13.81 hectares (four plots) of the ZILS. Preparing the land is the job of AICEP Global Parques.

    Source: ECO ↗
  3. Mar

    A Government order authorises felling 450 cork oaks on 2.68 hectares, covering 10 ZILS plots; six of them are earmarked for a Repsol Polímeros solar plant for its own use.

    Source: ECO ↗
20251 entry
  1. 30 Jun

    GEOTA criticises the plan to clear area 1A3 of the ZILS, where the environmental impact study foresees felling 14,890 trees, including 2,475 cork oaks. It notes that montado is a protected habitat and argues that solar plants should use already built-up land.

    Source: GEOTA ↗
