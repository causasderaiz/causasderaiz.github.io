Decontamination of soils and aquifers on Terceira Island: nearly seven decades of contamination from Lajes Base

Terceira Island, Azores, next to Lajes Base

⚡ Active petition · oversight committee created

Nearly seven decades of US military presence at Lajes Base left dozens of contaminated sites. Decontamination process stuck in a diplomatic deadlock.

Over nearly seven decades, the US military presence at Lajes Base, on Terceira Island, left dozens of contaminated sites, documented by the Portuguese Air Force and the National Civil Engineering Laboratory (LNEC). Between 2020 and 2022, the United States carried out no remediation work, holding that the soil and aquifer contamination was not harmful to health.

The decontamination process is stuck in a diplomatic deadlock, with neither Portugal nor the United States taking on concrete responsibility. An active public petition calls for effective environmental decontamination of the island's soils and aquifers.

The Azores Parliament approved, with support from several parties, the creation of an Independent Technical Committee to oversee, monitor and evaluate the process. The Azores branch of the Socialist Party and the Left Bloc have asked the national Government for updated information and political priority on the issue.
