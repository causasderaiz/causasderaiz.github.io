No. 109 ◐ Monitoring Data centre · municipal planning

Figueira da Foz approves, with Chega's votes, positioning the municipality as a data centre destination

Figueira da Foz municipality, Coimbra district

◐ Proposal approved at council meeting · municipal technical team preparing dossier

Figueira da Foz Municipal Council approved, at a meeting on 14 May 2026, a Chega proposal to position the municipality as a destination for data centre investment, with seven votes in favour and two abstentions from the Socialist Party. The mayor acknowledged difficulties in securing enough renewable energy areas to support this type of infrastructure.

At the public council meeting of 14 May 2026, Figueira da Foz Municipal Council approved, with seven votes in favour and two abstentions from the Socialist Party (PS), a proposal put forward by Chega's sole councillor, Hugo Fresta, to position the municipality as a preferred destination for data centre investment.

The original proposal called for a working group to be set up to produce, within 120 days, a preliminary technical dossier on the municipality's territorial readiness to host this kind of infrastructure. In the vote, that part was replaced by assigning the task to an existing multidisciplinary municipal team instead.

Mayor Pedro Santana Lopes said Figueira da Foz was already working in this area, with several investment opportunities under consideration, but admitted the proposal is only partially feasible given the difficulty of securing enough renewable energy capacity to support data centres: infrastructure with very high energy and water demands that has sparked environmental opposition elsewhere in the country (such as in Sines and Santiago do Cacém).
