Sintra sem Herbicidas: against glyphosate use in public spaces

Sintra municipality

⚡ Petition archived with no change to the law; movement launches mailing campaign to public bodies (Sep 2026)

Sintra sem Herbicidas is a citizens' movement active since 2015 campaigning to end the use of herbicides, glyphosate in particular, for weed control in public spaces across Sintra municipality. It filed a petition to the Portuguese Parliament for a ban on dangerous herbicides in urban public spaces, which was heard by the Agriculture and Fisheries Committee, and secured a motion passed by the Colares parish assembly in April 2026 demanding an end to glyphosate use. The group has documented and reported to Sintra Council and to GNR-SEPNA cases of herbicide applied near watercourses and schools, even after the council publicly committed to stopping the practice, and took part in public consultations on the national action plans for pollinators and for the sustainable use of pesticides.

What is at stake

The Sintra sem Herbicidas movement reports the use of glyphosate and other herbicides in public spaces in Sintra and calls for a nationwide ban in urban public spaces.
