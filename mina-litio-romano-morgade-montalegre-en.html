No. 166 ⚖ In court Lithium mining

Romano Mine: open-pit lithium and refinery in Morgade, Montalegre

Morgade, Montalegre (Barroso, Vila Real district)

⚖ Lawsuits by Montalegre council and Montalegre Com Vida · Conditionally favourable EIS (Sep 2023) · No approved site for the refinery

Lusorecursos wants to open an open-pit and underground lithium mine in Morgade, in Barroso, with a lithium hydroxide refinery. APA approved the mine with conditions in 2023 but rejected the refinery site because it lies in the breeding territory of an Iberian wolf pack. The Montalegre Com Vida association and Montalegre council are fighting the project in court.

What is at stake

Barroso, in Montalegre and Boticas, is a FAO Globally Important Agricultural Heritage System, with communal grazing, commons and Barrosã cattle. It faces two lithium projects: the Barroso Mine, in Covas do Barroso, and the Romano Mine, in Morgade. The Leiranco Iberian wolf pack has been known in the area since 1996.

The Leiranco pack. The project area overlaps the territory of the Leiranco Iberian wolf pack, known since 1996. The 2019-2021 national wolf census confirmed that the proposed refinery site interferes with its breeding site. The Iberian wolf is Endangered in the Red Book of Mammals. APA considers the impact of the industrial complex, running day and night, very significant.

The Alto Rabagão water. The industrial complex needs 10,000 cubic metres of water a day, from streams, rain, the mine itself and, from June to October, the Alto Rabagão reservoir. In drought, APA warns this abstraction may have significant impacts. The concession includes part of the protection area of the Reboredo public water supply.

People in Morgade, Carvalhais and Rebordelo. During mining about 150 lorries a day. APA points to very high landscape impacts on the villages of Morgade, Carvalhais and Rebordelo and dust in Carvalhais. The refinery uses hydrofluoric acid, with a risk of major accident for the surroundings.
