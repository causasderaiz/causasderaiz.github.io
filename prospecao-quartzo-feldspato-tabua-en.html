Residents oppose the quartz and feldspar prospecting sought by Minas de Cassiterite Sobreda in Tábua

Vale de Gaios, Midões, Póvoa de Midões, Candosa, Tábua and Covas, Tábua municipality, Coimbra district

⚡ Active petition · Prospecting and exploration application MN/PPP/535 at the DGEG, no known decision

⚡ Sign the petition

Minas de Cassiterite Sobreda has asked the DGEG, the Directorate-General for Energy and Geology, which grants mining rights, for the right to prospect and explore for quartz and feldspar across 22 km² of Tábua municipality, about 11% of its land, between the Cavalos river and the Aguieira reservoir on the Mondego, in the parishes of Midões, Póvoa de Midões, Candosa, Tábua and Covas e Vila Nova de Oliveirinha. In the 2023 public consultation all 254 submissions were against, including the Póvoa de Midões parish council, and residents, AZU and Munda gathered in protest at the Cavalos river weir in Vale de Gaios. The application came back in 2026 under the name «Tábua», and the Tábua Sem Minas movement launched a new petition.

What is at stake

In an opinion of 27 June 2022, Tábua council said there were no exclusion zones or restrictions on the application, but asked for protection of the villages of Vale de Gaios, Sevilha and Várzea de Candosa, the Trilho dos Gaios, the walking routes, watermills, the Sumes and Sevilha bridges, the Roman road, farmland, the Aguieira Reservoir Management Plan and the protection zones of water abstraction points. AZU and Munda criticised the fact that the parishes were not consulted and recalled the pollution left by the Urgeiriça and Mondego Sul mines. Many citizens said part of the population had no way to take part through the online portal, without internet or email. The DGEG report describes the application as one for silica sands and special clays, whereas the map, the public notice and the project description refer to quartz and feldspar. According to the Tábua Sem Minas movement, the same application reappeared on 3 September 2026 under the name «Tábua».

The Cavalos river, the Mondego and the springs. According to the petition, the area is crossed by the Cavalos river and the Mondego, by other streams and by many springs. The DGEG map shows the northern edge of the area on the shore of the Aguieira reservoir. The council itself asked for the protection zones of water abstraction points to be safeguarded. Citizens who took part in the consultation fear contamination of surface and groundwater, recalling what they lived through with the Urgeiriça uranium mines.

People who live and farm in the villages. The area takes in the villages of Vale de Gaios, Sevilha and Várzea de Candosa and small farms and olive groves. The Póvoa de Midões parish council told the consultation that the negative impacts are high for family livelihoods, the environment and public health. According to the petition, dust, blasting and lorry traffic from a future mine would harm crops, livestock and the people who live here. Citizens also fear erosion and the use of land without permission.

The valley, the trails and the mills. Exploration pits and trenches are dug into the ground, and mechanical drilling bores into the rock. The Cavalos valley holds the Trilho dos Gaios with its wooden walkways, watermills, the Sumes and Sevilha bridges and the Roman road, which the council and the «Crescer» association want protected. According to the petition, mining would bring deforestation and the destruction of ecosystems.
