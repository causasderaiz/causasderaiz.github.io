Super-intensive olive grove in Ervidel: the village opposes a plantation a few metres from its houses

Ervidel, Aljustrel, Beja district

◐ The movement wants the olive grove moved away · public path cut off in November 2025

In Ervidel, in the municipality of Aljustrel, a super-intensive olive grove was planted 10 to 20 metres from the houses, on a slope above the village and in the direction of the prevailing winds. The Movimento Ervidel em Ação wants the grove moved away: the municipal plan (PDM) provides for 250 metres between plantations and the village boundary. In January 2025 mud ran down from the grove into houses and yards in the lower part of the village.

What is at stake

The olive grove covers about 120 hectares around Ervidel and was planted over a watercourse that runs through the village. Between Ervidel, Ferreira do Alentejo and Beja, the movement counts more than 15 consecutive kilometres of olive groves. The area lies next to the Roxo reservoir.

The people of Ervidel. The olive trees stand 10 to 20 metres from the houses, while the PDM provides for 250 metres, and the 10-metre tree buffer the plan requires along the village boundary is missing. Pesticides are applied by aerial and high-pressure spraying, on a slope above the village with the wind blowing towards the houses. The movement wants this treated as a public health problem.

Water and soil. Soil scraping and a grove planted over the watercourse sent mud into the lower part of the village in January 2025, damaging houses and yards. Rainwater from the grove is still channelled into the village drainage system, which is overloaded. According to the movement, the land is losing its insects and its usual wildlife and plants. According to the local CDU councillors, rainwater from the grove flows through the village drainage into the Roxo reservoir, which supplies drinking water.

The village path. In November 2025 the owner cut off the public path, used for decades, between Travessa de Ferreira and Quinta Nova. Residents and farmers now have to go around the village.
