Alcoutim wind farms: 48 turbines over 200 metres tall in the northeast Algarve

Alcoutim (Martim Longo, Giões, Alcoutim and Pereiro), Faro district

◐ The Assessment Committee gave an unfavourable opinion on both projects · developers submitted revised versions · no final decision known yet

Two projects want to add wind farms to Alcoutim's solar plants: 23 turbines by Galp (Viçoso, Pereiro, São Marcos and Albercas) and 25 in the Solara4 project, over 200 metres tall, some less than 800 metres from villages. The Assessment Committee gave an unfavourable opinion on both: very significant, non-mitigable negative impacts. The developers submitted revised versions, which the Platform for Sustainability and Biodiversity of the Algarve and Alentejo and SPEA are contesting.

What is at stake

In the northeast Algarve, between the Caldeirão hills and the Guadiana, two projects want to add wind farms to existing solar plants: Galp's Wind Hybridisation Projects for the Viçoso, Pereiro, São Marcos and Albercas Solar Plants, with 23 turbines, and the Solara4 Wind Farm at the Alcoutim Solar Plant, with 25. They lie about 6 km apart, next to the Ribeira do Vascão Ramsar Site and near the Guadiana Valley Natural Park.

  3. 4 Feb

    Consultation closes on the revised Solara4 project (25 turbines, 165 MW), submitted after an unfavourable opinion. SPEA calls for its final rejection: Malfrades and Monte das Preguiças lie less than 800 metres from the turbines, and the main breeding core of Bonelli's eagle is at risk.

    Source: Postal do Algarve ↗
  4. 20 Jan

    The Platform for Sustainability and Biodiversity of the Algarve and Alentejo warns about the two projects, Solara4 and Galp, which could add up to 48 turbines over 200 metres tall, some a few hundred metres from homes, and criticises the short Solara4 consultation.

    Source: Postal do Algarve ↗

    The Assessment Committee gives an unfavourable opinion on Galp's projects: very significant, non-mitigable negative impacts on ecological systems, above all on birds with an unfavourable conservation status.

    Public consultation opens on Galp's wind projects in Alcoutim: 23 turbines of up to 6.2 MW, 142.6 MW in total, about 13 km of new access roads. It runs until 2 May 2025.
