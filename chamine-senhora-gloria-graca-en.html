Graça: black smoke from a chimney every night on Rua da Senhora da Glória

Rua da Senhora da Glória, Graça, São Vicente parish, Lisbon

⚡ Petition to the council and São Vicente parish; IGAMAOT says inspection is the council's job

Residents of Graça, in Lisbon, complain that the chimney of a bakery oven on Rua da Senhora da Glória gives off black smoke almost every night, with a strong smell of burnt plastic or rubber, causing respiratory irritation and worsening asthma. Complaints go back to 2025; in 2026 they launched a petition to Lisbon council and São Vicente parish.

On 26 July 2025, a public complaint on the DECO Proteste consumer platform reported that the chimney of a bakery's wood-fired oven at no. 90 Rua da Senhora da Glória, in Graça, gives off black smoke every day between 8.30 pm and 1 am, with a very strong smell of burnt rubber that, according to the complaint, burns the airways and worsens asthma symptoms. The complaint remains unresolved on the platform.

On 1 June 2026, residents launched a petition addressed to Lisbon City Council and São Vicente Parish Council, "End the toxic emissions from the chimney on Rua da Senhora da Glória, Graça, Lisbon". According to the petition, almost every night, usually between 9 pm and 1 am, the chimney releases dense black smoke with a strong smell of burnt plastic or rubber, and signatories report respiratory irritation and worsening asthma; despite several complaints, nothing has been solved. In an update on 26 June 2026, the petition says the General Inspectorate for Agriculture, Sea, Environment and Spatial Planning (IGAMAOT) confirmed that inspection is the council's responsibility and forwarded the case to it.
