Argemela mine: local people reject open-pit lithium mining

Serra da Argemela: Barco and Coutada (Covilhã), Silvares and Lavacolhos (Fundão), Castelo Branco district

◐ APA rejected the environmental impact study; the concession holder must restart the assessment

Since 2021, Neomia, Minérios Argemela (Almina Group) has held a concession contract to mine lithium and other minerals over 403.7 hectares in the Serra da Argemela, between Covilhã and Fundão. Local people and the Group for the Preservation of Serra da Argemela reject open-pit mining. In 2025, the APA environment agency rejected the environmental impact study for lack of detail, and the company has to restart the whole assessment process.

The Serra da Argemela lies between the municipalities of Covilhã and Fundão, near the villages of Barco, Coutada, Silvares and Lavacolhos, and was mined for tin for decades. In 2019, according to Diário Digital Castelo Branco, the Group for the Preservation of Serra da Argemela (GPSA) and local people already opposed open-pit mining ("Open-pit, no!"), and on 21 May that year the Government rejected a request for experimental mining by PANNN, Consultores de Geociências, because no environmental impact assessment had been submitted.

On 28 October 2021, a concession contract was signed to mine lithium and other minerals over an area of 403.7 hectares, with Neomia, Minérios Argemela (Almina Group).

In an opinion dated 30 December 2024 and made public in April 2025, the Portuguese Environment Agency (APA) rejected outright the request for an environmental impact assessment of the mine, because the study did not have "the level of detail needed for the execution project stage" and showed "gaps in the project and in key environmental factors". The president of GPSA, Gabriela Margarido, said she hoped the Government would "recognise, once and for all, the mistake that authorising mining in Argemela would be".

On 17 April 2025, the Secretary of State for Energy, Jean Barroca, told Rádio Covilhã that, to start mining the concession, the company "will have to restart a process" of assessment, with a new public consultation, and that there is no deadline for doing so. As of October 2026, we found no news of a new environmental impact study.
