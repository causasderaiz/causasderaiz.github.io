"Just Transition" at the Pego power plant: Gavião unanimously rejects PIN-status renewables project

Pego power plant, between Abrantes and Gavião, Centro/Alentejo

◐ Rejected by council, national PIN granted

A €700 million project combining solar, wind and storage on the site of the former Pego coal plant holds PIN status. Gavião rejected it unanimously, saying the municipality already has about 2% of its territory covered by renewables.

Endesa is moving forward with a €700 million project combining solar power, wind power and storage on the site of the former Pego coal-fired power plant, presented as part of the region's "just transition". The project already holds National Interest Project (PIN) status.

Gavião Municipal Council unanimously rejected the project's PIN status. The council says the municipality already has about 2% of its territory covered by renewable energy projects and argues it is becoming a "sacrifice zone" serving the energy transition of other regions.

In July 2026, the site of the former Pego power plant also became one of the locations eligible for a national 300 MVA renewables-plus-storage tender, part of the National Energy Storage Strategy, which also includes Rio Maior, Sines and Santo André. This tender is separate from Endesa's PIN project: of the roughly 600 MW of grid capacity available at the site, about 280 MW are already allocated to Endesa's project, leaving between 300 and 320 MW available for new developers. Tender applications opened on 14 September 2026; the mayor of Abrantes voiced support for the initiative.

The case adds to other energy and digital projects concentrated in the same area, next to the Pego power plant, including a second large-scale data center already reported (see the Hyperion II case, also documented by Causas de Raiz).
