Merlin data centre campus in Vila Franca de Xira: 180 MW, up to 300 MW

Castanheira do Ribatejo, by the station, Vila Franca de Xira

🏗 Under construction since October 2024; first unit expected in 2027

Merlin Properties and Edged Energy are building, next to Castanheira do Ribatejo station in Vila Franca de Xira, a campus of five data centres for artificial intelligence, with 180 MW, which may reach 300 MW. EDP will build a nearby solar plant of up to 100 MWp to power it.

What is at stake

The promoters have not disclosed the area occupied, the previous land use, the distance to homes in Castanheira do Ribatejo or the grid connection.
