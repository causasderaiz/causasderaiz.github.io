Humberto Delgado Airport: expanding Portela inside Lisbon

Portela, Lisbon

⚡ Environmental impact study at APA · works planned for 2027

ANA/Vinci wants to raise the capacity of Humberto Delgado Airport, inside Lisbon, from 38 to 40 and then 42 movements per hour, and submitted the environmental impact study in June 2026. The Public Prosecutor is challenging the works already under way without environmental assessment at the Supreme Administrative Court. Residents, the Menos Aviões (ATERRA) movement, ZERO and Climáximo oppose the expansion, the noise and night flights.

What is at stake

Humberto Delgado Airport, at Portela, has operated inside the city of Lisbon since 1942. ANA, the concession holder owned by the French group Vinci, wants to raise capacity from 38 movements per hour until the new airport in Alcochete opens. Residents, movements for reducing aviation and environmental groups contest the noise, night flights and works without environmental impact assessment.
