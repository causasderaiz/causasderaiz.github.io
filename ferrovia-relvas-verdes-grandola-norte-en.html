Relvas Verdes–Grândola Norte railway: a Sines freight line through the Serra de Grândola

Serra de Grândola, between Relvas Verdes (Santiago do Cacém) and Grândola, Setúbal district

◐ Section proposed in the National Rail Plan (2023), opposed by residents and Grândola council · unclear whether it stayed in the final version

In 2023 the National Rail Plan proposed a new freight section between Sines and Grândola Norte through the Serra de Grândola, a route first announced in 2006 and cancelled after opposition. The residents' association REVER says it destroys thousands of cork and holm oaks and passes near the Santo André, Sancha and Melides lagoons, and launched a petition. Grândola council considers the section unviable.

What is at stake

The Relvas Verdes–Grândola Norte section, also called Sines–Grândola Norte, is a new freight line linking the Port of Sines to the Linha do Sul along the coast, crossing the Serra de Grândola. REFER announced it in 2006 and it reappeared in the proposed National Rail Plan, in public consultation until the end of February 2023.

What you can do now

  • Monitor. When a public consultation or deadline opens, that is when participation has legal effect.
  • Share the sources, not just the headline. They are all listed above.
  • Talk to those already on the ground: REVER, Association for the Protection of the Montado against the Relvas Verdes–Grândola Norte Railway, Grândola Municipal Council, Hands off the Alentejo Coast platform (TAMLA).
  • If an infraction is happening, know who to report to before calling.
