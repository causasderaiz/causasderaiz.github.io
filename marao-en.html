Unfavourable environmental decision halts Energiekontor's plan for about 25,000 solar panels in Serra do Marão

Amarante and Baião, Serra do Marão

✓ Unfavourable environmental decision (20 Jul 2026)

Energiekontor wanted to install about 25,000 solar panels on 21 hectares next to the Penedo Ruivo wind farm in the Serra do Marão, in a Natura 2000 area. After 666 submissions in the public consultation, an unfavourable opinion from Baião Town Council and opposition from FAPAS and a petition by Visit Marão, the environmental decision was unfavourable on 20 July 2026.

What is at stake

Energiekontor's solar hybridisation project at the Penedo Ruivo wind farm planned about 25,000 panels on 21 hectares, between 1,120 and 1,220 metres above sea level, in the parishes of Teixeira e Teixeiró (Baião) and Ansiães (Amarante), in National Ecological Reserve and Natura 2000 areas. FAPAS warned of erosion and habitat loss in one of the highest areas of northern Portugal, affecting birds of prey and high-altitude vegetation.

The cliff birds. According to FAPAS, the Marão range matters for mountain and cliff birds such as the golden eagle, peregrine falcon, red-billed chough and blue rock thrush.

The mammals of the range. The plant would take 21 hectares inside the Alvão/Marão Special Area of Conservation, where red fox, badger, genet, Egyptian mongoose and small mammals depend on the scrub, rocks and patches of woodland. The 5 km of new tracks would fragment those habitats.

The soil and the water. The Marão slopes are steep and the soil erodes once the vegetation is removed. Cutting natural drainage lines increases runoff and the silting of watercourses downstream.
