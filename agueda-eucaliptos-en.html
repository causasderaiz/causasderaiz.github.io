Águeda: Quercus reports new eucalyptus plantations in burned areas and calls for permits to be revoked

Águeda municipality, Aveiro district

⚡ Quercus demands ICNF revoke the permits

Quercus is reporting new eucalyptus plantations next to homes, watercourses and roads in areas of Águeda hit by wildfires, including a plot next to the home of a victim of the September 2024 fire. The association is demanding ICNF revoke the reforestation permits, arguing they may breach the Municipal Master Plan; the Council accuses the institute of ignoring the municipality's restrictions for years.

Quercus's Aveiro regional branch is denouncing new eucalyptus plantations near homes, waterways and roads in parts of the Águeda municipality hit by the 2024 wildfires, including a plot adjoining the home of a victim of the September 2024 fire. The GNR's SEPNA nature-protection service confirmed the existence of authorisations issued by ICNF under the legal framework for afforestation and reforestation actions.

Quercus is demanding ICNF urgently revoke licences in sensitive areas, inspect existing plantations, suspend or reverse risky situations, and follow through on measures announced after the 2024 fires to convert the landscape.

Águeda's municipal council says it set clear restrictions on fast-growing forest species in sensitive areas through its Municipal Master Plan, but accuses ICNF of systematically ignoring those rules when issuing afforestation permits.
