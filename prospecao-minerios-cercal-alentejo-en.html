No. 173 ⚡ Active situation Mining and quarries

The State signs a contract to prospect for copper, lead, zinc, gold and silver over 163.9 km² around Cercal do Alentejo, in the Southwest Coast Natura 2000 area

Cercal do Alentejo and the hills of the Alentejo coast: Santiago do Cacém, Sines and Odemira

⚒ Prospecting and exploration contract in force since 10 May 2023 · DGEG case MN/PP/005/23 "Cercal"

The State signed a contract with Emisurmin for prospecting and exploration of copper, lead, zinc, gold and silver over 163.9 km² of Santiago do Cacém, Sines and Odemira, around Cercal do Alentejo. The area overlaps the Costa Sudoeste Natura 2000 site. In the 2022 public consultation, 112 of the 115 submissions were against.

What is at stake

The Juntos pelo Cercal do Alentejo movement, which also opposes the Cercal solar plant, asked in 2022 for the request to be suspended for lack of information on the works and pointed out that the area overlaps Natura 2000, the National Ecological Reserve and the Southwest Alentejo and Vicentine Coast Natural Park. In 2023, the manifesto of the Tirem as Mãos do Litoral Alentejano platform, signed in Sines by 18 groups, included the Cercal project among the prospecting threatening the coast. The same company holds another contract in force, "Santiago", in Santiago do Cacém and Grândola.

The Costa Sudoeste. Part of the area lies inside the Costa Sudoeste Special Area of Conservation (Natura 2000), and the movement also points to overlap with the National Ecological Reserve and the Southwest Alentejo and Vicentine Coast Natural Park.

The Morgavel and Campilhas waters. ZERO warned that the area covers part of the Campilhas and Morgavel water bodies; Sines Council asked for monitoring of the Morgavel system basin. Quercus asked for water testing before and after drilling.

The people of Cercal. The polygon includes Cercal do Alentejo and other villages. ZERO and Quercus asked to know where the drilling would be, and Odemira Council listed the villages inside the area among the reasons for its unfavourable opinion.
