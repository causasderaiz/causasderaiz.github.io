Pisão Dam: works suspended for the third time by court order

Crato, Portalegre district, Alto Alentejo

⚖ Works suspended by the courts since May · hearings at the Castelo Branco court on 22 and 26 October · Government extends the works to 2030

A dam costing over €220 million that would submerge the village of Pisão. Four environmental associations have contested the project's public interest in court since 2022; public water supply would account for just over 1% of the reservoir's annual volume, against 65% for agricultural use. Works began in September 2025 and have been suspended and resumed several times; the Southern Central Administrative Court confirmed a new suspension in May 2026.

What is at stake

The Pisão Dam, in the municipality of Crato (Alto Alentejo), budgeted at about 222 million euros, would submerge the village of Pisão and requires felling tens of thousands of holm and cork oaks.
