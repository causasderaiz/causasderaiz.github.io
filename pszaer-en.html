ZAER / PSZAER: over 800 renewable acceleration zones pre-approved without environmental impact assessment

National: about 170 municipalities, mainly the Algarve, Alentejo and Northeast Trás-os-Montes

◐ ZAER zones pre-approved · Quercus issued unfavourable opinion

800+ Renewable Energy Acceleration Zones (ZAER) pre-approved in 170 municipalities, exempt from individual EIA. Sectoral Programme for ZAER (PSZAER), Green Map, unfavourable Quercus opinion.

ZAER (Renewable Energy Acceleration Zones) stem from the EU's RED III directive and exempt solar and wind projects located within them from individual Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA). The exemption rests on a strategic environmental assessment carried out in bulk, for the whole zone, before any actual project exists.

The "Green Map" of candidate zones, under the Sectoral Programme for ZAER (PSZAER), was in public consultation between 17 June and 15 July 2026, with 1,302 zones mapped (792 for solar and 510 for wind), corresponding to roughly 371,000 hectares for solar and 84,000 for wind.

Quercus issued an unfavourable opinion on the last day of the consultation, calling the programme a "Black Desert": according to the association, the proposal covered around 7% of mainland territory (more than the country's entire urban footprint), when 1% would suffice to meet solar targets, with maps supplied at a scale useless for serious analysis (1:3,500,000) and without open geographic files. Plataforma Nordeste Vivo, in Northeast Trás-os-Montes, also contests the overlap of several zones with priority conservation areas.

After the consultation, the Government announced the pre-approval of more than 800 zones (500 for solar, 300 for wind) in about 170 municipalities, now saying this corresponds to roughly 1% of mainland territory, and guaranteed municipalities "full autonomy" to decide where these zones sit within their boundaries. Integration into municipal master plans (PDM) should happen between 2026 and 2028, but the exemption from individual EIA remains the core change, and it is what Causas de Raiz keeps monitoring.
