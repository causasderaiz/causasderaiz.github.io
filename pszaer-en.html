ZAER / PSZAER: over 800 renewable acceleration zones pre-approved without environmental impact assessment

National: about 170 municipalities, mainly the Algarve, Alentejo and Northeast Trás-os-Montes

◐ ZAER zones pre-approved · Quercus issued unfavourable opinion

800+ Renewable Energy Acceleration Zones (ZAER) pre-approved in 170 municipalities, exempt from individual EIA. Sectoral Programme for ZAER (PSZAER), Green Map, unfavourable Quercus opinion.

What is at stake

Renewable Energy Acceleration Zones (ZAER), under the EU RED III directive, exempt solar and wind projects within them from individual environmental impact assessment, based on a strategic environmental assessment of the whole zone carried out before any project exists.

Timeline

2026

20265 entries
  1. 20 Aug

    The Government announces the pre-approval of more than 800 zones (about 500 solar and 300 wind) in about 170 municipalities, about 1% of mainland territory, with exemption from project-specific environmental impact assessment.

    Source: SAPO ↗
  2. 17 Jul

    The Secretary of State for Energy guarantees municipalities "total autonomy" to choose the zones, up to 1% of their territory; the consultation received more than 9,000 submissions.

    Source: Green Savers ↗
  3. 15 Jul

    Quercus issues an unfavourable opinion, which it calls "Black Desert": the proposal covered about 7% of mainland territory, with maps at a scale useless for analysis.

    Source: Quercus ↗
  4. 23 Jun

    Plataforma Nordeste Vivo prepares new actions against zones overlapping conservation areas in the north-east.

    Source: Jornal Nordeste ↗
  5. 17 Jun to 15 Jul

    Public consultation on the Sectoral Programme for Renewable Energy Acceleration Zones, with 1,302 candidate zones (792 solar and 510 wind).

    Source: Ambiente Online ↗
