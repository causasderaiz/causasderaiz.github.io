No. 83 ⚡ Active situation Renewable acceleration zones

More than 800 renewable acceleration zones in about 170 municipalities were pre-approved without individual environmental impact assessment

National: about 170 municipalities, mainly the Algarve, Alentejo and Northeast Trás-os-Montes

⚡ ZAER zones pre-approved · Quercus issued unfavourable opinion

800+ Renewable Energy Acceleration Zones (ZAER) pre-approved in 170 municipalities, exempt from individual EIA. Sectoral Programme for ZAER (PSZAER), Green Map, unfavourable Quercus opinion.

What is at stake

Renewable Energy Acceleration Zones (ZAER), under the EU RED III directive, exempt solar and wind projects within them from individual environmental impact assessment, based on a strategic environmental assessment of the whole zone carried out before any project exists.

The birds and mammals. The Platform for Sustainability and Biodiversity of the Algarve and Alentejo says the zones overlap areas of Endangered or Critically Endangered species and the migration corridor of the Algarve hills. It names the cinereous vulture, Egyptian vulture, black stork, Spanish imperial eagle, great bustard, little bustard, European roller, lesser kestrel, Montagu's harrier, black-bellied sandgrouse, European wildcat, European rabbit and Iberian lynx.

The bats. The platform includes Mehely's horseshoe bat and the lesser mouse-eared bat. Quercus criticises the consultation maps, at a scale of 1:3,500,000 and with no geographic files, which make it impossible to check the zones against protected wildlife such as bats.

The montado. The zones cover montado and holm and cork oak mosaics. The platform warns of collision deaths, fragmentation and loss of habitat and barrier effects, and projects are exempt from individual environmental impact assessment.

Timeline

2026

20266 entries
  1. 20 Aug

    The Government announces the pre-approval of more than 800 zones (about 500 solar and 300 wind) in about 170 municipalities, about 1% of mainland territory, with exemption from project-specific environmental impact assessment.

    Source: SAPO ↗
  2. 17 Jul

    The Secretary of State for Energy guarantees municipalities "total autonomy" to choose the zones, up to 1% of their territory; the consultation received more than 9,000 submissions.

    Source: Green Savers ↗
  3. 15 Jul

    Quercus issues an unfavourable opinion, which it calls "Black Desert": the proposal covered about 7% of mainland territory, with maps at a scale useless for analysis.

    Source: Quercus ↗
  4. 14 Jul

    On the last day of the consultation, the Platform for Sustainability and Biodiversity of the Algarve and Alentejo opposes the programme: the zones include Iberian lynx breeding areas in the northeast Algarve and do not protect the migratory corridor of the Algarve hills. It calls for renewables on already built-up land.

    Source: Barlavento ↗
  5. 23 Jun

    Plataforma Nordeste Vivo prepares new actions against zones overlapping conservation areas in the north-east.

    Source: Jornal Nordeste ↗
  6. 17 Jun to 15 Jul

    Public consultation on the Sectoral Programme for Renewable Energy Acceleration Zones, with 1,302 candidate zones (792 solar and 510 wind).

    Source: Ambiente Online ↗

  • If an infraction is happening, know who to report to before calling.
