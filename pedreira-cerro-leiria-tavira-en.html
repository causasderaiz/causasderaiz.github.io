Cerro de Leiria quarry: limestone extraction on National Ecological Reserve land above the aquifer, in Tavira

Cerro de Leiria, Santa Catarina da Fonte do Bispo, Tavira, Faro district

✓ Victory: the court stopped the quarry in 2010

In 2009 Tavira council licensed a limestone quarry at Cerro de Leiria, on National Ecological Reserve land in a maximum infiltration zone of the Peral-Moncarapacho aquifer, after changing the master plan. At under 5 hectares, it was exempt from environmental impact assessment. Residents sought injunctions and in 2010 the Loulé Administrative and Tax Court ordered extraction to stop. PROBAAL, which had already stopped another quarry on the same site in 2001, backed the fight.

What is at stake

<p>Cerro de Leiria, in the Barrocal inland from Tavira, is National Ecological Reserve land and a maximum infiltration zone of the Peral-Moncarapacho aquifer. It was targeted by two quarries: one in 2000, stopped in 2001, and another licensed by Tavira council in 2009, run by a Tecnovia group company.</p>
