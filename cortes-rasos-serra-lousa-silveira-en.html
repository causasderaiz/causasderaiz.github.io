Serra da Lousã: clear-cutting in Natura 2000 next to the Silveira villages

Silveira and Talasnal, Lousã, Coimbra district

✓ Parliament approves prior ICNF authorisation for felling in classified areas (Mar. 2026)

In 2023 a logging company clear-cut pine woodland in the Serra da Lousã Special Conservation Zone, next to the Silveira villages, on land that Silveira Tech and Lousã council said was theirs. The Public Prosecutor closed the case in 2024, but the petition born from it led Parliament to approve, in March 2026, that felling in classified areas will need prior authorisation from ICNF.

What is at stake

Serra da Lousã is a Natura 2000 Special Conservation Zone with schist villages such as Talasnal and Silveira. To fell trees it was enough to submit a felling notice to ICNF, without proving who owns the timber.

    Parliament approves the final text of the law tightening tree-felling rules: in classified areas or under the forest regime, felling will need prior authorisation from ICNF, which has 45 days to decide.

20251 entry
  1. 3 Jul

    The petition "For the Preservation of Forests and Ecosystems in Portugal", born from the Lousã case, is debated in Parliament. It asks for an end to clear-cutting in protected areas, replanting of felled areas and that a felling notice alone should no longer be enough.

    Source: CNN Portugal ↗
20241 entry
  1. 28 Aug

    The Public Prosecutor closes the inquiry, finding no evidence of a crime by the logging company, which always said it had paid for the cutting rights. According to the ruling, Silveira Tech paid it 130,000 euros in a settlement and sought to drop its complaint.

    Source: RTP/Lusa ↗
20233 entries
  1. 19 Oct

    The C7 Coalition (ANP|WWF, FAPAS, GEOTA, LPN, Quercus, SPEA and ZERO) criticises the clear-cutting of 25 hectares on a slope in Natura 2000 and the National Ecological Reserve, pointing to legal gaps, missing management plans and weak enforcement.

    Source: LPN / C7 Coalition ↗
  2. 16 Oct

    According to Silveira Tech, after about 7 hectares were destroyed and a court summons, the company stops felling. A public petition had been launched on 10 October.

    Source: Silveira Tech ↗
  3. 27 Sep

    A logging company starts clear-cutting pine woodland near the Silveira villages, in the Serra da Lousã Special Conservation Zone, on land that Silveira Tech and Lousã council say is theirs. Two embargoes are imposed on 3 October.

    Source: Silveira Tech ↗
