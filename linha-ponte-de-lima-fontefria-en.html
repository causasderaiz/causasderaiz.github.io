No. 162 ⚡ Active situation Very high voltage power line

REN opens in July 2026 the 400 kV line across Peneda-Gerês National Park, after years of opposition from local people and five councils

Ribeira do Neiva (Vila Verde), Ponte de Lima, Ponte da Barca, Arcos de Valdevez, Monção and Melgaço, Braga and Viana do Castelo districts

Line inaugurated in Jul. 2026, after years of opposition from residents and five councils

REN built a double 400 kV line, about 68 km in Portugal, between the new Ponte de Lima substation and Galicia, crossing the Peneda-Gerês National Park. Ribeira do Neiva residents, in Vila Verde, petitioned against 75-metre pylons next to their homes, and five Alto Minho councils took the line to court. The Supreme Court sided with them on appeal, but the works went ahead and the line was inaugurated in July 2026.

What is at stake

The Ponte de Lima–Fontefria line links the Portuguese and Spanish grids: a double 400 kV line about 68 km long in Portugal, from the border at Melgaço to the new Ponte de Lima substation. It is part of an axis carrying power from Galicia to the Porto area and was classified by the European Commission as a project of common interest. On the Portuguese side it cost over 70 million euros. It crosses the Peneda-Gerês National Park, the country's only national park.

People in Ribeira do Neiva. Pylons about 75 metres tall close to homes, in a valley that already has an identical line 3 km away. Residents fear electromagnetic fields and say the line holds back the life and work of those who want to stay in the village.

Peneda-Gerês. The line crosses the Peneda-Gerês National Park, as the minister herself admitted at the inauguration. Residents objected to the felling of oaks and the effect on animals and plants.

The birds. According to the ICNF Manual (2019), very high voltage lines kill birds by collision, especially double 400 kV lines, with four planes of cables.

20243 entries
  1. 26 Sep

    The Braga court had already declared the DGEG licence ineffective (3 September). The Supreme Administrative Court admits the councils' appeal and sends the injunction back to Braga: the works could create a fait accompli and harm to health, the environment and heritage that is hard to repair.

    Source: Correio da Manhã/Lusa ↗
  2. 17 Jun

    The councils of Monção, Ponte de Lima, Ponte da Barca, Arcos de Valdevez and Melgaço have an injunction before the Braga Administrative and Fiscal Court. REN argues the line's "special urgency" and APA invokes the public interest; according to REN, the DGEG issued the licence on 26 May, before the court cases ended.

    Source: O Minho/Lusa ↗
  3. Feb

    APA issues the environmental compliance decision for the line's execution project.

    Source: Correio da Manhã/Lusa ↗
20231 entry
  1. 21 May

    Residents of Ribeira do Neiva, in Vila Verde, sign a petition to Parliament against the 400 kV line: pylons about 75 metres tall near homes, electromagnetic fields, felling of oaks and another identical line 3 km away. They ask REN for another route and APA to reassess the environmental impact.

    Source: O Minho ↗
