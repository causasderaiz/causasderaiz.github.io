Neoen data center in Alcoentre: Azambuja Council blocks PIN status request

Alcoentre, Azambuja, Lisbon district

◐ Unfavourable council opinion, PIN pending

French company Neoen requested PIN status for a €2 billion data center in Alcoentre, just metres from hundreds of homes. Azambuja Council gave an unfavourable opinion, citing missing environmental, noise and landscape impact studies: the developers submitted no noise mitigation measures or guarantees of real benefits for the municipality. Opposition councillors walked out of an extraordinary meeting on the process, accusing the executive of sharing documentation only three working days before the vote.

French company Neoen requested National Interest Project (PIN) status from AICEP for a data center valued at €2 billion, to be built in Alcoentre, a parish within Azambuja municipality.

In July 2026, Azambuja Municipal Council gave an unfavourable opinion on the request, citing missing environmental, noise and landscape impact studies. The council's opinion is not binding: the final decision on PIN status is made at the national level, by AICEP, and the process remains open.

The case illustrates a structural tension in the PIN regime: it speeds up licensing from Lisbon, even when the municipality where the project would be built objects for lack of environmental information.
