Meia Praia: citizens protest against two-hotel development in Lagos

Meia Praia, Lagos, Algarve

⚡ Citizen protest · Council admits legal limits to blocking it

About 50 people protested outside Lagos Council against the construction of two hotels and a tourist apartment block on 11,000 m² of Meia Praia, under a 1988 permit revised in 2008. The "Salvar a Meia Praia" movement is demanding a new environmental impact assessment, particularly of the lagoon basin on the site; the mayor says he cannot block the project if the developer meets the law, but confirms licensing is still conditional on the developer requalifying the basin.

Around 50 people protested outside Lagos City Council against the construction of two hotels and a block of tourist apartments on 11,000 m² of Meia Praia, under a 1988 building permit revised in 2008. The protest was organised by the civic movement "Salvar a Meia Praia" (Save Meia Praia), made up mostly of foreign residents in the area.

The movement argues the land should be turned into public green space rather than a tourist development that includes a five-star hotel. It is also calling for a new environmental impact assessment, particularly of the lagoon basin on the site.

Lagos's mayor said he was not in favour of building on the first line of coast, but acknowledged the council cannot block the project if the developer meets all applicable laws and regulations; he confirmed licensing is conditional on rehabilitating the lagoon basin.
