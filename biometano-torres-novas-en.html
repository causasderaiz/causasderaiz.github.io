Gasdaterra biomethane plant next to Árgea, Torres Novas

Árgea, Torres Novas, Médio Tejo

⚡ Consultation: 414 of 429 submissions against · Municipal Assembly unites against the project

An 18-million-euro investment to treat around 98,000 tonnes of waste a year next to a village mobilised residents, local councillors and the Municipal Assembly. Residents also object to the risk of bad odours and well contamination. The public consultation logged 429 submissions, 414 of them against the project; the council also points to planning and road-access obstacles, since the plant has no basis in the current Municipal Master Plan.

Gasdaterra plans to build a biomethane production unit in Árgea, in the municipality of Torres Novas, with an estimated investment of 18 million euros, to treat around 98,000 tonnes of waste a year and produce 6.5 million cubic metres of biomethane.

Residents, backed by the Residents Commission, are contesting the site, just a few hundred metres from the village, over heavy traffic, air emissions, the risk of bad odours and well contamination. Torres Novas Municipal Assembly united against the project, and the Residents Commission called a protest vigil.

The public consultation ran until 3 July 2026 on the Participa portal, but residents are asking for a further in-person public session and an unfavourable opinion from the Portuguese Environment Agency on the proposed site.
