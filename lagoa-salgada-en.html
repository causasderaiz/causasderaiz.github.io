No. 19 ⚡ Active petition Copper, lead and zinc mining

Local people oppose the Lagoa Salgada mine between Grândola and Alcácer do Sal

Grândola and Alcácer do Sal, Alentejo coast

⚡ Unfavourable EIA ruling (21 Jan 2026) · Redcorp went to the Beja court · Extraordinary Municipal Assembly in Grândola on 16 Nov 2026

⚡ Sign the petition

Redcorp wants to mine copper, lead and zinc between Grândola and Alcácer do Sal, over the Tejo-Sado aquifer, with a €196 million investment. The Portuguese Environment Agency (APA) has rejected the project twice, most recently on 21 January 2026, to protect water. The company filed an injunction at the Beja Administrative and Tax Court. The Grândola and Alcácer do Sal councils, the parish council and a public petition oppose the mine.

What is at stake

The project covers parts of the Grândola and Santa Margarida da Serra parish union, such as Água Derramada and Silha do Pascoal, over the Tejo-Sado aquifer, the source of public water supply and of local activities.

Water. APA rejected the project over its impacts on water resources and the sensitivity of the Tejo-Sado aquifer. In the public consultation, ZERO and the Grândola and Alcácer do Sal councils point to a falling water table and contamination linked to sulphides. The water pipeline crosses REN land, streams and protection zones of public water intakes.

Cork oak woodland. The area is mostly stone pine, cork oak and mixed cork oak and stone pine forest, with REN land. The environmental impact study itself admits the loss of cork and holm oak habitats of Community interest. ZERO counts more than a thousand cork and holm oaks destroyed; the Alcácer do Sal council speaks of thousands of cork oaks lost for good.

Rare plants and wetlands. The study rates the cork oak woodland, the pine forest and cork oak stands with scrub, the interdune hollows, the rush beds and the pond as most important, home to the greatest variety of plants and animals. The Portuguese Botanical Society warns that populations of Centaurea exarata will be harmed and that there is no data on the Lagoa Salgada itself.

The villages. The parish council warns about the safety of homes, water and air quality, the local economy and quality of life. The municipal council points to dust and the transport of hazardous substances.
