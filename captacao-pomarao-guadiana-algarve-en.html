No. 109 ⚡ Active situation Water and inter-basin transfers

Water abstraction from the Guadiana at Pomarão for the Algarve

Pomarão and Formôa, Mértola (Beja), with a pipeline through Alcoutim to the Odeleite dam, Castro Marim (Faro)

⚡ 122.23 million euro tender for the first phase launched in July 2026 · lawsuit against the Environmental Impact Statement at the Beja court

Águas do Algarve wants to abstract up to 30 cubic hectometres a year from the Guadiana estuary near Pomarão for the Odeleite dam. The Government says the water is for human consumption; ZERO, the Plataforma Água Sustentável and the Amigos da Formôa association contest the works, which they say serve intensive farming and threaten the Guadiana, and Mértola Council also opposes them.

What is at stake

Águas do Algarve wants to abstract water from the Guadiana estuary near Pomarão (Mértola) and carry it through a pipeline of about 40 kilometres to the Odeleite dam, in Castro Marim, to reinforce the Algarve's supply.

  1. 14 Jan

    In the RECAPE public consultation, the Plataforma Água Sustentável, now with 15 organisations (including Amigos da Formôa and ZERO), reaffirms its opposition: the intake rises to 30 cubic hectometres, almost double the 16 planned, and EDIA concludes there is "no water available" for the project.

    Source: Plataforma Água Sustentável ↗
20246 entries
  1. 13 Dec

    The Government launches a 109 million euro international tender and states that the water is exclusively for human consumption in the Algarve.

    Source: Governo ↗
  2. Nov

    The Amigos da Formôa association files an action at the Beja Administrative and Tax Court to annul the Environmental Impact Statement, citing risks to the Guadiana and to threatened species such as the saramugo.

    Source: ODigital ↗
  3. Oct

    At the Portuguese-Spanish summit, Portugal and Spain set a maximum of 30 cubic hectometres a year for the abstraction.

    Source: ODigital ↗
  4. 26 Sep

    Mértola Council contests the project and EDIA, the Alqueva company, warns that there may not be water available for this new abstraction.

    Source: Rádio Pax ↗
  5. 8 May

    The Plataforma Água Sustentável, with 13 associations, contests the works for allowing "the increase of intensive farming, polluting and wasteful of the scarce resource".

    Source: Ambiente Online ↗
  6. 3 May

    ZERO contests the project: increasing abstraction of a scarce resource lets the region's agriculture maintain or increase consumption, and the study downplays impacts on the Guadiana estuary.

    Source: Barlavento ↗
