Paiva Wind Farm: 45 turbines in Iberian wolf territory

Aguiar da Beira, Sátão, Sernancelhe, Vila Nova de Paiva and Viseu (Viseu and Guarda districts)

◐ 93% of submissions against · Assessment Committee gives unfavourable opinion over the Iberian wolf · APA's final decision pending

Lightsource BP wants to install 45 wind turbines (324 MW) in five municipalities in the Viseu and Guarda districts. In the public consultation, about 93% of the 590 submissions were against it, including Pinheiro residents, the "Aldeias Vivas de Aguiar da Beira" civic group and environmental associations. APA's Assessment Committee gave an unfavourable opinion, mainly over impacts on the Iberian wolf; the final decision is pending.

What is at stake

Between the Viseu and Guarda districts, in the municipalities of Aguiar da Beira, Sátão, Sernancelhe, Vila Nova de Paiva and Viseu, Lightsource BP proposes the Paiva Wind Farm: 45 wind turbines with 324 MW and power lines, to be combined with the Paiva solar plant, which already has a favourable Environmental Impact Statement. The investment is around 250 million euros. The area is used by the Iberian wolf, a species strictly protected in Portugal since 1988, and already has a high concentration of wind farms.

    The consultation closes. According to APA's report, about 93% of the 590 submissions are unfavourable, including a petition from 117 Pinheiro residents, with turbines about 750 metres from their homes, and the "Aldeias Vivas de Aguiar da Beira" civic group.

    Source: APA ↗
