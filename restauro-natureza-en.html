No. 71 ◐ Monitoring Nature restoration

National Nature Restoration Plan: submitted to Brussels on 1 September

National: public consultation via the Participa Portal

◐ Plan submitted to Brussels on 1 September · European Commission has 6 months to assess

€500 million a year and 386 measures to halt biodiversity loss across terrestrial, coastal, marine and urban ecosystems. The public consultation, from 9 July to 19 August, received 184 submissions; the Government delivered the Plan to the European Commission on 1 September, three days ahead of the deadline. Brussels now has six months to assess it, followed by a further six months for Portugal to publish the final version.

The National Nature Restoration Plan was under public consultation until August 19, 2026, through the Participa Portal. The document sets out an investment of €500 million a year until 2030, spread across 407 measures in eight domains, from rivers and wetlands to forests and marine ecosystems.

It is the national transposition of the European Nature Restoration Regulation, and the public consultation was the formal moment when anyone could propose changes to the plan before its final version, which Portugal must submit to EU authorities by September 2026.

Organizations such as ZERO and SPEA submitted technical input during the process. The plan's final version has not yet been published.
