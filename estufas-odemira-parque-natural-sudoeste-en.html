Greenhouses in the Southwest Alentejo Natural Park: intensive farming in the Mira Irrigation Perimeter

Mira Irrigation Perimeter, Odemira (Beja) and Aljezur (Faro), Southwest Alentejo and Vicentine Coast Natural Park

◐ Greenhouses remain inside the Natural Park · Government eased irrigation limits in 2025 and there is water for new areas in 2026

Inside the Southwest Alentejo and Vicentine Coast Natural Park, the Mira Irrigation Perimeter has become a hub of intensive farming in plastic greenhouses and tunnels. Since 2020 the Juntos pelo Sudoeste movement has contested the spread of greenhouses and the use of water from the Santa Clara dam, with a petition to Parliament and complaints to the courts, the Ombudsman and Brussels.

What is at stake

The Mira Irrigation Perimeter, about 12,000 hectares in Odemira and Aljezur supplied by the Santa Clara dam, lies inside the Southwest Alentejo and Vicentine Coast Natural Park. Intensive farming in plastic greenhouses and tunnels, mainly of berries, has grown there.
