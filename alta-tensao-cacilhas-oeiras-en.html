No. 161 ⚖ In court High voltage power line

A 60 kV power line diverted over the former CHEO cooperative neighbourhood in Cacilhas, Oeiras, makes way for the Vistabella development, and residents object

Cacilhas, Oeiras, Lisbon district

Injunction admitted in Oct. 2025; the pole went up anyway and the line still runs over the neighbourhood

To make way for the Vistabella luxury development in Cacilhas de Oeiras, the 60 kV high voltage line that was meant to be buried was diverted over the neighbourhood of the former CHEO cooperative, with a pole at one resident's door. Residents petitioned and filed an injunction, which the court admitted; works continued even after notification. The council says the diversion is temporary.

What is at stake

Cacilhas is a locality in Oeiras, next to Oeiras Parque, long crossed by the 60 kV overhead high voltage lines 6111 and 6112. The Vistabella subdivision by Major Development was approved in 2019 on condition that the line be buried. In 2022 the council instead authorised a temporary diversion of the line over the neighbouring estate of the former Oeiras Economic Housing Cooperative. E-Redes says the final route will be different, so burying this stretch is not justified.

People in the neighbourhood. The 60 kV line runs over the homes and a pole stands at one resident's door. Residents fear electromagnetic fields and noise and see their homes lose value.

The trees. The diversion runs next to the wooded land beside Oeiras Parque. Residents ask for the poles to go, not the trees.
