Penamacor 2 wind farm revamp authorises felling of 755 protected trees

Serra de Santo André, Meimoa and Penamacor parishes, Castelo Branco district

⚡ Government authorises felling of 755 cork and holm oaks · Petition seeks revocation

Through Order (Despacho) No. 775/2026, the Government authorised the felling of 451 cork oaks and 304 holm oaks (755 protected trees) on the Serra de Santo André, for the re-equipment of the Penamacor 2 wind farm: Lestenergia will replace seven old turbines with four more powerful ones, raising annual output from 23.48 to 88.44 GWh. The Portuguese Environment Agency waived the project from a full environmental impact assessment. A public petition demands the full revocation of the felling authorisation, an assessment of siting alternatives with less ecological impact, and stronger application of the mitigation hierarchy: avoid, minimise, and only then compensate for environmental damage.

The government authorised, through Order (Despacho) no. 775/2026 of 23 January, the felling of 451 cork oaks (383 young, 68 mature) and 304 holm oaks (284 young, 20 mature), 755 protected trees in total, across 6.76 hectares in the Serra de Santo André, in the Meimoa and Penamacor parishes, Castelo Branco district.

The felling is for the Penamacor 2 Wind Farm repowering project: developer Lestenergia will replace the seven existing turbines with four more powerful ones, nearly quadrupling annual output from 23.48 GWh to 88.44 GWh. The Portuguese Environment Agency exempted the project from its own environmental impact assessment, under the regime applicable to repowering already-licensed wind farms.

The authorisation is conditional on an environmental compensation plan requiring the company to plant trees across 12.7 hectares, almost double the affected area. A public petition is calling for the felling authorisation to be revoked entirely, for alternative, lower-impact sites to be assessed, and for the principle of avoiding and minimising harm before compensating for it to be reinforced.
