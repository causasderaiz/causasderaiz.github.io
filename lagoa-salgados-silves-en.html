Silves won't give up on the Lagoa dos Salgados Natural Reserve

Silves, Alcantarilha e Pêra, Algarve

◐ Process moving forward

After more than 20 years of campaigning, ICNF has proposed a 400-hectare Natural Reserve, but the Praia Grande Eco-Resort tourism project still threatens the wetland.

Lagoa dos Salgados, between Armação de Pêra and Albufeira, is the last stretch of the Algarve coast with no ecological protection status at all. On November 22, 2025, the Institute for Nature Conservation and Forests made public a proposal to classify a 400-hectare Natural Reserve at the site, after more than 20 years of requests from environmental organizations.

The proposal directly conflicts with the Praia Grande Eco-Resort tourism project, which plans an investment of around €200 million in three hotels, two villages, an 18-hole golf course and over two kilometres of beachfront, totalling 4,000 beds. Silves's new Municipal Master Plan already excludes the resort from the Praia Grande area, next to the lagoon.

On July 28, 2026, Silves City Council met with the State Secretary for the Environment to reiterate the need to move forward with the classification process. The Movimento Participativo Lagoa dos Salgados brings together nine organizations, Almargem, A Rocha, ANP|WWF, Vita Nativa, LPN, Quercus-ANCN, Sociedade Portuguesa de Botânica, SPEA and ZERO, in a long-running campaign through the "Save Salgados" petition, which remains active.
