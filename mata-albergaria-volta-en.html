Tour of Portugal through Mata da Albergaria: Quercus warns of "serious precedent" in Gerês

Mata da Albergaria, Peneda-Gerês National Park, Terras de Bouro

◐ Citizens' petition to Parliament · Quercus calls for the stretch to be excluded from future editions

⚡ Sign the petition →

ICNF authorised the 7th stage of the Tour of Portugal cycling race to cross Mata da Albergaria, one of the country's few well-preserved native oak woods, inside Portugal's only National Park. The authorisation imposed strict conditions (no public access, no sound amplification, support vehicles kept to a minimum), but Quercus objected anyway, warning the impacts are not eliminated and that the decision sets a "serious precedent"; the association is calling for the route to be excluded from future editions. In September, a group of citizens who use the Park launched a petition to Parliament asking for the National Park's roads to be closed to organised events, a director for the Park and visitor limits.

What is at stake

The Mata da Albergaria, in the Homem river valley, is one of the country's few well-preserved native oak forests, inside Portugal's only National Park (Peneda-Gerês). The area includes partial-protection zones and is bordered in part by total-protection zones.
