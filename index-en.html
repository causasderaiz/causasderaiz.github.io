What you can do today

How to act

01

Sign

A petition is formal pressure: above a certain number of signatures, Parliament or the local council has to respond.

02

Take part

In a public consultation, your opinion is recorded in the process and must be considered before the decision.

03

Support those on the ground

Behind every case there are associations, movements and residents' committees. The case page shows who they are and how to help.
