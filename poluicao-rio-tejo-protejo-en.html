Tagus river: pollution from discharges in the Upper Tagus

From the Upper Tagus (Vila Velha de Ródão) to Chamusca, in the Tagus Lezíria

⚡ proTEJO demands urgent testing and accountability for polluters (Jun 2026)

The proTEJO movement reported, in June 2026, a resurgence of pollution in the Tagus river between Vila Velha de Ródão and Chamusca, with abundant foam and darkened waters, a pattern the movement has documented for over a decade and attributes to industrial and urban effluent discharges along the Upper Tagus. proTEJO is demanding that the Portuguese Environment Agency and IGAMAOT carry out full water tests, conduct night-time inspections of discharges, and suspend effluent discharge licences in cases of non-compliance.

What is at stake

According to proTEJO, since at least 2015 the Tagus river has suffered successive episodes of intense pollution originating in the Upper Tagus, with abundant foam and dark water between Vila Velha de Ródão and Chamusca, which the movement attributes to industrial and urban effluent discharges.
