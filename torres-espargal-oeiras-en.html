Espargal: towers next to Parque dos Poetas, in Oeiras

Espargal neighbourhood, next to Parque dos Poetas, Oeiras, Lisbon district

Project revised after residents' struggle; construction has not started

Oeiras council wanted to approve two 19-storey towers in Espargal, a neighbourhood where buildings are around 7 storeys, next to Parque dos Poetas. The residents' struggle forced the project to be revised. 300 metres away, in Moinho das Antas, towers of 25 and 15 storeys are planned. The two projects add up to more than 500 homes and, according to residents, neither went through an Environmental Impact Assessment.

What is at stake

Espargal is a neighbourhood in Oeiras, between Parque dos Poetas and the Cascais railway line, where buildings average 7 storeys. Nearby, in Moinho das Antas, Paço de Arcos, there is an empty plot of about 12 hectares. For both sites, the council replaced the old detailed plans (from 1991 and 1996) with execution units, which allow high-rise building. The council says the towers free up land for green spaces and a green corridor; residents say they take sunlight from homes and from Parque dos Poetas, fill the streets with cars and were decided without hearing them.
