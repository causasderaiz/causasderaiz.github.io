Capwatt biomethane plant in Tougues, Vila do Conde

Tougues, Vila do Conde, Porto district

⚡ Around 200 residents protested on 28 September 2026 · Information session postponed with no date · PIN project under technical assessment

Capwatt (Sonae group) wants to build a biomethane plant in Tougues, Vila do Conde, recognised by the Government as a Project of National Interest. According to JN, the unit would stand about 20 metres from homes and from a care home with 16 elderly residents. Residents fear bad smells, noise, heavy lorries, soil contamination and falling property values; around 200 people protested outside the church on 28 September 2026, the day the information session with the company was postponed with no new date. Licensing depends on the DGEG and the APA.

Capwatt, a Sonae group company, plans to build a biomethane production unit in the parish of Tougues, in the municipality of Vila do Conde. The project has already been recognised by the Government as a Project of National Interest (PIN) and is at the stage of technical assessments and studies.

The application reached Vila do Conde City Council in December 2025 and, in May 2026, the council issued a favourable decision on the Prior Information Request. The council told the Lusa news agency that this decision concerns only the planning viability of the land and not the licensing of the activity, which depends on opinions and authorisations from the Directorate-General for Energy and Geology (DGEG) and the Portuguese Environment Agency (APA); according to JN, as cited by Viva Porto, also from the Northern Regional Coordination Commission (CCDR Norte).

According to Jornal de Notícias, as cited by Viva Porto, the unit would stand about 20 metres from some homes and from a care home where 16 elderly people live, some with respiratory problems. Residents fear bad smells, gases, flies, noise, heavy lorry traffic, soil contamination and falling property values, and point to National Agricultural Reserve land nearby. Many say they do not oppose biomethane production as such, but its location so close to homes.

A public information session with the company, organised by Tougues Parish Council, was scheduled for 28 September 2026 and was postponed with no new date; the parish council cited a lack of logistical conditions, and its president told JN that it was the company that decided to postpone. That same evening, around two hundred residents gathered outside Tougues Church in protest, and they are considering further actions at the City Council and at Sonae.
