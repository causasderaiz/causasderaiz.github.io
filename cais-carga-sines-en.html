New General Cargo Dock at the Port of Sines: military use without public debate

Port of Sines, Alentejo coast

◐ Public consultation closed on 9 September · awaiting Environmental Impact Statement

The Environmental Impact Study for the new General Cargo Dock at the Port of Sines, a €90 million investment, allows for "dual" civil and military use of the infrastructure. Iniciativa Cidadã, a Sines residents' group led by Eugénia Santa Bárbara and Bruno Candeias, said there had been no public information on the issue and organised a public meeting on 4 September; the Left Bloc questioned the government about a possible link to NATO's logistics network. The Administration of the Ports of Sines and the Algarve confirms "dual-use requirements" but denies a permanent military installation. The public consultation closed on 9 September, with the Portuguese Environment Agency yet to publish its Environmental Impact Statement.

The Environmental Impact Study for the new General Cargo Dock at the Port of Sines, a €90 million investment promoted by the Administration of the Ports of Sines and the Algarve, sets out "dual" civil and military use requirements for the infrastructure.

The Left Bloc questioned the government about the terms, scale and timeline of any military use, and whether the new dock is part of a wider strategy linking it to NATO's logistics network. The port authority denies a permanent military installation.

Iniciativa Cidadã, a Sines residents' group led by Eugénia Santa Bárbara and Bruno Candeias, organised a public meeting on 4 September 2026 at the Sines Fire Station, arguing there had not been enough public information before the Environmental Impact Assessment's public consultation.
