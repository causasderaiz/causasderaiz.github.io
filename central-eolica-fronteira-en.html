No. 55 ⚡ Active situation Large-scale wind farm

Engie plans 35 wind turbines in an area that overlaps the Douro Internacional Natural Park, in Avelanoso and Vimioso

Avelanoso and Vimioso, hybridised with the Picote Hydroelectric Plant, Bragança district

⚡ Public consultation closed on 17 August with 233 submissions

35 turbines and 157.5 MW of capacity, in a 5,893-hectare study area overlapping the Douro Internacional Natural Park and Natura 2000 sites. The public consultation closed on 17 August with 233 submissions recorded. The Movimento Cultural da Terra de Miranda opposes the Engie project and says that "the Land of Miranda is not for sale".

What is at stake

The birds of the Douro Internacional. The study area reaches the Douro Internacional and Águeda Valley Special Protection Area. The developer's document lists birds threatened in mainland Portugal (Red List of Birds, 2022) that may use the area, such as the black stork, Egyptian vulture, cinereous vulture, golden eagle, Bonelli's eagle, little bustard and red-billed chough.

The Avelanoso and Cirouco packs. The wind farm area overlaps the protection zones of two Iberian wolf packs, a threatened species: Avelanoso and Cirouco, both confirmed and probably breeding.

The bats. The wind farm touches the protection area of a bat roost of regional importance, and the power line corridors cross a roost of national importance. ICNF requires searches for dead bats around every turbine.

The plants. The area may hold the monkshood Aconitum napellus subsp. lusitanicum, Critically Endangered on the Red List of the Vascular Flora of mainland Portugal (2020).
