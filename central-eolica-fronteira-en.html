Fronteira Wind Farm: 35 turbines next to the Douro Internacional Natural Park

Avelanoso and Vimioso, hybridised with the Picote Hydroelectric Plant, Bragança district

◐ Public consultation closed on 17 August with 233 submissions

35 turbines and 157.5 MW of capacity, in a 5,893-hectare study area overlapping the Douro Internacional Natural Park and Natura 2000 sites. The public consultation closed on 17 August with 233 submissions recorded.

The Fronteira Wind Farm plans to install 35 wind turbines, with 157.5 MW of installed capacity, in a 5,893-hectare study area across the municipalities of Vimioso and Miranda do Douro. The project is designed as a hybrid with the Picote Hydroelectric Plant, sharing its grid connection point.

The study area overlaps the Douro Internacional Natural Park and three Natura 2000 Special Conservation Areas. The proposal that was in consultation defined the scope of the future environmental impact study, in other words, what will (or will not) be assessed in depth before licensing.

Miranda do Douro Municipal Council made the consultation public; it ran on the Participa portal until August 17, 2026. The final scope of the environmental impact study follows next.
