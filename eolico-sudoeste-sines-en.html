Southwest Wind Farm in Santiago do Cacém: Quercus warns of underassessed impacts

Santiago do Cacém and Cercal do Alentejo, Alentejo coast

◐ Public consultation on the assessment scope closed on 18 August

Up to 31 turbines and a lithium battery park across 1,098 hectares, to power the Sines Data Center that could eventually use 20% of the country's electricity. The public consultation on the scope of the environmental impact study closed on August 18; the full environmental impact study now follows.

Hyperion Renewables Sines wants to build the Southwest Wind Farm, split into three clusters, São Bartolomeu, Vale Miguel and Monte das Oliveiras, between Santiago do Cacém and Cercal do Alentejo. The project envisages 23 to 31 turbines over 100 metres tall, 138 MW of total capacity, an estimated annual output of 386.4 GWh, and a storage system of 61 lithium battery containers with 200 MWh of capacity, across a 1,098.4-hectare study area. The energy is intended almost exclusively to power the Start Campus Data Center in Sines, which at full capacity could consume up to 20% of the electricity used in Portugal.

Quercus warns that the document under public consultation systematically classifies impacts as "of little significance" despite affecting classified habitats, such as cork and holm oak groves, dry European heathlands and watercourses, legally protected cork and holm oaks, bird and bat mortality from collisions, and disturbance to nationally protected agricultural and ecological reserve areas. The association also found, on page 96 of the document, a reference to energy production "from the sun": a sign, it says, that the study was hastily copy-pasted from another process, symptomatic of assembly-line environmental impact studies with little rigour.

The citizen movement RENOVAR Santiago do Cacém also opposes the project, criticising that the public consultation is running during the summer holiday period with little involvement from affected communities. The movement notes the municipality already hosts two other large contested solar projects: about 1,260 hectares between São Domingos and Vale de Água, and about 394 hectares in Cercal do Alentejo. It is calling for a cumulative impact study covering all the energy megaprojects planned for the region. The public consultation on the proposed scope of the environmental impact study was open on the Participa portal until 18 August 2026.
