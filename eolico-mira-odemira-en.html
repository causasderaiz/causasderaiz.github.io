No. 36 ⚡ Active petition Wind and solar plant

Galp plans 22 wind turbines and to hybridise a solar park between Odemira and Ourique

Odemira and Ourique, Beja district

⚡ The APA found the scoping proposal insufficient (23 Oct 2026) · 476 submissions in the consultation

⚡ Sign the petition

Galp is moving forward with 22 wind turbines (158 MW) and the hybridisation of a solar plant between Odemira and Ourique (18 of them in Odemira, affecting Relíquias, São Martinho das Amoreiras, Luzianes-Gare and Santa Clara-a-Velha), after its earlier Cachenas project was stopped by strong local opposition. The Serra da Vigia Neighbours Group, in São Martinho das Amoreiras, denounces in its petition constant aerodynamic noise and shadow flicker from the blades near homes, on top of falling property values and a heightened wildfire risk from new access roads and high-voltage lines; the petition remains active, and Odemira Municipal Council is demanding public discussions in the affected parishes before anything moves forward. The public consultation on the scoping of the Environmental Impact Study ran from 4 to 24 September 2026 and received 476 submissions; Quercus issued an unfavourable opinion on the project. The review of Odemira's master plan, which the group also contests, is open for public comment until 23 October.

What is at stake

Galp proposes the "Mira Wind Project", a hybrid wind-solar park with about 22 turbines and about 158 megawatts between Odemira and Ourique, near the parishes of Relíquias, São Martinho das Amoreiras, Luzianes-Gare and Santa Clara-a-Velha.

Cork oak woodland. Opening roads and platforms means felling cork and holm oaks, both protected species. The project cuts straight through the Cintura de Ourique ecological corridor defined in the Alentejo Regional Forest Plan.

The birds. Quercus calls for attention to raptors and steppe birds, given the nearby Castro Verde SPA (5.1 km); the Piçarras SPA is 8.6 km away and the Costa Sudoeste SAC 5.1 km. The proposal has no wildlife inventory and no modelling of migration routes. SPEA points out that soaring birds, especially raptors, are among those most often killed by collisions with wind turbines and power lines. The ICNF asks for the turbines to be kept at least 3 km from Bonelli's eagle breeding territories.

The bats. Quercus calls for bat monitoring. ICNF requires searches for dead bats around every turbine and pays special attention to species that fly above the tree canopy, such as Pipistrellus, Savi's pipistrelle, the serotine and the noctules (Nyctalus).

The headwaters of the Mira and the Sado. Streams of the Mira and Sado basins rise in São Martinho das Amoreiras, Luzianes-Gare and Santa Clara-a-Velha, on soils of maximum infiltration. Earthworks and roads on steep slopes increase erosion and the risk of desertification.

The people of Serra da Vigia. The Serra da Vigia Neighbours Group denounces constant noise and the strobe effect of the blades near homes, falling house values and greater fire risk from new roads and high-voltage lines.

The extra-high-voltage line. The 24.5 km of 150 kV line pass close to villages. Odemira Council asks for at least 200 metres between the lines and homes and says the route is incompatible with São Martinho das Amoreiras' urban expansion zone and with land set aside for municipal housing. Ourique Council proposes burying the lines. In the consultation, the line worried above all residents and landowners near the corridor.

Timeline

2026

20268 entries
  1. 23 Oct

    The APA decides that the scoping proposal lacks the necessary elements: the environmental impact study will have to compare alternative routes for the 150 kV line, the zero option and cumulative impacts. The ICNF asks for 3 km of distance from Bonelli's eagle territories.

    Source: APA ↗
  2. 25 Sep

    Quercus issues an unfavourable opinion: impacts on birds and sensitive habitats are underestimated, and the turbines, up to 200 metres tall, would stand about 5 km from protected areas.

    Source: Quercus, 25 Sep 2026 ↗
  3. 4 to 24 Sep

    Public consultation on the scoping proposal for the environmental impact study, with 476 submissions.

    Source: Participa (Portuguese Environment Agency) ↗
  4. Sep

    The Serra da Vigia Neighbours Group, in São Martinho das Amoreiras, organises against two threats: Galp's Mira Wind Farm and the changes proposed in the review of Odemira's master plan. The petition against the project is the group's.

    Source: BRAVA, Rural Resistance Network ↗
  5. 30 Jul

    Public comment opens on the review of Odemira's Municipal Master Plan, until 23 October 2026.

    Source: Barlavento ↗
  6. 22 Jul

    Galp studies a new project, the "Mira Wind Project", with about 22 turbines and a solar component between Odemira and Ourique.

    Source: ECO ↗
  7. 27 May

    Odemira Council announces that Galp has dropped the Cachenas wind farm.

    Source: Local council position (Odemira, Ourique) · Rádio Pax ↗

    Galp's previous project in the area, the Cachenas wind farm (19 turbines between Vila Nova de Milfontes and Porto Covo), is under public consultation; a petition calls for its suspension.
