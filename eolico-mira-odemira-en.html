Stop Galp's Mira Wind Project in Odemira

Odemira and Ourique, Beja district

⚡ Environmental impact assessment underway

Galp is moving forward with 22 wind turbines (158 MW) and the hybridisation of a solar plant between Odemira and Ourique (18 of them in Odemira, affecting Relíquias, São Martinho das Amoreiras, Luzianes-Gare and Santa Clara-a-Velha), after its earlier Cachenas project was stopped by strong local opposition. Residents' petition denounces constant aerodynamic noise and shadow flicker from the blades near homes, on top of falling property values and a heightened wildfire risk from new access roads and high-voltage lines; the petition remains active, and Odemira Municipal Council is demanding public discussions in the affected parishes before anything moves forward.

Galp submitted to the Portuguese Environment Agency a scope-definition proposal for the "Mira Wind Project", a hybrid wind-solar plant with around 22 turbines and about 158 megawatts of capacity, spanning the municipalities of Odemira and Ourique.

Residents of the parishes of Relíquias, São Martinho das Amoreiras, Luzianes-Gare and Santa Clara-a-Velha express total rejection of the project. This is already Galp's second project in the region, after the Cachenas wind farm, next to its green hydrogen unit in Sines, was abandoned in 2026 following an unfavourable opinion and a petition with over 5,595 signatures.

The current petition asks Odemira City Council and the competent authorities to stop the new project before it advances to the next stage of environmental licensing.
