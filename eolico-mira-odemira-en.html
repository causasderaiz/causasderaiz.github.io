Stop Galp's Mira Wind Project in Odemira

Odemira and Ourique, Beja district

◐ The scoping consultation for the environmental impact study closed with 476 submissions · Odemira's master plan review open for public comment until 23 October

⚡ Sign the petition →

Galp is moving forward with 22 wind turbines (158 MW) and the hybridisation of a solar plant between Odemira and Ourique (18 of them in Odemira, affecting Relíquias, São Martinho das Amoreiras, Luzianes-Gare and Santa Clara-a-Velha), after its earlier Cachenas project was stopped by strong local opposition. The Serra da Vigia Neighbours Group, in São Martinho das Amoreiras, denounces in its petition constant aerodynamic noise and shadow flicker from the blades near homes, on top of falling property values and a heightened wildfire risk from new access roads and high-voltage lines; the petition remains active, and Odemira Municipal Council is demanding public discussions in the affected parishes before anything moves forward. The public consultation on the scoping of the Environmental Impact Study ran from 4 to 24 September 2026 and received 476 submissions; Quercus issued an unfavourable opinion on the project. The review of Odemira's master plan, which the group also contests, is open for public comment until 23 October.

What is at stake

Galp proposes the "Mira Wind Project", a hybrid wind-solar park with about 22 turbines and about 158 megawatts between Odemira and Ourique, near the parishes of Relíquias, São Martinho das Amoreiras, Luzianes-Gare and Santa Clara-a-Velha.
