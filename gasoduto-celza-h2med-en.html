CelZa gas pipeline: a new pipeline from Celorico da Beira to Zamora, across the rivers of the Northeast

Celorico da Beira to the border, towards Zamora (Spain); and Figueira da Foz–Cantanhede

⏳ At the environmental studies stage; public consultation on the environmental impact study is expected in Q4 2026

REN plans to build CelZa, a pipeline of around 270 km between Celorico da Beira and Zamora, around 185 km of it in Portugal, as part of the European H2MED project, plus a new pipeline between Figueira da Foz and Cantanhede. REN says it is dedicated to green hydrogen. According to Linha Vermelha, the route may cross the Mondego, Douro, Maçãs and Sabor rivers and Montesinho Natural Park, and the pipeline may be used to carry fossil gas.

What is at stake

Linha Vermelha is mapping the route and informing the people who live along the pipeline routes.
