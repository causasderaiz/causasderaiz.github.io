Revive: Forte Velho do Outão becomes a 50-year tourism concession inside the Natural Park

Forte Velho do Outão / 7th Outão Battery, Setúbal, Serra da Arrábida

◐ Council approved conversion into hotel (Jan. 2025)

Under the Revive programme, the Portuguese state granted a 50-year concession over the Forte Velho do Outão, a former coastal defence battery inside the Arrábida Natural Park, next to the Secil cement plant. The concession was awarded in 2023 to the catering company Real Bolhão, first for a restaurant; in January 2025 Setúbal City Council approved converting the project instead into a 5-star hotel with around 35 rooms. There is no public confirmation of works having started since then.

Under the Revive programme, which grants private companies concessions to run publicly-owned properties of historical value, the Forte Velho do Outão (also known as the 7th Outão Battery), a former coastal defence battery inside the Arrábida Natural Park, next to the Secil quarries, was granted for 50 years.

The contract was signed on 25 August 2023 with Real Bolhão, Restaurantes, Unipessoal, Lda., with a minimum annual rent of €130,987.32 and a construction area of 6,909 m². Press reports put the actual contracted rent at close to €201,000 a year. The 2022 tender allowed for a hotel, local accommodation or another tourism project; the concession was initially announced for a restaurant. In February 2025, Setúbal City Council approved a reconversion of the project into a 5-star hotel with around 35 rooms.

According to the official Revive page, operations are expected to start by September 2027. There is no public confirmation of works having started on site.
