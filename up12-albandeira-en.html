No. 92 ⚖ In court Coastal tourism development

UP12: a 1,030-bed resort threatens Albandeira's last wild coastline

Praia de Albandeira, Caramujeira, Porches, Lagoa municipality, Algarve

⚡ STOP UP12's court injunction has blocked the Albandeira detailed plan since 23 January 2026

Donate: Movimento STOP UP12 →

The civic group STOP UP12 has fought since 2023 against the UP12 tourism development, which plans 1,030 beds in hotels, aparthotels and villas on the last stretch of wild coastline between Praia da Marinha and Nossa Senhora da Rocha, in Lagoa. The detailed plan for ATE A1 was rejected by the APA, ICNF, CCDR-Algarve and DGT over serious flaws in the environmental assessment, but Lagoa Council reopened a new process to revive it in October 2025, even though the mayor himself admitted that approving this development back in 2008 "was a mistake".

What is at stake

Planning Unit 12 (UP12) provides for hotels, aparthotels and tourist villas with 1,030 beds on the coastal strip between Praia da Marinha and Nossa Senhora da Rocha, in Lagoa, next to the Pedra do Valado Marine Protected Area. The ATE A1 detailed plan received unfavourable opinions from the Environment Agency, ICNF, CCDR-Algarve and DGT.
