"Alqueva Cluster": Chinese group Chint seeks to license 1,300 MW of solar, wind and batteries in Portel and Vidigueira

Portel and Vidigueira municipalities, Évora district

◐ Environmental assessment process under way · around 2.17 million solar panels planned

The Chinese group Chint began the environmental assessment process for the "Alqueva Cluster", combining three battery-backed solar plants (São Gião, Tapada Branca and Monte Santos) and two wind plants in Portel and Vidigueira municipalities, totalling around 1,300 MW of installed capacity and about 2.17 million photovoltaic panels.

The Chinese group Chint Solar began the environmental impact assessment process for the "Alqueva Cluster", a set of three battery-backed solar plants, São Gião (563 MW), Tapada Branca (580.5 MW) and Monte Santos (111 MW), combined with around 50 MW of wind power, in Portel and Vidigueira municipalities, Évora district. In total, the cluster foresees around 1,300 MW of installed capacity, battery storage systems with a combined capacity of 895 MW, and an estimated average annual output of 1,867.9 GWh.

The project involves installing around 2.17 million photovoltaic solar panels spread across the two municipalities, in the Alqueva area. The investment has been in development since 2019, but only moved to the formal environmental assessment stage in 2026; the total investment value is not yet publicly known.

Causas de Raiz is flagging this project as one to monitor because of its scale: it is one of the largest renewable energy clusters ever proposed in the Alqueva region, with potentially significant cumulative impact on the territory, alongside other solar and wind projects already under way in the same municipalities (such as the Sobreira de Baixo photovoltaic plant).
