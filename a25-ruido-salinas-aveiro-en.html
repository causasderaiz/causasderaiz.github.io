No. 180 ⚡ Active situation Motorway beside the Ria

Aveiro Cidade–Ria raises the alarm over the noise, exhaust fumes and runoff of the A25 motorway beside the Ria de Aveiro salt pans

Foz de Prata and São Roque canal, Aveiro, Aveiro district

⚡ Requests to APA, ICNF, Ascendi and the Environment Minister unanswered · Complaint to the European Commission

The A25 runs through Aveiro between the Ria salt pans, a Natura 2000 special protection area for birds since 1999, and the neighbourhood by the São Roque canal and Foz de Prata. In November 2026, Anna Kuzmina of Aveiro Cidade–Ria described constant noise, the smell of exhaust fumes and the lack of tall noise barriers along the stretches she walked, and wrote to APA, the Portuguese Environment Agency, to ICNF, the body that manages protected areas, to Ascendi, the motorway concession holder, and to the Environment Minister. She asks for measurements of noise, air and road runoff, and for measures to reduce them; so far she has had no reply.

What is at stake

In 1989, a letter to the Diário de Aveiro was already warning about the IP5, the road that became the A25, beside the salt pans. The Ria de Aveiro became a Natura 2000 special protection area for birds in 1999. The A25 noise barriers come from the 2012 and 2016 noise action plans.

Salt pan birds. The salt pans beside the A25 are part of the Ria de Aveiro special protection area. Aveiro Cidade–Ria photographed flamingos flying by the motorway and small waders in the salt pans. She asks for a study of flight paths, feeding and disturbance from noise and light.

The Ria's water. Rain washes oil, metals and tyre residue off the road. According to Aveiro Cidade–Ria, it is not known where this stretch's drainage discharges or whether the water is treated before reaching the salt pans and canals.

People living and walking by the canal. The 2016 noise action plan found homes along the A25 exposed to noise above the legal limit. Aveiro Cidade–Ria describes constant noise and exhaust smell by the canal and Foz de Prata, and saw no tall noise barriers along the path.
