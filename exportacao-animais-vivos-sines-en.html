No. 160 ⚡ Active situation Live animal exports

About 400,000 live cattle and sheep leave the ports of Sines and Setúbal each year on old ships, and a petition calls for ending this trade by sea

Port of Sines, Setúbal district (shipments also from the port of Setúbal)

⚡ PATAV petition to abolish live animal exports by sea heard in Parliament on 24 February 2026

According to PATAV, around 400,000 live cattle and sheep leave the ports of Sines and Setúbal every year on old ships, bound for countries outside the European Union, mainly in the Middle East and North Africa. Voyages last 10 days on average. The Platform Against the Transport of Live Animals protests in Sines and took a petition to abolish these exports to Parliament, which heard it on 24 February 2026.

What is at stake

Portugal exports live livestock by sea to countries outside the European Union, mainly in the Middle East and North Africa, from the ports of Sines and Setúbal. At both ports, shipments are inspected by staff of the Directorate-General for Food and Veterinary Affairs (DGAV). According to PATAV, around 400,000 animals leave every year, on voyages lasting 10 days on average, on ships around 40 years old converted from other uses.

The animals. According to PATAV, voyages last 10 days on average and there is no vet on board. The animals travel with little space and suffer injuries, stress and dehydration; on arrival many have fractures and blindness caused by ammonia. The platform reports overcrowding, electric prods and poor sanitary conditions during loading. In October 2021 the Phoenix III left Sines with 1,200 young bulls and 5,644 sheep for Haifa, Israel, was stuck for two days off Italy and arrived with 14 animals dead.

People living by the ports. In Setúbal, residents complain of bad smells during loading. According to PATAV, dead animals have washed ashore in several places around the country.

The sea and the air. According to PATAV, the ships are generally around 40 years old, converted from other uses and highly polluting. The platform also points to the methane emissions linked to these exports.

    The Agriculture and Fisheries Committee hears PATAV on petition no. 44/XVII. The platform calls for a moratorium and then an end to live animal exports by sea, with slaughter close to where animals are raised and exports of meat instead.

  1. 14 Jun

    On the International Day of Awareness against the Transport of Live Animals, PATAV protests in Sines and calls for an end to exports through the ports of Sines and Setúbal. In February the platform launched a petition to Parliament to abolish these exports.

    Source: Agroportal (Lusa) ↗
  2. 28 May

    Live animals are being loaded at the port of Sines. According to PATAV, 430,662 sheep and 188,809 cattle left through Portuguese ports in 2022.

    Source: Agroportal (Lusa) ↗
