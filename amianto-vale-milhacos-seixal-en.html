Asbestos at the Vale de Milhaços primary school, Seixal

Vale de Milhaços, Corroios parish, Seixal municipality

⚡ Asbestos still at the main school; staff protested at Seixal City Council (22 Apr 2026) and outside the Ministry of Education (May 2026)

Over a hundred education professionals from the Vale de Milhaços school group in Seixal protested in March 2026, closing more than half of the group's 10 schools, demanding removal of the asbestos plates still present at the main school and more support staff. This was followed by a protest outside Seixal City Council on 22 April and a rally outside the Ministry of Education, in Lisbon, in May 2026.

What is at stake

Vale de Milhaços Primary and Middle School, head of a cluster of 10 schools and kindergartens in Corroios, Seixal, still has asbestos-cement panels on the roofs of some areas where pupils and staff spend long periods.
