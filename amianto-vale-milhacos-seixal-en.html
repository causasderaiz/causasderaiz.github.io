Asbestos at the Vale de Milhaços primary school, Seixal

Vale de Milhaços, Corroios parish, Seixal municipality

⚡ Union gave the Ministry of Education until 13 April 2026 to respond; protest at Seixal City Council planned for 22 April

Over a hundred education professionals from the Vale de Milhaços school group in Seixal protested in March 2026, closing more than half of the group's 10 schools, demanding removal of the asbestos plates still present at the main school and more support staff.

The Vale de Milhaços primary school, the main site of the Vale de Milhaços school group (10 establishments, including primary schools and kindergartens) in the Corroios parish, Seixal municipality, still has asbestos fibre panels in the roofing of some of its spaces. Asbestos use has been banned in the European Union, with mandatory removal required since 2005, but the material remains in place, with students and staff spending long periods of time in those corridors.

On 12 March 2026, over a hundred education professionals from the school group gathered in protest and a plenary meeting at the main school, leading to the closure of more than half of the municipality's 10 schools that morning. According to Daniel Martins, of the S.TO.P. union's board, the protest's primary aim was the non-removal of the asbestos roofing, requested for years, but it also raised complaints about a shortage of support staff and precarious contracts for specialised technicians.

On 20 March 2026, following the plenary meeting, S.TO.P. sent a formal letter to the Ministry of Education, Science and Innovation and to Seixal City Council, detailing the school's infrastructure problems (asbestos, no sports hall, leaks, damaged furniture, black mould in classrooms and corridors) and demanding a response with dates and deadlines for resolution by 13 April 2026, the first day of the third term. The letter also requests meetings with both entities by 22 April 2026, warning that if the responses are unsatisfactory, education professionals unanimously decided to protest outside Seixal City Council on that same date.
