Abrunheira landfill, Mafra: extension to 2030

Abrunheira, São Miguel de Alcainça, Mafra

⚡ Mafra Municipal Assembly rejected the landfill extension to 2030 on 24 September 2026 · Residents complain of foul smells

The Abrunheira landfill, run by Tratolixo, the inter-municipal company of Cascais, Mafra, Oeiras and Sintra, passed 91% of its capacity in April 2026 and was due to close in 2027. The proposal to recognise the public interest of extending it by three years, to 2030, with one million more tonnes of capacity, was rejected by Mafra Municipal Assembly on 24 September 2026. Residents of São Miguel de Alcainça delivered a petition against the extension and complain of foul smells.

The Abrunheira landfill, at the Abrunheira Ecopark in the parish of São Miguel de Alcainça, Mafra, takes the waste of the municipalities of Cascais, Mafra, Oeiras and Sintra and is run by Tratolixo, an inter-municipal company owned by those four municipalities. According to Diário de Notícias, the landfill passed 91% of its capacity in April 2026 and was due to close in 2027.

On 18 August 2026, Tratolixo asked Mafra City Council to recognise the public interest of extending the landfill by three years, which would add one million tonnes of capacity until 2030. The council approved the request on 21 August, with the three PSD councillors voting against and stating that the landfill "is harmful to the interests of the populations of the Municipality of Mafra".

On 24 September 2026, Mafra Municipal Assembly rejected the proposal, with 23 votes against and 6 abstentions. At the session, residents delivered a petition against the extension; according to Lusa, the petitioners' representative, Jorge Silva, said it was unacceptable to extend operation to 2030 given the effects the population feels every day, and resident Carla Inverno described a "nauseating smell" that makes it impossible to air homes, especially on hot days. The president of the Malveira and São Miguel de Alcainça Parish Union, Carla Galrão, asked for studies justifying keeping the landfill open and for a new waste disposal site to be identified.

The mayor of Mafra, Hugo Moreira Luís, defended the proposal, recalling that Tratolixo had already decided in 2022 to extend the landfill's life for lack of an alternative. According to Diário de Notícias, an independent study identified nine possible sites for a new landfill, five in the municipality of Sintra and four in Mafra, and the mayor will ask Tratolixo to hold an information session for the population.
