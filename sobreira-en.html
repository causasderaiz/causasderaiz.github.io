Sobreira de Baixo Photovoltaic Plant (EDP)

Pedrógão, Vidigueira, with marginal effect on Portel, Évora district

◐ Project reformulated after assessment found very significant negative impacts · new public consultation

EDP's largest solar plant in Europe, with "very significant" impacts on Bonelli's eagle and protected bats; the developer reformulated the project, reducing its footprint, and resubmitted it for public consultation.

EDP is preparing, in Pedrógão, in the municipality of Vidigueira, its largest photovoltaic plant in Europe, with construction planned to start in November 2026.

The project's environmental impact study classifies the impacts on Bonelli's eagle, a threatened species with confirmed presence in the region, and on several protected bat species, as "very significant".

Environmental organizations are asking for the project layout to be revised to preserve flight corridors and hunting areas essential to these species' survival in the region.

September 2026 update: the project, promoted by Empresa Hidroelétrica do Guadiana (EHG) in partnership with EDP Renováveis, was planned with an installed capacity of 292.6 MVA and estimated annual output of 418 GWh, with marginal effect on Portel municipality. After an initial public consultation, the Portuguese Environment Agency and the Assessment Commission confirmed, in an opinion, very significant negative impacts on Bonelli's eagle and on bats associated with the nationally important Alqueva shelter. In response, the developer reformulated the project under article 16 of Decree-Law 151-B/2013, reducing the direct footprint to 96 hectares, within an 836-hectare property. The reformulated version, dated September 2025, was resubmitted for a new public consultation in 2026.
