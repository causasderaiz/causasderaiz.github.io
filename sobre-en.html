About

Connecting those defending the land with those who want to act

Portugal is full of people and communities defending the land, the water, the forests, biodiversity and the right to decide about their own territory. We map those struggles in Portugal, Madeira and the Azores, verify the information and bring together in one place the communities, organisations, sources and ways to take part.

What this map does

  • Brings onto one screen what is scattered across posts that vanish in two days.
  • Shows where it is, who is defending it, what has been verified and what closes first.
  • Gives each person a concrete way to act: sign, take part in a consultation, report to the right body, join an association.
  • It also makes visible the projects, practices and communities building alternatives and bringing land back to life.

No struggle is won alone.

Who makes it

Open to anyone

Helping

Verification, photography, translation, contact with local movements. If you want to lend a hand, write.

Independence

No party affiliation. No funding from companies with interests in the projects documented. No advertising and no third-party trackers. The method is explained in Methodology.
