About

Connecting those who defend the land with those who want to act

Across Portugal, communities are defending a river, a mountain range, a beach, a cork oak woodland. Almost always they do it alone, with few resources, and on social media their struggle lasts as long as a single post. Causas de Raiz exists so that these struggles are no longer scattered. It brings them together on a single map of mainland Portugal, Madeira and the Azores, verifies each case and shows who is on the ground and how you can help.

What this map does

  • Gives place and context to what is scattered: each conflict appears where it happens, next to the others, and patterns become visible.
  • Tells you the essentials of each case: what is at stake, where, who is resisting, where things stand and what closes first.
  • Turns concern into action: sign a petition, take part in a public consultation, report to the right authority, join those already fighting.
  • Also shows those building alternatives and regenerating the land, because defending and restoring are the same work.

No struggle is won alone.

Independence

We have no party affiliation. We do not accept funding from companies with interests in the projects we document. There is no advertising. Every case cross-checks identified press coverage, the voices of the movements themselves and official public information; the full method is in Methodology.

Get in touch

Know of a struggle that is not on the map yet? Found a mistake? Want to help with verification, photography or translation? Write to geral@causasderaiz.org.
