Map of environmental conflicts in Portugal

Causas de Raiz is an independent, non-profit citizen initiative that maps environmental conflicts across mainland Portugal, Madeira and the Azores. By placing each struggle on the map, it turns dozens of isolated resistances, often invisible beyond their own municipality, into a picture of the whole country: where conflicts repeat, which patterns cut across different regions, and how a seemingly local problem turns out to be systemic and national in scale.

What is available at causasderaiz.org

  • 131 documented cases in mainland Portugal, Madeira and the Azores (updated October 17).
  • Constant updates: new cases and new developments added continuously.
  • Thematic layers and geolocation: every case is placed on the map and can be filtered by theme, such as data centres, wind farms, mining, environmental racism or dams, and by status: open petition, ongoing public consultation or court case.
  • History of each case: every conflict has its own page, with a timeline, actions and the people and groups involved.
  • Sources always cited: each case cross-checks identified press sources with the movements' own actions and statements and with official public information (see Methodology).
  • Action guide for anyone who wants to get involved: sign petitions, take part in public consultations or support local groups.
  • Competent authority finder, which helps anyone identify the right body to report an environmental offence to (SEPNA, ICNF, APA, among others).

"Every case we document is a community resisting on its own, often without institutional support and without visibility beyond its municipality. This map gives it memory, context and a link to those who want to help."

Sílvia Jorge, founder of Causas de Raiz
