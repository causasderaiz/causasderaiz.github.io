Mira Wind Project: public consultation on 22 turbines between Odemira and Ourique closes 24 September

Luzianes-Gare, São Martinho das Amoreiras and Santa Clara-a-Velha parishes (Odemira) and Santana da Serra (Ourique), Beja district

🔴 Public consultation on the environmental scope closes on 24 September 2026

Galp is studying the installation of around 22 wind turbines, with an estimated 158 MW capacity, to hybridise the existing Ourique photovoltaic plant, spread across the parishes of Luzianes-Gare, São Martinho das Amoreiras and Santa Clara-a-Velha, in Odemira, and Santana da Serra, in Ourique. The scoping proposal for the environmental impact study is in public consultation until 24 September 2026, and the project sits less than 9 km from three protected areas.

Galp submitted a Scope Definition Proposal (PDA) to the Portuguese Environment Agency in June 2026 for the "Mira Wind Project", which consists of wind-hybridising the existing Ourique photovoltaic plant. The project foresees around 22 wind turbines with an estimated total capacity of 158 MW, spread across four parishes: Luzianes-Gare (4), São Martinho das Amoreiras (10) and Santa Clara-a-Velha (4), in Odemira municipality, and Santana da Serra (4), in Ourique municipality, both in Beja district.

The scoping stage of the Environmental Impact Study was in public consultation, as part of the process promoted by Galp, with a deadline of 24 September 2026. According to the environmental outlet Green PT, the project sits less than 9 km from three protected areas, reinforcing the need for scrutiny of impacts on the region's sensitive fauna and habitats.

According to Galp, construction of the park is not expected to start before 2029, given the different licensing and development phases still under way; it is now, during the environmental scoping stage, that public participation has the greatest effect on which impacts actually get assessed.
