Galé Campsite has been closed since 2021, with no reopening in sight

Melides, Grândola

⚡ Closed since 2021 · no reopening announced

The Galé campsite in Melides closed in October 2021 after being bought for around €25 million by Discovery Land Company, which plans to turn the site into the Costa Terra luxury resort. The Reabrir a Galé movement took a petition to Parliament and organised protests; Grândola's council says it cannot intervene since it is private investment. No confirmed reopening has been reported to date.

The Galé campsite in Melides (Grândola municipality) closed in October 2021, after being bought for around €25 million by Discovery Land Company, the developer behind the Costa Terra Golf & Ocean Club luxury resort. Widely regarded as the country's best public campsite, it has never reopened.

The Reabrir a Galé civic movement, born from a group of regular campsite users, launched a petition for the campsite's continued operation, discussed at a parliamentary hearing in October 2022 with the participation of the Dunas Livres Association. The group also alleges environmental irregularities linked to the resort, such as dune destruction to build a golf course roughly 500 metres from the sea. Grândola's municipal council has stated it cannot intervene since it is private investment. The same resort (and the same stretch of coast between Tróia and Melides) is also linked to recurring complaints about restricted public beach access, documented in official inspections and denounced by Quercus.
