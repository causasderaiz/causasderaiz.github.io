No. 64 ◐ Monitoring Kaolin mining

Royal China Clay kaolin mine in Serra d'El-Rei

Serra d'El-Rei, Peniche, Leiria district

◐ Public consultation closed with 7,368 submissions · Óbidos and Peniche councils and Quercus give unfavourable opinion

The C-185 “Royal China Clay” mining concession, held by Motamineral – Minerais Industriais, would exploit 51.4 hectares of kaolinitic sands across three cores near Serra d'El-Rei (Peniche), with an on-site industrial washing plant. The public consultation closed on 1 September with 7,368 submissions; the councils of Óbidos and Peniche gave an unfavourable opinion on the project, and Quercus joined the call to reject it, warning of cumulative impacts on the region.

Mining concession C-185 "Royal China Clay", awarded to Motamineral – Minerais Industriais, S.A., aims to extract around 101 hectares of kaolinitic sand and clay across three sites near Serra d'El-Rei, in the municipality of Peniche, with an on-site washing plant. The extracted material is destined for the ceramic-paste factory of MCS – Mota Ceramic Solutions.

The public consultation on the environmental impact study ran from 22 July to 1 September 2026. According to Quercus, the concession would affect 471 of the 662 cork oaks in the area, with compensation planned for just 300 new specimens (which the group calls "derisory") as well as nine vulnerable animal species, a wet-heathland habitat and the native plant Drosophyllum lusitanicum.

The Óbidos and Peniche municipal councils, the Atouguia da Baleia, Peniche and Serra d'El-Rei parish councils, Quercus and the Portuguese Association of Landscape Architects all issued unfavourable opinions on the project. The Left Bloc questioned the government about the process on 3 September.
