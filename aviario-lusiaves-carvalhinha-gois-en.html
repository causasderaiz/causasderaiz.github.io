Lusiaves industrial poultry farm in Góis: a public consultation the population does not know about

Carvalhinha, Góis, Coimbra district, in the Serra da Lousã

🗳 Public consultation on the Quinta da Carvalhinha poultry unit until 2 November 2026

🗳 Take part in the public consultation

At Carvalhinha, in Góis, in the Serra da Lousã, the Lusiaves group wants to build a poultry unit with six groups of sheds. The public consultation runs until 2 November 2026, but many people in nearby villages do not know about the project: the first regional news report came out on 29 October, four days before the deadline. Residents point out that part of the site overlaps the National Ecological Reserve and that the project lies about 1 km from the Serra da Lousã Special Area of Conservation, part of Natura 2000.

What is at stake

The population has not been heard: residents say many people in nearby villages do not know the project is planned, that the first regional news report on the consultation only came out on 29 October, and that the Participa portal sent no notification to users who have them switched on. They are asking for the consultation to be publicised. They are worried about public health, water, soils, the landscape and ecosystems, since part of the site overlaps the National Ecological Reserve and the project lies about 1 km from the Serra da Lousã Special Area of Conservation, part of Natura 2000. And there are the animals: in intensive poultry farming, the birds spend their whole lives shut inside sheds, in their thousands, never going outdoors; in chicken meat production they are slaughtered at around six weeks old, when a hen can live for several years. Don't eat meat that comes from suffering.
