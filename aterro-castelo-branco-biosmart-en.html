Expansion of the BioSmart industrial waste landfill, Castelo Branco

Vedulho de Baixo, Castelo Branco municipality

⚡ ZERO says the project is "clearly illegal"; consultation closed, under review at CCDR Centro

BioSmart, Soluções Ambientais, S.A. wants to expand the Castelo Branco non-hazardous industrial waste landfill with a third cell, raising total capacity from 488,750 to 639,250 tonnes. The ZERO association issued a negative opinion, arguing the environmental impact study allowed for the possibility of receiving organic waste, whose landfill deposition has been banned by law since 2026, and is asking CCDR Centro not to license the new cell.

The Castelo Branco non-hazardous industrial waste landfill, in Vedulho de Baixo, opened in 2003 and is run by BioSmart, Soluções Ambientais, S.A.. The expansion project, in public consultation on the Participa portal between 13 July and 21 August 2026, involves building a third cell, raising total deposition capacity from 488,750 tonnes (425,000 m³) to 639,250 tonnes (565,000 m³).

In a statement on 8 September 2026, the ZERO association issued a negative opinion on the project, saying it did not understand how CCDR Centro could have put it out for public consultation given it was "clearly illegal". According to ZERO, the environmental impact study allowed for the possibility of the new cell receiving organic waste, whose landfill deposition has been banned since 1 January 2026 under the Legal Regime for Waste Landfill Deposition, which requires prior treatment and stabilisation of the organic fraction before deposition.

ZERO is asking CCDR Centro not to license the new cell until organic waste is removed from the list of waste the site is authorised to receive, citing as precedent the Resilei landfill in Leiria, where CCDR Centro refused to authorise organic waste deposition in a similar situation. The landfill has faced previous controversies, including environmental infractions recorded in 2018 over receiving asbestos-containing construction materials.
