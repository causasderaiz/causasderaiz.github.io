Cerro do Meio quarry: court orders closure in Tavira over noise and dust

Cerro do Meio, Luz de Tavira e Santo Estêvão parish, Tavira, Algarve

✓ Court orders permanent closure · Verdasca Group under police investigation

The Tavira court ordered the immediate and permanent closure of the Cerro do Meio quarry, for violating the personality rights of residents of the Luz de Tavira e Santo Estêvão parish union. The operation, run by Miner Blanc (part of the Verdasca Group), had been reactivated in 2025 after 12 years suspended, and complaints of excessive noise, vibration and dust multiplied since then; the company now risks disobedience proceedings and a €2,000 fine for each day of non-compliance. The Verdasca Group is also a target of the Judicial Police's "Terra Limpa" operation, investigating suspected environmental crimes and corruption linked to another of its companies, in Ourém.

The Tavira Court ordered the immediate and permanent closure of the "Cerro do Meio" quarry, operated by Miner Blanc, Lda. (Verdasca group), in the Luz de Tavira and Santo Estêvão parish union. A group of residents had filed a special personality-rights protection case asking for the activity (suspended since 2013 and resumed in 2025) to be shut down entirely.

Residents' complaints centred on excessive noise, vibration and dust. The court also found that vibrations and earthmoving in the area could damage an essential water-supply pipeline for the eastern Algarve that runs near the quarry.

The company was given 24 hours to halt operations, on pain of a disobedience offence.
