No. 61 ◐ Monitoring Real estate + water

Expansion of the Monte Rei golf course amid a water emergency

Vila Nova de Cacela, Vila Real de Santo António, Algarve

◐ Unfavourable opinion

A second 56-hectare golf course, called by ZERO "incompatible with the water emergency" facing the Algarve.

The Monte Rei resort, in Vila Nova de Cacela, wants to build a second 56-hectare golf course, in an Algarve region that has faced critical water scarcity for several consecutive years.

ZERO called the project "incompatible with the water emergency" the region faces, questioning the priority given to water use for recreational purposes over the water supply needs of local residents.

The licensing process received an unfavourable opinion, but the developer keeps the project under review, with the possibility of appealing the decision.
