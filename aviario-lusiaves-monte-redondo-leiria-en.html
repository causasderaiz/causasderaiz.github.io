No. 117 ◐ Monitoring Pollution

Lusiaves mega poultry farm in Monte Redondo: 4.5 million chickens a year 500 metres from homes

Quinta de D. Dinis, Monte Redondo and Carreira parish union, Leiria municipality

◐ Expansion approved by CCDR Centro (conditional favourable declaration) against the opinions of Leiria council and the parishes

Meigal, part of the Lusiaves Group, wants to expand the Quinta de D. Dinis poultry farm in Monte Redondo (Leiria) from about 40,000 to 767,000 birds per cycle, about 4.5 million chickens a year. CCDR Centro approved it with conditions in May 2025, against the opinions of the council and parishes and more than 500 consultation submissions. The Frente Unida association denounces irregularities and risks to health, water and safety near schools.

What is at stake

Quinta de D. Dinis, in the Monte Redondo and Carreira parish union (Leiria), has a poultry farm run by Meigal, part of the Lusiaves Group, with two small sheds and capacity for 39,622 chickens per cycle. The expansion adds four sheds and raises capacity to 767,000 birds per cycle, about 4.5 million chickens a year over six cycles, roughly 19 times current output, and creates 10 jobs. Next door is the Racentro plant, a company of the same group.

  3. 9 Jul

    Frente Unida denounces "serious irregularities in the approval process" and notes that the farm lies just over 500 metres from Aroeira, Porto Longo and Coimbrão, with lorries passing two schools. It says it gathered more than 2,400 signatures in under 15 days.

    Source: Diário de Leiria ↗
  4. Jun

    The head of the Monte Redondo and Carreira parish union, Celine Gaspar, keeps her opposition: "We are against this unit being installed in our parish."

    Source: Jornal de Leiria ↗
  5. 29 May

    CCDR Centro issues a conditionally favourable Environmental Impact Declaration for the expansion, with 23 conditions (water, soil, noise and monitoring). The public consultation drew 536 submissions, nearly all against, and Leiria council had given an unfavourable opinion.

    Source: Região de Leiria ↗
