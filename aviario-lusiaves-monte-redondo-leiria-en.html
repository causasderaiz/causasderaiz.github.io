No. 117 ◐ Monitoring Intensive farming and livestock

Mega poultry farm approved against residents' wishes: 4.5 million chickens a year for slaughter 500 metres from Monte Redondo

Quinta de D. Dinis, Monte Redondo and Carreira parish union, Leiria municipality

◐ Expansion approved by CCDR Centro (conditional favourable declaration) against the opinions of Leiria council and the parishes

Meigal, part of the Lusiaves Group, wants to expand the Quinta de D. Dinis poultry farm in Monte Redondo (Leiria) from about 40,000 to 767,000 birds per cycle, about 4.5 million chickens a year. CCDR Centro approved it with conditions in May 2025, against the opinions of the council and parishes and more than 500 consultation submissions. The Frente Unida association denounces irregularities and risks to health, water and safety near schools. In 2025, Frente Animal exposed chicken abuse at another Lusiaves farm, in Figueira da Foz, which lost its animal welfare seal for months.

What is at stake

Quinta de D. Dinis, in the Monte Redondo and Carreira parish union (Leiria), has a poultry farm run by Meigal, part of the Lusiaves Group, with two small sheds and capacity for 39,622 chickens per cycle. The expansion adds four sheds and raises capacity to 767,000 birds per cycle, about 4.5 million chickens a year over six cycles, roughly 19 times current output, and creates 10 jobs. Next door is the Racentro plant, a company of the same group. According to Frente Unida, the operator's own figures foresee 250 chickens dying per day at the expanded farm.

    Source: Observador/Lusa ↗
  3. 10 Jul

    Frente Unida, set up in June, announces it will seek meetings with regional authorities and hearings at Parliament's Environment and Energy Committee. It says the declaration left out the 2,415 signatures and the opposing opinions.

    Source: Região de Leiria ↗
  4. 9 Jul

    Frente Unida denounces "serious irregularities in the approval process" and notes that the farm lies just over 500 metres from Aroeira, Porto Longo and Coimbrão, with lorries passing two schools. It says it gathered more than 2,400 signatures in under 15 days.

    Source: Diário de Leiria ↗
  5. Jun

    The head of the Monte Redondo and Carreira parish union, Celine Gaspar, keeps her opposition: "We are against this unit being installed in our parish."

    Source: Jornal de Leiria ↗
  6. 29 May

    CCDR Centro issues a conditionally favourable Environmental Impact Declaration for the expansion, with 23 conditions (water, soil, noise and monitoring). The public consultation drew 536 submissions, nearly all against, and Leiria council had given an unfavourable opinion.

    Source: Região de Leiria ↗
  7. 5 Apr

    DGAV, the food and veterinary authority, says it acted "promptly and immediately" after the Lusiaves abuse complaint, with inspections, corrective measures and an administrative offence procedure.

    Source: Observador/Lusa ↗
  8. 17 Mar

    Welfair suspends until August the animal welfare seal of Lusiaves' Quinta de Matinhos farm (Figueira da Foz). The images show workers hitting birds, broken wings, exposed organs and dozens of dead animals; at the slaughterhouse, "higher than expected mortality" was found.

    Source: Renascença/Lusa ↗
  9. Mar

    Frente Animal releases hidden-camera footage from Lusiaves farms: "animals beaten, some to death", "extremely serious injuries" and birds dying "slowly, without water or food". Lusiaves calls it an "isolated episode" and moves to dismiss four workers for just cause.

    Source: Renascença ↗
