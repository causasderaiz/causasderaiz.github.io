Fish kill in the Corgo River, Vila Real, after a probable abnormal wastewater discharge

Corgo River, Vila Real municipality, Trás-os-Montes

⚡ Under APA investigation · Samples in laboratory analysis

APA is investigating the death of dozens of fish in the Corgo River, Vila Real, after a probable abnormal wastewater discharge that entered through the stormwater network between 15 and 16 August 2026.

The Portuguese Environment Agency (APA) is investigating the death of dozens of fish recorded in the Corgo River, in Vila Real municipality, in an action carried out together with the GNR's Nature and Environment Protection Service (SEPNA). Water samples and dead fish specimens were collected for laboratory analysis.

The authorities' preliminary assessment points to a probable abnormal wastewater discharge, whose source is still being determined, that entered the watercourse through the stormwater drainage network. The pollution event appears to have been a one-off, between 15 and 16 August 2026, days with thunderstorms and rain in the region; combined with high temperatures and the low summer flow typical of the season, this likely triggered the fish kill.

APA continues to monitor the situation on the ground and is awaiting laboratory reports to determine the exact extent of the ecological damage and the source of the discharge.
